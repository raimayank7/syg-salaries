NRI Tax Status

What income is taxed in India?

Section 5 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 sets what a person’s total income includes, depending on residential status (read on 13 November 2026).

The words of the section

  • Residents, 5(1): income that is received or deemed received in India, or accrues or arises or is deemed to accrue or arise in India, or accrues or arises outside India; but for a person not ordinarily resident, income accruing outside India is included only when derived from a business controlled in or a profession set up in India.
  • Non-residents, 5(2): income received or deemed received in India, or accruing or arising, or deemed to, in India.
  • 5(3): foreign income is not treated as received in India merely because it is taken into account in a balance sheet prepared in India.
  • 5(4): income included on one basis (accrued) is not included again on another (received).

Which rates and deductions apply to each status, and any relief under a tax treaty, are outside this site.

Common questions

Is income I earn abroad taxed in India if I am an NRI?

Not under section 5(2): a non-resident is taxed on income received in India or accruing or arising in India. It is taxed for a resident who is ordinarily resident, and for an RNOR only when it comes from a business controlled in India or a profession set up in India.
