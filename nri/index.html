Am I an NRI for income tax?

Work out whether you are resident, resident but not ordinarily resident, or non-resident in India under the Income-tax Act, 2025. Rules from sections 5 and 6 of the Act, read on 13 November 2026. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Common questions

How many days make me a resident of India for tax?

Under section 6(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 an individual is resident if in India for 182 days or more in the tax year, or for 60 days or more in the year and 365 days or more in the four years before it. Some people get different limits: see the rules page.

Is the tax year the same as the financial year?

The Act counts by "tax year", the period for which income is charged, which replaces the older previous year and assessment year terms.

Can a citizen be resident without staying in India?

Yes. Section 6(7) deems a citizen resident when the citizen is not liable to tax in any other country because of domicile or residence and has total income, other than from foreign sources, above ₹15 lakh. Such a person is a resident but not ordinarily resident.
