  • A median is the middle: in every occupation here, half the workers earn more than the figure shown and half earn less, so the columns for the lowest and top tenth matter as much as the median.
  • Sorted by number of jobs, largest first.

What is in this band

Inside the band, the medians run from $20.00 an hour (Personal Care and Service Workers, All Other) to $24.93 (Community Health Workers). For full-time work of 2,080 hours, the edges of the band, $20.00 and $25.00 an hour, come to $41,600 and $52,000 a year before tax. The three largest occupations hold 19% of the 35,026,120 jobs in the band.

The kinds of work

Counted by the Bureau's broad groups of occupations, the band holds most occupations from production (56), office and administrative support (30), construction and extraction (20) and installation, maintenance, and repair (14). In all, 17 of the 22 broad groups have at least one occupation here, with a single one from life, physical, and social science.

How wide the pay is inside an occupation

A median hides how far pay stretches inside one occupation. The widest range here is Broadcast Announcers and Radio Disc Jockeys: the lowest-paid tenth earn under $13.55 an hour and the highest-paid tenth over $66.56, a gap of $53.01. The narrowest is Tellers, from $16.52 to $23.88. The national median of $24.51 an hour falls inside this band.

Other bands: under $15 an hour, $15 to $20 an hour, $25 to $30 an hour, $30 to $40 an hour, $40 to $50 an hour, $50 to $75 an hour, over $75 an hour.
