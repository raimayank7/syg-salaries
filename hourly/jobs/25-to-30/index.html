What is in this band

Inside the band, the medians run from $25.00 an hour (Recreational Vehicle Service Technicians) to $29.98 (Tax Examiners and Collectors, and Revenue Agents). For full-time work of 2,080 hours, the edges of the band, $25.00 and $30.00 an hour, come to $52,000 and $62,400 a year before tax. The three largest occupations hold 26% of the 13,287,180 jobs in the band.

The kinds of work

Counted by the Bureau's broad groups of occupations, the band holds most occupations from construction and extraction (24), installation, maintenance, and repair (14), office and administrative support (12) and production (11). In all, 21 of the 22 broad groups have at least one occupation here, with a single one from management, computer and mathematical and legal.

How wide the pay is inside an occupation

A median hides how far pay stretches inside one occupation. The widest range here is Actors: the lowest-paid tenth earn under $16.50 an hour and the highest-paid tenth over $143.68, a gap of $127.18. The narrowest is Postal Service Mail Sorters, Processors, and Processing Machine Operators, from $21.89 to $36.19. In 0 of the 122 occupations, even the lowest-paid tenth earn more than the national median of $24.51.
