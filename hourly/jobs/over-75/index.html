In short

  • The occupations with the most jobs in this band are Financial Managers (841,710 jobs, $80.08 an hour), Lawyers (754,500 jobs, $76.76 an hour), Computer and Information Systems Managers (670,570 jobs, $84.20 an hour).
  • The median for all occupations in the country was $24.51 an hour.
  • A median is the middle: in every occupation here, half the workers earn more than the figure shown and half earn less, so the columns for the lowest and top tenth matter as much as the median.
  • Sorted by number of jobs, largest first.

What is in this band

Inside the band, the medians run from $76.76 an hour (Lawyers) to $268.76 (Pediatric Surgeons). For full-time work of 2,080 hours, $75.00 an hour comes to $156,000 a year before tax. The band has no upper edge. The three largest occupations hold 53% of the 4,300,490 jobs in the band.

The kinds of work

Counted by the Bureau's broad groups of occupations, the band holds most occupations from healthcare practitioners and technical (24), management (6), life, physical, and social science (2) and architecture and engineering (1).

How wide the pay is inside an occupation

A median hides how far pay stretches inside one occupation. The widest range here is Cardiologists: the lowest-paid tenth earn under $51.53 an hour and the highest-paid tenth over $342.37, a gap of $290.84. The narrowest is Computer Hardware Engineers, from $44.68 to $108.33. In all 34 occupations, even the lowest-paid tenth earn more than the national median of $24.51.
