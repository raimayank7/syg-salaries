  • A median is the middle: in every occupation here, half the workers earn more than the figure shown and half earn less, so the columns for the lowest and top tenth matter as much as the median.
  • Sorted by number of jobs, largest first.

What is in this band

Inside the band, the medians run from $15.00 an hour (Fast Food and Counter Workers) to $19.89 (Door-to-Door Sales Workers, News and Street Vendors, and Related Workers). For full-time work of 2,080 hours, the edges of the band, $15.00 and $20.00 an hour, come to $31,200 and $41,600 a year before tax. The three largest occupations hold 27% of the 44,253,950 jobs in the band.

The kinds of work

Counted by the Bureau's broad groups of occupations, the band holds most occupations from production (22), personal care and service (20), food preparation and serving related (13) and transportation and material moving (13). In all, 15 of the 22 broad groups have at least one occupation here, with a single one from educational instruction and library, healthcare practitioners and technical and construction and extraction.

How wide the pay is inside an occupation

A median hides how far pay stretches inside one occupation. The widest range here is Entertainers and Performers, Sports and Related Workers, All Other: the lowest-paid tenth earn under $14.48 an hour and the highest-paid tenth over $56.12, a gap of $41.64. The narrowest is Farmworkers and Laborers, Crop, Nursery, and Greenhouse, from $15.82 to $21.96. In 54 of the 111 occupations, the highest-paid tenth earn more than the national median of $24.51, although the median of the occupation is below it.

Other bands: under $15 an hour, $20 to $25 an hour, $25 to $30 an hour, $30 to $40 an hour, $40 to $50 an hour, $50 to $75 an hour, over $75 an hour.
