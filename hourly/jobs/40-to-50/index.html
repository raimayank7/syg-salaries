In short

  • The occupations with the most jobs in this band are Registered Nurses (3,379,720 jobs, $46.90 an hour), Accountants and Auditors (1,449,500 jobs, $40.23 an hour), Project Management Specialists (1,066,670 jobs, $49.19 an hour).
  • The median for all occupations in the country was $24.51 an hour.
  • A median is the middle: in every occupation here, half the workers earn more than the figure shown and half earn less, so the columns for the lowest and top tenth matter as much as the median.
  • Sorted by number of jobs, largest first.

What is in this band

Inside the band, the medians run from $40.15 an hour (Credit Analysts) to $49.72 (Medical Scientists, Except Epidemiologists). For full-time work of 2,080 hours, the edges of the band, $40.00 and $50.00 an hour, come to $83,200 and $104,000 a year before tax. The three largest occupations hold 50% of the 11,722,910 jobs in the band.

The kinds of work

Counted by the Bureau's broad groups of occupations, the band holds most occupations from life, physical, and social science (16), healthcare practitioners and technical (10), business and financial operations (8) and management (5). In all, 13 of the 22 broad groups have at least one occupation here, with a single one from personal care and service and sales and related.

How wide the pay is inside an occupation

A median hides how far pay stretches inside one occupation. The widest range here is Musicians and Singers: the lowest-paid tenth earn under $20.35 an hour and the highest-paid tenth over $132.56, a gap of $112.21. The narrowest is Postmasters and Mail Superintendents, from $40.91 to $53.72. In 58 of the 67 occupations, even the lowest-paid tenth earn more than the national median of $24.51.

Other bands: under $15 an hour, $15 to $20 an hour, $20 to $25 an hour, $25 to $30 an hour, $30 to $40 an hour, $50 to $75 an hour, over $75 an hour.
