In short

  • The occupations with the most jobs in this band are General and Operations Managers (3,503,020 jobs, $50.85 an hour), Software Developers (1,687,890 jobs, $65.38 an hour), Sales Managers (637,080 jobs, $71.28 an hour).
  • The median for all occupations in the country was $24.51 an hour.
  • A median is the middle: in every occupation here, half the workers earn more than the figure shown and half earn less, so the columns for the lowest and top tenth matter as much as the median.
  • Sorted by number of jobs, largest first.

What is in this band

Inside the band, the medians run from $50.00 an hour (Web and Digital Interface Designers) to $74.03 (Judges, Magistrate Judges, and Magistrates). For full-time work of 2,080 hours, the edges of the band, $50.00 and $75.00 an hour, come to $104,000 and $156,000 a year before tax. The three largest occupations hold 41% of the 14,207,810 jobs in the band.

The kinds of work

Counted by the Bureau's broad groups of occupations, the band holds most occupations from management (17), computer and mathematical (14), architecture and engineering (14) and life, physical, and social science (9). In all, 13 of the 22 broad groups have at least one occupation here, with a single one from arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media, protective service and construction and extraction.

How wide the pay is inside an occupation

A median hides how far pay stretches inside one occupation. The widest range here is Personal Financial Advisors: the lowest-paid tenth earn under $24.13 an hour and the highest-paid tenth over $171.65, a gap of $147.52. The narrowest is Nuclear Power Reactor Operators, from $47.42 to $71.78. In 73 of the 76 occupations, even the lowest-paid tenth earn more than the national median of $24.51.

Other bands: under $15 an hour, $15 to $20 an hour, $20 to $25 an hour, $25 to $30 an hour, $30 to $40 an hour, $40 to $50 an hour, over $75 an hour.
