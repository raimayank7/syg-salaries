In short

  • The occupations with the most jobs in this band are First-Line Supervisors of Office and Administrative Support Workers (1,436,680 jobs, $33.41 an hour), Sales Representatives of Services, Except Advertising, Insurance, Financial Services, and Travel (1,256,010 jobs, $33.65 an hour), Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Except Technical and Scientific Products (1,238,190 jobs, $34.65 an hour).
  • The median for all occupations in the country was $24.51 an hour.
  • A median is the middle: in every occupation here, half the workers earn more than the figure shown and half earn less, so the columns for the lowest and top tenth matter as much as the median.
  • Sorted by number of jobs, largest first.

What is in this band

Inside the band, the medians run from $30.03 an hour (Chefs and Head Cooks) to $39.93 (Business Operations Specialists, All Other). For full-time work of 2,080 hours, the edges of the band, $30.00 and $40.00 an hour, come to $62,400 and $83,200 a year before tax. The three largest occupations hold 16% of the 24,271,870 jobs in the band.

The kinds of work

Counted by the Bureau's broad groups of occupations, the band holds most occupations from business and financial operations (17), arts, design, entertainment, sports, and media (17), installation, maintenance, and repair (17) and architecture and engineering (15). In all, 20 of the 22 broad groups have at least one occupation here, with a single one from food preparation and serving related and personal care and service.

How wide the pay is inside an occupation

A median hides how far pay stretches inside one occupation. The widest range here is Agents and Business Managers of Artists, Performers, and Athletes: the lowest-paid tenth earn under $23.08 an hour and the highest-paid tenth over $136.81, a gap of $113.73. The narrowest is Roof Bolters, Mining, from $30.65 to $44.63. In 17 of the 166 occupations, even the lowest-paid tenth earn more than the national median of $24.51.

Other bands: under $15 an hour, $15 to $20 an hour, $20 to $25 an hour, $25 to $30 an hour, $40 to $50 an hour, $50 to $75 an hour, over $75 an hour.
