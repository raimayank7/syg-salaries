  • The median for all occupations in the country was $24.51 an hour.
  • A median is the middle: in every occupation here, half the workers earn more than the figure shown and half earn less, so the columns for the lowest and top tenth matter as much as the median.

Why this list is so short

Only one occupation in the national figures has a median under $15 an hour: Cooks, Fast Food. For Cooks, Fast Food, the lowest-paid tenth earn under $10.86 an hour and the highest-paid tenth over $20.63; a full-time year of 2,080 hours at the median of $14.85 comes to $30,888 before tax.

That does not mean few people earn under $15 an hour. A band holds occupations by their middle wage, and many large occupations have a median only just above this line while a good part of their workers earn below it. The lowest-paid tenth of every occupation named below earn less than the median shown, in most cases less than $15 an hour.
