How it is worked out

  • A week of pay is the hourly rate times your regular hours, plus overtime hours at the overtime rate.
  • A year is the week times your paid weeks. A month is one twelfth of the year.
  • If you enter a weekly, monthly or yearly amount, the calculator first finds the hourly rate from your regular hours, then works out the rest.
  • Your hours, weeks and currency sign are remembered in this browser for next time.

Common questions

How do I turn an hourly rate into a yearly salary?

Multiply the rate by the hours you work in a week and by the weeks you are paid in a year. For full-time work that is the rate times 2,080.

$20 an hour is how much a year?

$20 an hour for 40 hours a week and 52 weeks is $41,600 a year before tax, or about $3,467 a month.

Does the calculator take off tax?

No. Tax depends on the country, the state and your own situation, so the results are before tax and deductions.

Can I use it for rupees, pounds or euros?

Yes. Choose the currency sign. The arithmetic is the same; the calculator does not change one currency into another.
