The quick rule

For full-time work, double the hourly rate and add three zeros to get close to the yearly figure: $20 an hour is about $40,000 a year (exactly $41,600). To go the other way, halve the salary and drop three zeros.

What the table assumes

  • 40 paid hours every week and 52 paid weeks. Unpaid holidays or shorter weeks lower the yearly figure; use the calculator for your own hours.
  • No overtime. Overtime hours are usually paid at a higher rate.
  • A month is one twelfth of the year, so it is a little more than four weeks.
  • The first row, $7.25, is the federal minimum wage of the United States.
  • The dollar sign is only a label. The same arithmetic holds in any currency.

If your hours are not 40 a week

The yearly figure is always rate times hours a week times paid weeks, so it scales in a straight line. At 30 hours a week the year has 1,560 paid hours, three quarters of the table: $20 an hour gives $31,200. At 20 hours it has 1,040, exactly half: $20,800. With two unpaid weeks, a 40-hour job has 2,000 paid hours, which makes the sum easy: $20 an hour is $40,000. For any other pattern, type your own hours and weeks into the calculator.

Fortnightly pay and twice-monthly pay are not the same

The fortnight column is two weeks of pay, 80 hours. A worker paid every two weeks gets 26 pay packets a year. A worker paid twice a month gets 24, each a little larger: one twenty-fourth of the year instead of one twenty-sixth. At $20 an hour that is $1,600 a fortnight or $1,733 twice a month. The year comes to the same $41,600 either way.

Common questions

$15 an hour is how much a year?

$15 an hour for 40 hours a week and 52 weeks is $31,200 a year before tax, about $2,600 a month and $600 a week.

$25 an hour is how much a year?

$25 an hour for 40 hours a week and 52 weeks is $52,000 a year before tax, about $4,333 a month and $1,000 a week.

$30 an hour is how much a year?

$30 an hour for 40 hours a week and 52 weeks is $62,400 a year before tax, about $5,200 a month and $1,200 a week.

How many working hours are in a year?

The standard full-time figure is 2,080: 40 hours for 52 weeks. It counts paid holidays and leave as paid hours.
