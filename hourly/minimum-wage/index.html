How the two levels fit together

The federal Fair Labor Standards Act sets a floor of $7.25 an hour for the workers it covers. A state can set its own rate. Where both apply, the worker gets the higher one. Some cities and counties go higher still; those local rates are not in this table.

Common questions

What is the federal minimum wage?

The federal minimum wage is $7.25 an hour. It has been at that level since 24 July 2009.

Which state has the highest minimum wage?

In the Department of Labor's table updated on 5 November 2026, the highest rates are District of Columbia ($18.40), Washington ($17.13), New York ($17.00), Connecticut ($16.94), California ($16.90).

If the state and federal rates differ, which applies?

A worker covered by both laws is entitled to the higher of the two rates.
