Reading this list

  • It ranks the median: the pay of the person in the middle. Half the people in the occupation earn less.
  • It covers employees only. Owners of practices and businesses, and the self-employed, are not counted.
  • Occupations for which BLS publishes only hourly pay are not ranked here.
  • Many of these occupations need long training and a licence. High pay says nothing about how many openings there are: check the "Jobs" column.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025. Checked in November 2026. Figures are for employees, before tax, and leave out overtime pay and benefits. They are official statistics, not advice or a pay offer; see bls.gov/oes for the original tables.
