Senior Citizen Tax India

Health insurance and medical deduction

Section 126 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (read on 13 November 2026) allows up to ₹25,000 for a health insurance premium (including CGHS contributions and up to ₹5,000 of preventive check-up) for the assessee and family, and the same for parents; if the insured person is a senior citizen the ₹25,000 becomes ₹50,000 (126(8)). Each of the two groups is capped at ₹50,000 together with medical expenditure (126(4)). "Family" means the spouse and dependent children.

Specified diseases, section 128

The smaller of the amount paid and ₹40,000, or ₹1,00,000 where the patient is a senior citizen, after taking off insurance or employer reimbursement. A prescription from a specialist is required (128(2)).

Common questions

Can a senior citizen claim medical expenses without insurance?

Section 126(2)(c) and (d) allow medical expenditure up to ₹50,000, and 126(7) says that for a senior citizen it counts only if no health insurance premium has been paid for that person. This site applies it to senior citizens only.

What is the limit for specified diseases?

Section 128: the amount paid or ₹40,000, whichever is less, or ₹1,00,000 for a senior citizen, reduced by any insurance or employer reimbursement.
