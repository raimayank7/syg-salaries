How DA is worked out

DA is a percentage of basic pay. With a basic pay of ₹56,100 and a DA of 63%, the allowance is ₹35,343 a month; at the old rate of 60% it was ₹33,660, so the increase is ₹1,683 a month or ₹20,196 a year. The 3-point rise announced on 28 October 2026 applies from 1 July 2026, which is before the date of the decision, so the difference for July to October 2026 (four months) is the amount this calculator shows as arrears: about ₹6,732 on this basic pay. How and when the arrears are paid is decided by your pay office; the Cabinet release does not say.

Common questions

What is the DA rate for central government employees now?

The Cabinet approved 63% of Basic Pay from 1 July 2026, a rise of 3 points over 60% (PIB, 28 October 2026).

What is the difference between DA and DR?

Dearness Allowance goes to serving employees and Dearness Relief to pensioners. The Cabinet decisions give the same percentage for both.

Is DA a percentage of basic pay?

Yes. The Cabinet releases state the increase over "the existing rate of x%% of the Basic Pay/Pension".

Do state government employees get the same DA?

No. This site covers the central government only; each state decides its own rate.
