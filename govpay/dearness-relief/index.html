Dearness Relief calculator for pensioners

Type the basic pension to see the Dearness Relief at the latest rate (63% from 1 July 2026) and the arrears of the increase. Rates from pib.gov.in, read on 13 November 2026.

The rates filled in are the latest two in the Cabinet decisions: 63% from 1 July 2026, replacing 60%. Change them to try any other rate or period. Arrears = months × the monthly increase.

Example

On a basic pension of ₹30,000, DR at 63% is ₹18,900 a month. At the earlier 60% it was ₹18,000, so the pension plus relief rose from ₹48,000 to ₹48,900 a month, an increase of ₹900. For the four months July to October 2026 that is ₹3,600.

Common questions

What is Dearness Relief?

It is the cost-of-living increase paid to pensioners, shown in the Cabinet releases as a percentage of Pension.

What is the DR rate now?

63% from 1 July 2026, up from 60% (PIB, 28 October 2026).

How is it worked out?

Pension × rate ÷ 100. The calculator rounds to whole rupees; your pension disbursing office applies the exact rules.
