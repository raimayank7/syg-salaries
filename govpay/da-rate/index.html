Reading the table

  • Rates are percentages of basic pay (or pension for DR) and apply from 1 January or 1 July.
  • The Cabinet decides some months later; the gap between the date and the decision is paid as arrears.
  • Together the six rises since January 2024 are 17 points, which on a basic pay of ₹56,100 adds ₹9,537 a month.
  • The figures for ₹18,000, ₹56,100 and ₹1,00,000 are examples; use the calculator for your own pay.

Common questions

How many times has DA risen since January 2024?

Six times in the Cabinet decisions read for this site: 4 points from January 2024, then 3, 2, 3, 2 and 3 points, taking it from 46% to 63%.

Why do the decisions come months after the date?

Each release says the rate applies "w.e.f." an earlier date (1 January or 1 July) and was posted later, for example the 1 July 2026 rate on 28 October 2026. The difference is paid as arrears.

Is there a rate before 46%?

Yes, but the earlier releases were not read for this site, so the table starts at January 2024.
