DA on a basic pay of ₹2,50,000

For a central government employee with a basic pay of ₹2,50,000 a month, the Dearness Allowance at the rate from 1 July 2026 (63%) is ₹1,57,500 a month, so basic pay plus DA is ₹4,07,500. The rise over the earlier 60% is ₹7,500 a month, or ₹90,000 a year.

What this tells you

  • Since the 46% rate before January 2024 the DA on this pay has gone up by ₹42,500 a month.
  • The 3-point rise decided on 28 October 2026 applies from 1 July 2026, so July to October 2026 (four months) comes to ₹30,000 in arrears if the basic pay did not change.
  • These figures cover DA only. The release does not give the date of payment of the arrears; your pay office decides.

Common questions

What is the DA on a basic pay of ₹2,50,000?

₹1,57,500 a month at 63% (from 1 July 2026), up from ₹1,50,000 at 60%.

How much arrears for July to October 2026?

About ₹30,000 (four months × ₹7,500), if the basic pay stayed the same.
