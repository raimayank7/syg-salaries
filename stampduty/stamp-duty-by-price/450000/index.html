Stamp Duty on a £450,000 property

Buying a residential property for £450,000 in England or Northern Ireland costs £12,500 in Stamp Duty Land Tax for a standard buyer. A first-time buyer pays £7,500, and someone buying an additional property pays £35,000. In Wales the Land Transaction Tax at the main rates is £14,250.

The working, standard buyer

  1. 0% on £125,000 (£0 to £125,000): £0
  2. 2% on £125,000 (£125,001 to £250,000): £2,500
  3. 5% on £200,000 (£250,001 to £450,000): £10,000

Total: £12,500, which is 2.78% of the price.

The other cases

First-time buyer£7,500
Additional property (higher rates)£35,000
Wales, main rates£14,250
Wales, higher rates£36,200

Things to know

  • A first-time buyer saves £5,000 at this price.
  • The higher rates add 5 percentage points to each standard band in England and Northern Ireland, so the extra on this price is £22,500.
  • The tax is paid within 14 days of completion, usually by your conveyancer on completion day.
