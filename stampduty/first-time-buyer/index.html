UK Stamp Duty

First-time buyer Stamp Duty

A first-time buyer in England or Northern Ireland pays no Stamp Duty Land Tax on the first £300,000 and 5% on the portion from £300,001 to £500,000. Above £500,000 the relief is lost and standard rates apply to the whole price (GOV.UK, read on 13 November 2026).

The cliff at £500,000

At £500,000 a first-time buyer pays £10,000; at £500,001 the relief goes and the tax is £15,000, because the standard rates apply to the whole price. That is £5,000 more for a pound more.

Planning around it

Because of that cliff, a buyer near £500,000 who is a first-time buyer may find that a price just under the limit saves several thousand pounds in tax. The tax is only one cost: check your own figures with a conveyancer, who will also confirm whether you really are a first-time buyer, which GOV.UK says depends on everyone you buy with.

Common questions

Who is a first-time buyer?

GOV.UK says you are eligible if you and anyone else you are buying with are first-time buyers.

What if the price is over £500,000?

You cannot claim the relief and the rules for people who have bought a home before apply.
