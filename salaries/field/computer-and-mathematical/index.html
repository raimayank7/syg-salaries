The field as a whole

Computer and Mathematical holds 5,260,110 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 3 in every 100. Its median pay of $109,280 a year is 2.1 times the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 2 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $58,200 and the highest-paid tenth more than $191,450; the middle half earned between $78,820 and $155,830. The average was $120,080, 10% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 2.3 times the lowest ($140,300 against $61,860). All 21 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Statisticians, at $105,650. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 56% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Mathematicians, has 2,030. Pay stretches furthest within Web and Digital Interface Designers, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $201,550 and the lowest-paid tenth under $53,750.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Software Developers, the state medians run from $79,380 in Puerto Rico to $174,410 in California; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
