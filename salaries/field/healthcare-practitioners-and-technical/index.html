The field as a whole

Healthcare Practitioners and Technical holds 9,823,860 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 6 in every 100. Its median pay of $86,530 a year is 70% above the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 5 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $46,270 and the highest-paid tenth more than $171,790; the middle half earned between $63,860 and $123,540. The average was $108,700, 26% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 14.9 times the lowest ($559,030 against $37,640). 63 of the 71 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980, and 8 are below it. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Occupational Therapists, at $100,330. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 46% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Prosthodontists, has 870. Pay stretches furthest within Surgeons, All Other, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $655,320 and the lowest-paid tenth under $80,320.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Registered Nurses, the state medians run from $39,880 in Puerto Rico to $140,270 in California; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
