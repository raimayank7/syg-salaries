The field as a whole

Educational Instruction and Library holds 9,100,570 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 6 in every 100. Its median pay of $60,570 a year is 19% above the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 9 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $32,990 and the highest-paid tenth more than $105,240; the middle half earned between $43,900 and $79,470. The average was $67,540, 12% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 3.5 times the lowest ($128,500 against $36,780). 56 of the 64 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980, and 8 are below it. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Recreation and Fitness Studies Teachers, Postsecondary, at $77,270. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 43% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Forestry and Conservation Science Teachers, Postsecondary, has 1,520. Pay stretches furthest within Health Specialties Teachers, Postsecondary, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $322,020 and the lowest-paid tenth under $59,270.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Teaching Assistants, Except Postsecondary, the state medians run from $21,250 in Guam to $49,120 in Washington; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
