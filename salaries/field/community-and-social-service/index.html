The field as a whole

Community and Social Service holds 2,668,680 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 2 in every 100. Its median pay of $58,300 a year is 14% above the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 12 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $37,970 and the highest-paid tenth more than $97,630; the middle half earned between $46,370 and $75,910. The average was $63,410, 9% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 1.6 times the lowest ($71,900 against $45,280). 13 of the 17 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980, and 4 are below it. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Child, Family, and School Social Workers, at $59,550. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 50% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Religious Workers, All Other, has 15,760. Pay stretches furthest within Religious Workers, All Other, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $93,900 and the lowest-paid tenth under $31,200.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Substance Abuse, Behavioral Disorder, and Mental Health Counselors, the state medians run from $37,910 in Louisiana to $80,770 in Alaska; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
