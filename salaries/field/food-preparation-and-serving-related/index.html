The field as a whole

Food Preparation and Serving Related holds 13,680,750 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 9 in every 100. Its median pay of $35,050 a year is 31% below the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 22 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $23,120 and the highest-paid tenth more than $51,340; the middle half earned between $28,760 and $41,860. The average was $37,150, 6% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 2.0 times the lowest ($62,470 against $30,890). 1 of the 17 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980, and 16 are below it. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Food Servers, Nonrestaurant, at $35,360. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 55% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Cooks, Private Household, has 1,100. Pay stretches furthest within Bartenders, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $73,770 and the lowest-paid tenth under $20,110.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Fast Food and Counter Workers, the state medians run from $20,770 in Guam to $42,280 in California; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
