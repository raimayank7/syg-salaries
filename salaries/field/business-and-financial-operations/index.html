The field as a whole

Business and Financial Operations holds 10,537,240 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 7 in every 100. Its median pay of $82,660 a year is 62% above the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 6 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $48,820 and the highest-paid tenth more than $153,990; the middle half earned between $62,940 and $114,820. The average was $95,230, 15% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 2.2 times the lowest ($117,330 against $52,230). All 32 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Market Research Analysts and Marketing Specialists, at $78,760. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 34% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Farm Labor Contractors, has 310. Pay stretches furthest within Personal Financial Advisors, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $357,020 and the lowest-paid tenth under $50,190.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Accountants and Auditors, the state medians run from $43,530 in Puerto Rico to $111,530 in District of Columbia; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
