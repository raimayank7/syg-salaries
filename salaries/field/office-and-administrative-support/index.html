The field as a whole

Office and Administrative Support holds 17,753,430 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 11 in every 100. Its median pay of $47,450 a year is 7% below the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 14 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $33,530 and the highest-paid tenth more than $76,170; the middle half earned between $38,540 and $60,060. The average was $51,560, 9% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 2.2 times the lowest ($76,590 against $35,070). 16 of the 54 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980, and 38 are below it. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Billing and Posting Clerks, at $48,500. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 38% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Communications Equipment Operators, All Other, has 1,200. Pay stretches furthest within Desktop Publishers, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $96,170 and the lowest-paid tenth under $35,680.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Customer Service Representatives, the state medians run from $26,700 in Puerto Rico to $50,330 in Washington; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
