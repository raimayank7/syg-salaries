The field as a whole

Healthcare Support holds 7,901,670 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 5 in every 100. Its median pay of $38,340 a year is 25% below the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 18 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $28,980 and the highest-paid tenth more than $54,230; the middle half earned between $34,320 and $45,930. The average was $40,800, 6% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 2.1 times the lowest ($72,300 against $35,240). 3 of the 17 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980, and 14 are below it. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Psychiatric Aides, at $44,910. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 83% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Occupational Therapy Aides, has 4,310. Pay stretches furthest within Massage Therapists, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $100,200 and the lowest-paid tenth under $33,640.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Home Health and Personal Care Aides, the state medians run from $21,840 in Puerto Rico to $47,730 in Washington; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
