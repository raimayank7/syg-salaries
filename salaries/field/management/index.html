The field as a whole

Management holds 11,132,700 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 7 in every 100. Its median pay of $126,520 a year is 2.5 times the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 1 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $60,130 and the highest-paid tenth more than $257,310; the middle half earned between $82,970 and $176,280. The average was $145,260, 15% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 3.6 times the lowest ($213,990 against $59,300). All 37 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Administrative Services Managers, at $114,130. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 45% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Gambling Managers, has 5,030. Pay stretches furthest within Chief Executives, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $507,730 and the lowest-paid tenth under $75,700.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, General and Operations Managers, the state medians run from $67,690 in Guam to $173,690 in New Jersey; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
