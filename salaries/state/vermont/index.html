How pay is spread in Vermont

By median pay, Vermont is number 14 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $35,910 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $111,480. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $43,350 to $79,240. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $69,050, 22% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 303,890 jobs counted here are 0.2% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Vermont are Office and Administrative Support (30,640 jobs, 11%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (25,280 jobs, 9%), Educational Instruction and Library (24,560 jobs, 8%) and Sales and Related (24,510 jobs, 8%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Community and Social Service Specialists, All Other (13.5 times the national share), Highway Maintenance Workers (3.9 times the national share) and Computer Numerically Controlled Tool Operators (3.6 times the national share). The table on this page lists 541 occupations; for 12 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 74 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 40 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 16 pay less and 18 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Waiters and Waitresses ($59,140 here, $35,230 nationally), Bartenders ($45,450 here, $34,340 nationally) and Cooks, Institution and Cafeteria ($46,370 here, $37,450 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Financial Managers ($128,690 here, $166,570 nationally), Project Management Specialists ($80,800 here, $102,320 nationally) and Computer and Information Systems Managers ($138,460 here, $175,140 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Vermont

What is the median salary in Vermont?

The median pay of all employees in Vermont was $56,390 a year ($27.11 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Vermont?

Retail Salespersons, with 8,250 jobs and a median pay of $37,400 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Vermont?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Surgeons, All Other ($438,530), Anesthesiologists ($434,780) and Neurologists ($434,730). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
