How pay is spread in Louisiana

By median pay, Louisiana is number 48 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $22,710 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $100,110. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $30,350 to $66,340. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $56,600, 24% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 1,934,900 jobs counted here are 1.2% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Louisiana are Office and Administrative Support (222,940 jobs, 12%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (186,900 jobs, 10%), Transportation and Material Moving (184,330 jobs, 10%) and Sales and Related (168,650 jobs, 9%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Extraction Workers, All Other (23.9 times the national share), Sailors and Marine Oilers (19.2 times the national share) and Chemical Plant and System Operators (16.4 times the national share). The table on this page lists 693 occupations; for 14 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 280 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 30 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 235 pay less and 15 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Chemical Equipment Operators and Tenders ($78,580 here, $58,040 nationally), Physicians, All Other ($350,920 here, $265,930 nationally) and Coaches and Scouts ($60,960 here, $47,320 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Waiters and Waitresses ($15,080 here, $35,230 nationally), Gambling Dealers ($17,760 here, $34,320 nationally) and Firefighters ($33,120 here, $59,280 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Louisiana

What is the median salary in Louisiana?

The median pay of all employees in Louisiana was $45,520 a year ($21.89 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Louisiana?

Retail Salespersons, with 49,570 jobs and a median pay of $29,490 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Louisiana?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Pediatricians, General ($502,720), Anesthesiologists ($410,500) and Obstetricians and Gynecologists ($404,550). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
