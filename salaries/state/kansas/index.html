How pay is spread in Kansas

By median pay, Kansas is number 34 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $28,530 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $103,250. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $35,880 to $72,280. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $60,640, 26% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 1,437,520 jobs counted here are 0.9% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Kansas are Office and Administrative Support (162,120 jobs, 11%), Transportation and Material Moving (134,580 jobs, 9%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (123,800 jobs, 9%) and Sales and Related (123,570 jobs, 9%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Aircraft Structure, Surfaces, Rigging, and Systems Assemblers (30.9 times the national share), Layout Workers, Metal and Plastic (11.1 times the national share) and Farm Equipment Mechanics and Service Technicians (6.1 times the national share). The table on this page lists 677 occupations; for 8 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 247 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 24 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 196 pay less and 27 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Meat, Poultry, and Fish Cutters and Trimmers ($48,080 here, $38,300 nationally), Preschool Teachers, Except Special Education ($47,750 here, $38,140 nationally) and Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products ($130,850 here, $104,920 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Financial Specialists, All Other ($51,160 here, $81,100 nationally), Legal Secretaries and Administrative Assistants ($36,620 here, $55,570 nationally) and Lawyers ($106,650 here, $159,670 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Kansas

What is the median salary in Kansas?

The median pay of all employees in Kansas was $48,010 a year ($23.08 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Kansas?

Fast Food and Counter Workers, with 47,140 jobs and a median pay of $27,580 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Kansas?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Anesthesiologists ($437,030), Orthopedic Surgeons, Except Pediatric ($311,630) and Psychiatrists ($278,100). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
