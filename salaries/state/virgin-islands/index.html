How pay is spread in Virgin Islands

By median pay, Virgin Islands is number 51 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $26,340 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $89,620. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $31,070 to $62,100. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $52,730, 23% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 32,090 jobs counted here are less than 0.1% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Virgin Islands are Office and Administrative Support (3,940 jobs, 15%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (3,900 jobs, 14%), Sales and Related (3,060 jobs, 11%) and Management (2,470 jobs, 9%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Security Guards (3.9 times the national share), Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners (3.3 times the national share) and Maintenance and Repair Workers, General (2.2 times the national share). Questions about pay in Virgin Islands

What is the median salary in Virgin Islands?

The median pay of all employees in Virgin Islands was $42,870 a year ($20.61 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Virgin Islands?

What are the highest-paying jobs in Virgin Islands?
