How pay is spread in Minnesota

By median pay, Minnesota is number 12 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $33,540 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $125,730. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $39,680 to $82,520. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $71,160, 25% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 2,949,820 jobs counted here are 1.9% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Minnesota are Office and Administrative Support (326,230 jobs, 11%), Sales and Related (251,030 jobs, 9%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (242,410 jobs, 8%) and Transportation and Material Moving (230,100 jobs, 8%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Underground Mining Machine Operators, All Other (15.4 times the national share), Gambling Service Workers, All Other (11.0 times the national share) and Prepress Technicians and Workers (6.4 times the national share). The table on this page lists 733 occupations; for 9 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 359 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 239 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 67 pay less and 53 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Brickmasons and Blockmasons ($95,220 here, $62,120 nationally), Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters ($94,410 here, $63,800 nationally) and General Internal Medicine Physicians ($373,700 here, $256,560 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Firefighters ($38,110 here, $59,280 nationally), Waiters and Waitresses ($25,470 here, $35,230 nationally) and Bartenders ($26,900 here, $34,340 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Minnesota

What is the median salary in Minnesota?

The median pay of all employees in Minnesota was $56,920 a year ($27.36 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Minnesota?

Home Health and Personal Care Aides, with 128,680 jobs and a median pay of $38,370 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Minnesota?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Radiologists ($708,340), Dermatologists ($625,790) and Surgeons, All Other ($568,150). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
