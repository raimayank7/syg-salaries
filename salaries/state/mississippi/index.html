How pay is spread in Mississippi

By median pay, Mississippi is number 52 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $22,970 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $84,500. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $29,760 to $60,180. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $50,810, 27% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 1,163,030 jobs counted here are 0.7% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Mississippi are Office and Administrative Support (139,920 jobs, 12%), Transportation and Material Moving (122,270 jobs, 11%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (109,450 jobs, 10%) and Sales and Related (108,950 jobs, 10%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Upholsterers (16.0 times the national share), Psychiatric Aides (9.4 times the national share) and Logging Equipment Operators (7.8 times the national share). The table on this page lists 689 occupations; for 16 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 210 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 4 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 202 pay less and 4 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Health Specialties Teachers, Postsecondary ($137,220 here, $107,310 nationally), Tutors ($49,610 here, $43,350 nationally) and Coating, Painting, and Spraying Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders ($53,170 here, $48,250 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for First-Line Supervisors of Firefighting and Prevention Workers ($48,250 here, $93,530 nationally), Substitute Teachers, Short-Term ($22,340 here, $41,670 nationally) and First-Line Supervisors of Police and Detectives ($59,860 here, $106,040 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Mississippi

What is the median salary in Mississippi?

The median pay of all employees in Mississippi was $40,120 a year ($19.29 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Mississippi?

Retail Salespersons, with 38,400 jobs and a median pay of $28,640 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Mississippi?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Orthopedic Surgeons, Except Pediatric ($544,130), General Internal Medicine Physicians ($325,610) and Physicians, Pathologists ($261,570). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
