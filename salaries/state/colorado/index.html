How pay is spread in Colorado

By median pay, Colorado is number 5 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $36,350 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $136,330. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $43,680 to $93,220. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $77,190, 29% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 2,873,900 jobs counted here are 1.8% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Colorado are Office and Administrative Support (301,150 jobs, 11%), Sales and Related (296,170 jobs, 10%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (269,680 jobs, 9%) and Business and Financial Operations (264,100 jobs, 9%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Atmospheric and Space Scientists (6.2 times the national share), Mining and Geological Engineers, Including Mining Safety Engineers (6.0 times the national share) and Information and Record Clerks, All Other (5.6 times the national share). The table on this page lists 750 occupations; for 13 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 365 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 289 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 40 pay less and 36 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers ($100,080 here, $69,990 nationally), Administrative Services Managers ($157,000 here, $114,130 nationally) and Lodging Managers ($91,750 here, $69,250 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Physicists ($104,580 here, $172,250 nationally), Business Teachers, Postsecondary ($78,210 here, $99,080 nationally) and Plant and System Operators, All Other ($51,600 here, $62,470 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Colorado

What is the median salary in Colorado?

The median pay of all employees in Colorado was $59,800 a year ($28.75 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Colorado?

Fast Food and Counter Workers, with 82,010 jobs and a median pay of $36,270 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Colorado?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Orthodontists ($416,000), Dermatologists ($398,810) and Orthopedic Surgeons, Except Pediatric ($385,410). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
