How pay is spread in Montana

By median pay, Montana is number 30 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $29,390 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $100,610. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $37,210 to $72,640. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $60,880, 25% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 511,580 jobs counted here are 0.3% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Montana are Office and Administrative Support (59,560 jobs, 12%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (52,830 jobs, 11%), Sales and Related (45,940 jobs, 9%) and Transportation and Material Moving (38,100 jobs, 8%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners (35.6 times the national share), Forest and Conservation Technicians (17.6 times the national share) and Gambling Service Workers, All Other (17.1 times the national share). The table on this page lists 614 occupations; for 10 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 117 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 23 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 72 pay less and 22 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters ($79,960 here, $63,800 nationally), Electricians ($76,760 here, $63,190 nationally) and Highway Maintenance Workers ($60,960 here, $50,260 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners ($22,030 here, $34,980 nationally), Lawyers ($103,720 here, $159,670 nationally) and Waiters and Waitresses ($23,370 here, $35,230 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Montana

What is the median salary in Montana?

The median pay of all employees in Montana was $48,740 a year ($23.43 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Montana?

Fast Food and Counter Workers, with 14,960 jobs and a median pay of $29,090 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Montana?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Orthopedic Surgeons, Except Pediatric ($560,820), Physicians, All Other ($438,850) and Radiologists ($360,330). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
