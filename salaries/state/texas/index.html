How pay is spread in Texas

By median pay, Texas is number 31 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $27,250 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $125,220. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $35,370 to $77,840. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $65,750, 35% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 14,071,160 jobs counted here are 9.0% of all jobs in the national figures.

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Texas are Office and Administrative Support (1,725,580 jobs, 12%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (1,299,230 jobs, 9%), Transportation and Material Moving (1,293,420 jobs, 9%) and Management (1,212,070 jobs, 9%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Wellhead Pumpers (6.3 times the national share), Derrick Operators, Oil and Gas (6.1 times the national share) and Roustabouts, Oil and Gas (6.0 times the national share). The table on this page lists 801 occupations; for 4 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 609 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 83 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 445 pay less and 81 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Court Reporters and Simultaneous Captioners ($110,080 here, $72,420 nationally), Geoscientists, Except Hydrologists and Geographers ($145,220 here, $101,920 nationally) and Chemical Plant and System Operators ($102,750 here, $78,120 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Fashion Designers ($40,040 here, $80,960 nationally), Surgeons, All Other ($222,820 here, $414,010 nationally) and Anesthesiologists ($215,230 here, $391,490 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Texas

What is the median salary in Texas?

The median pay of all employees in Texas was $48,620 a year ($23.38 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Texas?

Fast Food and Counter Workers, with 460,530 jobs and a median pay of $27,740 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Texas?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Cardiologists ($410,150), Neurologists ($360,640) and Physicians, Pathologists ($345,830). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
