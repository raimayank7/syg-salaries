How pay is spread in Iowa

By median pay, Iowa is number 32 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $28,930 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $99,630. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $36,570 to $70,290. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $59,830, 23% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 1,559,240 jobs counted here are 1.0% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Iowa are Office and Administrative Support (169,290 jobs, 11%), Transportation and Material Moving (145,490 jobs, 9%), Production (141,560 jobs, 9%) and Sales and Related (140,810 jobs, 9%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Wind Turbine Service Technicians (9.0 times the national share), Food Processing Workers, All Other (8.0 times the national share) and Farm and Home Management Educators (6.3 times the national share). The table on this page lists 718 occupations; for 7 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 263 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 49 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 170 pay less and 44 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Crushing, Grinding, and Polishing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders ($62,590 here, $48,540 nationally), Driver/Sales Workers ($47,120 here, $38,770 nationally) and Parts Salespersons ($46,670 here, $38,630 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Waiters and Waitresses ($21,000 here, $35,230 nationally), Bartenders ($21,720 here, $34,340 nationally) and Lifeguards, Ski Patrol, and Other Recreational Protective Service Workers ($25,460 here, $33,580 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Iowa

What is the median salary in Iowa?

The median pay of all employees in Iowa was $48,540 a year ($23.34 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Iowa?

Cashiers, with 41,450 jobs and a median pay of $28,970 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Iowa?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Emergency Medicine Physicians ($429,050), Dermatologists ($424,970) and Radiologists ($405,220). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
