Common questions

Where do these salary figures come from?

From the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, which surveys employers across the country every year. This site shows the May 2025 estimates and adds nothing of its own.

What is the median salary in the United States?

The median pay for all occupations was $50,980 a year, or $24.51 an hour, in May 2025. The average was $69,770 a year.

Why can I not find my exact job title?

Official statistics group jobs into 830 broad occupations. Search for the main word of the job, such as engineer, clerk or technician, and choose the closest occupation.

Are these figures before or after tax?

Before tax. They also leave out overtime pay and benefits. How to read the figures.

Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025. Checked in November 2026. Figures are for employees, before tax, and leave out overtime pay and benefits. They are official statistics, not advice or a pay offer; see bls.gov/oes for the original tables.
