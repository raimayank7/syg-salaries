Postsecondary Teachers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Postsecondary Teachers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $77,640 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Postsecondary Teachers, All Other is $77,640, which is 52% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 30 by median pay. With 149,840 jobs, it makes up about 9.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($90,630) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Postsecondary Teachers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Delaware ($101,070), California ($100,690) and Massachusetts ($94,480). The lowest was in Wyoming ($46,710), Hawaii ($49,730) and Alabama ($51,060). The most jobs were in California (37,770), Florida (14,730) and Pennsylvania (12,100). The occupation is most concentrated in Louisiana, where its share of all jobs is 3.7 times the national share.
