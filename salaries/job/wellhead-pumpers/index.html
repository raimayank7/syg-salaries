Wellhead Pumpers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Wellhead Pumpers in the United States earned a median wage of $69,960 a year ($33.63 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Wellhead Pumpers is $69,960, which is 37% higher. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 11 by median pay. With 17,990 jobs, it makes up about 1.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Wellhead Pumpers pay by state

The highest median pay was in North Dakota ($79,820), New Mexico ($78,010) and Louisiana ($76,930). The lowest was in Pennsylvania ($45,490), Ohio ($46,750) and Michigan ($47,630). The most jobs were in Texas (10,250), North Dakota (1,540) and New Mexico (1,060). The occupation is most concentrated in North Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 31.0 times the national share.
