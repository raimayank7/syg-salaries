Special Education Teachers, Kindergarten and Elementary School: pay in the United States, May 2025

Special Education Teachers, Kindergarten and Elementary School in the United States earned a median wage of $65,120 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Special Education Teachers, Kindergarten and Elementary School is $65,120, which is 28% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 45 by median pay. With 260,870 jobs, it makes up about 17 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($72,650) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Special Education Teachers, Kindergarten and Elementary School pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($97,910), California ($97,170) and Washington ($95,040). The lowest was in West Virginia ($50,380), Mississippi ($50,570) and Oklahoma ($50,630). The most jobs were in Texas (56,760), California (23,280) and New York (21,130). The occupation is most concentrated in Texas, where its share of all jobs is 2.4 times the national share.
