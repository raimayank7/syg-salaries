Fish and Game Wardens: pay in the United States, May 2025

Fish and Game Wardens in the United States earned a median wage of $74,060 a year ($35.60 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Patrol assigned area to prevent fish and game law violations. Investigate reports of damage to crops or property by wildlife. Compile biological data.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 33-3031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Fish and Game Wardens is $74,060, which is 45% higher. Among the 24 occupations of the field Protective Service, it ranks number 9 by median pay. With 5,770 jobs, it makes up about 0.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Fish and Game Wardens pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($100,090), Iowa ($98,260) and Indiana ($93,990). The lowest was in Florida ($31,200), Pennsylvania ($50,250) and Kentucky ($54,570). The most jobs were in Texas (490), New York (390) and California (330). The occupation is most concentrated in Maine, where its share of all jobs is 7.9 times the national share.
