Credit Counselors: pay in the United States, May 2025

Credit Counselors in the United States earned a median wage of $52,230 a year ($25.11 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Advise and educate individuals or organizations on acquiring and managing debt. May provide guidance in determining the best type of loan and explain loan requirements or restrictions. May help develop debt management plans or student financial aid packages. May advise on credit issues, or provide budget, mortgage, bankruptcy, or student financial aid counseling.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 13-2071.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Credit Counselors is $52,230, which is 2% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 32 by median pay. With 27,770 jobs, it makes up about 1.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Credit Counselors pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($67,120), Georgia ($64,080) and District of Columbia ($63,580). The lowest was in Missouri ($30,580), Tennessee ($38,730) and Wyoming ($42,580). The most jobs were in Texas (3,950), Florida (2,410) and California (2,160). The occupation is most concentrated in Arizona, where its share of all jobs is 1.7 times the national share.
