Fallers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Fallers in the United States earned a median wage of $52,100 a year ($25.05 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Use axes or chainsaws to fell trees using knowledge of tree characteristics and cutting techniques to control direction of fall and minimize tree damage.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 45-4021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Fallers is $52,100, which is 2% higher. Among the 13 occupations of the field Farming, Fishing, and Forestry, it ranks number 2 by median pay. With 3,130 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Fallers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oregon ($84,490), Idaho ($78,330) and Washington ($77,340). The lowest was in Indiana ($26,210), New York ($36,190) and Georgia ($39,380). The most jobs were in Michigan (410), Virginia (330) and California (260). The occupation is most concentrated in Idaho, where its share of all jobs is 11.0 times the national share.
