Engineering Technologists and Technicians, Except Drafters, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Engineering Technologists and Technicians, Except Drafters, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $78,350 a year ($37.67 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Engineering Technologists and Technicians, Except Drafters, All Other is $78,350, which is 54% higher. Among the 36 occupations of the field Architecture and Engineering, it ranks number 23 by median pay. With 59,930 jobs, it makes up about 3.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Engineering Technologists and Technicians, Except Drafters, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Maine ($107,060), New Mexico ($101,750) and Maryland ($99,970). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($43,640), Vermont ($52,770) and Tennessee ($58,700). The most jobs were in California (7,330), Texas (7,110) and Virginia (3,720). The occupation is most concentrated in New Mexico, where its share of all jobs is 4.1 times the national share.
