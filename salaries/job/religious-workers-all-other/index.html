Religious Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Religious Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $45,280 a year ($21.77 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Religious Workers, All Other is $45,280, which is 11% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Community and Social Service, it ranks number 17 by median pay. With 15,760 jobs, it makes up about 1.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($53,870) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Religious Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($62,620), New York ($51,150) and Arizona ($50,750). The lowest was in Kentucky ($34,090), Pennsylvania ($34,580) and Texas ($36,420). The most jobs were in California (1,390), Pennsylvania (940) and Michigan (810). The occupation is most concentrated in Oregon, where its share of all jobs is 3.3 times the national share.
