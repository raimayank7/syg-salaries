First-Line Supervisors of Transportation and Material Moving Workers, Except Aircraft Cargo Handling Supervisors pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($74,250), Maryland ($74,070) and Rhode Island ($72,750). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($35,720), Guam ($37,550) and Virgin Islands ($48,160).
