Computer Network Architects: pay in the United States, May 2025

Computer Network Architects in the United States earned a median wage of $134,050 a year ($64.45 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Design and implement computer and information networks, such as local area networks (LAN), wide area networks (WAN), intranets, extranets, and other data communications networks. Perform network modeling, analysis, and planning, including analysis of capacity needs for network infrastructures. May also design network and computer security measures. May research and recommend network and data communications hardware and software. Excludes “Information Security Analysts” (15-1212), “Computer Network Support Specialists” (15-1231), and “Network and Computer Systems Administrators” (15-1244).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 15-1241.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Computer Network Architects is $134,050, which is 163% higher. Among the 21 occupations of the field Computer and Mathematical, it ranks number 4 by median pay. With 179,740 jobs, it makes up about 12 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Computer Network Architects pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($168,070), New Jersey ($162,350) and Maryland ($159,460). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($78,200), Mississippi ($90,990) and Wyoming ($100,520). The most jobs were in Colorado (16,380), California (14,870) and Texas (13,650). The occupation is most concentrated in Colorado, where its share of all jobs is 4.9 times the national share.
