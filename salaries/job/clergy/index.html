Clergy: pay in the United States, May 2025

Clergy in the United States earned a median wage of $60,810 a year ($29.24 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Clergy is $60,810, which is 19% higher. Among the 17 occupations of the field Community and Social Service, it ranks number 7 by median pay. With 57,200 jobs, it makes up about 3.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Clergy pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($80,830), California ($77,710) and Nevada ($73,910). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($28,880), Guam ($37,460) and West Virginia ($48,460). The most jobs were in New York (7,790), California (7,110) and Oregon (3,850). The occupation is most concentrated in Oregon, where its share of all jobs is 5.3 times the national share.
