Loan Interviewers and Clerks: pay in the United States, May 2025

Loan Interviewers and Clerks in the United States earned a median wage of $50,020 a year ($24.05 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Interview loan applicants to elicit information; investigate applicants’ backgrounds and verify references; prepare loan request papers; and forward findings, reports, and documents to appraisal department. Review loan papers to ensure completeness, and complete transactions between loan establishment, borrowers, and sellers upon approval of loan.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 43-4131.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Loan Interviewers and Clerks is $50,020, which is about the same. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 23 by median pay. With 164,790 jobs, it makes up about 11 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Loan Interviewers and Clerks pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($77,080), Maryland ($59,430) and Connecticut ($58,980). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($35,940), Mississippi ($39,120) and Alabama ($40,300). The most jobs were in Texas (17,090), California (14,680) and Florida (10,690). The occupation is most concentrated in South Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 3.5 times the national share.
