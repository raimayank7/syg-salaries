Transportation Security Screeners: pay in the United States, May 2025

Transportation Security Screeners in the United States earned a median wage of $66,770 a year ($32.10 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Conduct screening of passengers, baggage, or cargo to ensure compliance with Transportation Security Administration (TSA) regulations. May operate basic security equipment such as x-ray machines and hand wands at screening checkpoints.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 33-9093.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Transportation Security Screeners is $66,770, which is 31% higher. Among the 24 occupations of the field Protective Service, it ranks number 10 by median pay. With 50,290 jobs, it makes up about 3.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Transportation Security Screeners pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Hampshire ($76,130), New Jersey ($74,400) and California ($73,610). The lowest was in Missouri ($51,210), Montana ($54,770) and North Dakota ($54,950). The most jobs were in Florida (6,920), California (5,410) and Texas (4,300). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 12.9 times the national share.
