Social Work Teachers, Postsecondary: pay in the United States, May 2025

Social Work Teachers, Postsecondary in the United States earned a median wage of $77,570 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Teach courses in social work. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 25-1113.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Social Work Teachers, Postsecondary is $77,570, which is 52% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 31 by median pay. With 12,610 jobs, it makes up about 0.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Social Work Teachers, Postsecondary pay by state

The highest median pay was in Hawaii ($133,340), Kansas ($108,610) and Delaware ($100,550). The lowest was in Montana ($43,010), South Dakota ($60,100) and Arkansas ($60,470). The most jobs were in New York (1,840), Pennsylvania (1,050) and Ohio (670). The occupation is most concentrated in New Mexico, where its share of all jobs is 3.0 times the national share.
