First-Line Supervisors of Housekeeping and Janitorial Workers: pay in the United States, May 2025

First-Line Supervisors of Housekeeping and Janitorial Workers in the United States earned a median wage of $49,100 a year ($23.61 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for First-Line Supervisors of Housekeeping and Janitorial Workers is $49,100, which is 4% lower. Among the 10 occupations of the field Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance, it ranks number 3 by median pay. With 178,760 jobs, it makes up about 11 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

First-Line Supervisors of Housekeeping and Janitorial Workers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($63,550), Rhode Island ($63,520) and California ($61,470). The lowest was in Guam ($28,030), Arkansas ($37,500) and Louisiana ($38,640). The most jobs were in California (18,060), Florida (15,320) and Texas (13,370). The occupation is most concentrated in Virgin Islands, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.
