Choreographers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Choreographers in the United States earned a median wage of $55,310 a year ($26.59 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Choreographers is $55,310, which is 8% higher. Among the 41 occupations of the field Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media, it ranks number 31 by median pay. With 2,860 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($73,100) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Choreographers pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($173,100), California ($75,000) and Minnesota ($74,810). The lowest was in Utah ($33,640), Illinois ($34,560) and Florida ($36,670). The most jobs were in California (410), Texas (380) and New York (340). The occupation is most concentrated in South Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 29.8 times the national share.
