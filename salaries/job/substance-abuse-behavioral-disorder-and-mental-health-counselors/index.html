Substance Abuse, Behavioral Disorder, and Mental Health Counselors: pay in the United States, May 2025

Substance Abuse, Behavioral Disorder, and Mental Health Counselors in the United States earned a median wage of $59,350 a year ($28.53 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Substance Abuse, Behavioral Disorder, and Mental Health Counselors is $59,350, which is 16% higher. Among the 17 occupations of the field Community and Social Service, it ranks number 10 by median pay. With 491,930 jobs, it makes up about 32 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Substance Abuse, Behavioral Disorder, and Mental Health Counselors pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($80,770), Oregon ($74,590) and New Mexico ($74,100). The lowest was in Louisiana ($37,910), Puerto Rico ($46,790) and Mississippi ($46,990). The most jobs were in California (75,610), Pennsylvania (30,620) and Florida (29,750). The occupation is most concentrated in New Hampshire, where its share of all jobs is 1.7 times the national share.
