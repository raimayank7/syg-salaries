Pharmacists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Pharmacists in the United States earned a median wage of $140,910 a year ($67.75 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Dispense drugs prescribed by physicians and other health practitioners and provide information to patients about medications and their use. May advise physicians and other health practitioners on the selection, dosage, interactions, and side effects of medications.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1051.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Pharmacists is $140,910, which is 176% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 26 by median pay. With 321,970 jobs, it makes up about 21 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Pharmacists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($167,310), Oregon ($165,960) and California ($164,610). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($111,270), Rhode Island ($128,180) and Guam ($129,090). The most jobs were in California (34,030), Texas (24,700) and Florida (21,540). The occupation is most concentrated in West Virginia, where its share of all jobs is 1.6 times the national share.
