Water and Wastewater Treatment Plant and System Operators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Water and Wastewater Treatment Plant and System Operators in the United States earned a median wage of $60,020 a year ($28.86 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Operate or control an entire process or system of machines, often through the use of control boards, to transfer or treat water or wastewater.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Water and Wastewater Treatment Plant and System Operators is $60,020, which is 18% higher. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 17 by median pay. With 128,490 jobs, it makes up about 8.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Water and Wastewater Treatment Plant and System Operators pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($81,770), Nevada ($81,440) and Washington ($80,800). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($29,220), Arkansas ($44,320) and Kentucky ($45,430). The most jobs were in California (11,630), Texas (10,520) and Florida (7,440). The occupation is most concentrated in New Mexico, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.
