Kindergarten Teachers, Except Special Education: pay in the United States, May 2025

Kindergarten Teachers, Except Special Education in the United States earned a median wage of $62,680 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Kindergarten Teachers, Except Special Education is $62,680, which is 23% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 53 by median pay. With 108,870 jobs, it makes up about 7.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($69,370) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Kindergarten Teachers, Except Special Education pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($99,960), Connecticut ($90,030) and Rhode Island ($85,470). The lowest was in Montana ($47,590), Oklahoma ($47,860) and Mississippi ($48,880). The most jobs were in Florida (8,810), California (7,430) and New York (6,950). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 6.3 times the national share.
