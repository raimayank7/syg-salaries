Couriers and Messengers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Couriers and Messengers in the United States earned a median wage of $39,200 a year ($18.85 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Pick up and deliver messages, documents, packages, and other items between offices or departments within an establishment or directly to other business concerns, traveling by foot, bicycle, motorcycle, automobile, or public conveyance. Excludes “Light Truck Drivers” (53-3033).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 43-5021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Couriers and Messengers is $39,200, which is 23% lower. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 49 by median pay. With 68,640 jobs, it makes up about 4.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Couriers and Messengers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Nevada ($46,520), Washington ($46,500) and Vermont ($45,600). The lowest was in Virgin Islands ($21,840), Puerto Rico ($23,160) and Guam ($26,400). The most jobs were in New York (7,320), California (7,240) and Florida (4,910). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 7.0 times the national share.
