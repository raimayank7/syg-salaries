Life, Physical, and Social Science Technicians, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Life, Physical, and Social Science Technicians, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $62,280 a year ($29.94 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Life, Physical, and Social Science Technicians, All Other is $62,280, which is 22% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 39 by median pay. With 73,910 jobs, it makes up about 4.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Life, Physical, and Social Science Technicians, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oklahoma ($86,260), District of Columbia ($79,230) and California ($78,350). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($35,060), Wisconsin ($47,210) and Nebraska ($48,640). The most jobs were in California (10,090), Texas (9,170) and New York (7,390). The occupation is most concentrated in Hawaii, where its share of all jobs is 3.5 times the national share.
