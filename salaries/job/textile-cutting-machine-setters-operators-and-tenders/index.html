Textile Cutting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders: pay in the United States, May 2025

Textile Cutting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders in the United States earned a median wage of $38,760 a year ($18.64 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Textile Cutting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders is $38,760, which is 24% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 93 by median pay. With 9,000 jobs, it makes up about 0.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Textile Cutting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders pay by state

The highest median pay was in Connecticut ($47,160), New York ($46,810) and New Hampshire ($45,930). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,880), Nevada ($28,630) and Arkansas ($29,140). The most jobs were in Georgia (1,560), North Carolina (840) and California (750). The occupation is most concentrated in Mississippi, where its share of all jobs is 5.9 times the national share.
