Office and Administrative Support Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Office and Administrative Support Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $45,670 a year ($21.96 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Office and Administrative Support Workers, All Other is $45,670, which is 10% lower. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 38 by median pay. With 192,260 jobs, it makes up about 12 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Office and Administrative Support Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($68,600), Idaho ($61,140) and Rhode Island ($58,470). The lowest was in West Virginia ($26,580), Kansas ($29,970) and Ohio ($30,060). The most jobs were in Florida (27,020), California (23,120) and Texas (22,960). The occupation is most concentrated in Maryland, where its share of all jobs is 4.7 times the national share.
