Computer, Automated Teller, and Office Machine Repairers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Computer, Automated Teller, and Office Machine Repairers in the United States earned a median wage of $47,810 a year ($22.99 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Computer, Automated Teller, and Office Machine Repairers is $47,810, which is 6% lower. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 44 by median pay. With 65,600 jobs, it makes up about 4.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Computer, Automated Teller, and Office Machine Repairers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Connecticut ($61,190), New Hampshire ($60,570) and South Dakota ($59,700). The lowest was in Guam ($32,360), Puerto Rico ($37,560) and Nevada ($38,430). The most jobs were in California (7,250), Texas (7,140) and Florida (3,470). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 3.1 times the national share.
