Food Scientists and Technologists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Food Scientists and Technologists in the United States earned a median wage of $88,720 a year ($42.65 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Use chemistry, microbiology, engineering, and other sciences to study the principles underlying the processing and deterioration of foods; analyze food content to determine levels of vitamins, fat, sugar, and protein; discover new food sources; research ways to make processed foods safe, palatable, and healthful; and apply food science knowledge to determine best ways to process, package, preserve, store, and distribute food.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 19-1012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Food Scientists and Technologists is $88,720, which is 74% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 25 by median pay. With 13,060 jobs, it makes up about 0.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Food Scientists and Technologists pay by state

The highest median pay was in South Carolina ($108,660), Missouri ($105,930) and New Jersey ($104,340). The lowest was in Montana ($52,230), Indiana ($69,350) and South Dakota ($71,330). The most jobs were in California (2,210), Texas (840) and Illinois (780). The occupation is most concentrated in Kansas, where its share of all jobs is 3.2 times the national share.
