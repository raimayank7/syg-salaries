Obstetricians and Gynecologists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Obstetricians and Gynecologists in the United States earned a median wage of $292,910 a year ($140.82 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Provide medical care related to pregnancy or childbirth. Diagnose, treat, and help prevent diseases of women, particularly those affecting the reproductive system. May also provide general care to women. May perform both medical and gynecological surgery functions.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1218.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Obstetricians and Gynecologists is $292,910, which is 475% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 13 by median pay. With 21,260 jobs, it makes up about 1.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Obstetricians and Gynecologists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Utah ($437,170), Alaska ($421,450) and Vermont ($418,760). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($131,890), North Carolina ($134,540) and Rhode Island ($166,390). The most jobs were in New York (2,750), Florida (1,620) and California (1,610). The occupation is most concentrated in Delaware, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.
