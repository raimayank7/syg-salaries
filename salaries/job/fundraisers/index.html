Fundraisers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Fundraisers in the United States earned a median wage of $72,550 a year ($34.88 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Organize activities to raise funds or otherwise solicit and gather monetary donations or other gifts for an organization. May design and produce promotional materials. May also raise awareness of the organization’s work, goals, and financial needs.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 13-1131.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Fundraisers is $72,550, which is 42% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 25 by median pay. With 111,040 jobs, it makes up about 7.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Fundraisers pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($80,840), New York ($80,540) and Maryland ($79,780). The lowest was in Arkansas ($50,190), Puerto Rico ($52,090) and West Virginia ($54,760). The most jobs were in California (12,860), New York (11,600) and Pennsylvania (6,180). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 4.7 times the national share.
