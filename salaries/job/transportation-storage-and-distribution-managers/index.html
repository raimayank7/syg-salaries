Transportation, Storage, and Distribution Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Transportation, Storage, and Distribution Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $107,230 a year ($51.55 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate transportation, storage, or distribution activities in accordance with organizational policies and applicable government laws or regulations. Includes logistics managers.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Transportation, Storage, and Distribution Managers is $107,230, which is 110% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 20 by median pay. With 221,180 jobs, it makes up about 14 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($121,600) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Transportation, Storage, and Distribution Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Delaware ($145,000), Washington ($136,500) and Colorado ($132,610). The lowest was in Virgin Islands ($56,390), Guam ($83,360) and Puerto Rico ($83,560). The most jobs were in California (30,540), Texas (28,710) and Illinois (11,030). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 1.5 times the national share.
