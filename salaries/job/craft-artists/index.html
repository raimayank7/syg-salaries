Craft Artists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Craft Artists in the United States earned a median wage of $46,080 a year ($22.15 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Create or reproduce handmade objects for sale and exhibition using a variety of techniques, such as welding, weaving, pottery, and needlecraft.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 27-1012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Craft Artists is $46,080, which is 10% lower. Among the 41 occupations of the field Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media, it ranks number 35 by median pay. With 4,580 jobs, it makes up about 0.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Craft Artists pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($100,540), Missouri ($61,780) and Wisconsin ($58,650). The lowest was in Michigan ($25,960), Minnesota ($27,560) and Tennessee ($32,830). The most jobs were in Texas (870), Michigan (290) and Florida (270). The occupation is most concentrated in Oregon, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.
