Morticians, Undertakers, and Funeral Arrangers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Morticians, Undertakers, and Funeral Arrangers in the United States earned a median wage of $55,010 a year ($26.45 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Perform various tasks to arrange and direct individual funeral services, such as coordinating transportation of body to mortuary, interviewing family or other authorized person to arrange details, selecting pallbearers, aiding with the selection of officials for religious rites, and providing transportation for mourners. Excludes “Funeral Home Managers” (11-9171).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 39-4031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Morticians, Undertakers, and Funeral Arrangers is $55,010, which is 8% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Personal Care and Service, it ranks number 4 by median pay. With 25,100 jobs, it makes up about 1.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Morticians, Undertakers, and Funeral Arrangers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Delaware ($81,530), Utah ($72,800) and Illinois ($69,600). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($25,630), Arkansas ($36,120) and South Carolina ($40,020). The most jobs were in California (2,240), Florida (1,510) and Texas (1,500). The occupation is most concentrated in South Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 2.7 times the national share.
