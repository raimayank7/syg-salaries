Epidemiologists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Epidemiologists in the United States earned a median wage of $87,220 a year ($41.93 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Epidemiologists is $87,220, which is 71% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 27 by median pay. With 12,090 jobs, it makes up about 0.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($97,890) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Epidemiologists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($115,890), District of Columbia ($112,370) and California ($110,760). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($48,380), Mississippi ($56,930) and Idaho ($66,280). The most jobs were in California (1,480), Texas (1,160) and Washington (1,010). The occupation is most concentrated in Washington, where its share of all jobs is 3.7 times the national share.
