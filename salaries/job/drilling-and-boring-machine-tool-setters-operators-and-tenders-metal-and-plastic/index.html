Drilling and Boring Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Drilling and Boring Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $49,080 a year ($23.60 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Drilling and Boring Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic is $49,080, which is 4% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 39 by median pay. With 4,680 jobs, it makes up about 0.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Drilling and Boring Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in Virginia ($90,390), Massachusetts ($77,780) and Idaho ($69,590). The lowest was in Indiana ($39,110), Connecticut ($41,080) and Iowa ($43,860). The most jobs were in California (690), Tennessee (400) and Illinois (390). The occupation is most concentrated in Oklahoma, where its share of all jobs is 4.9 times the national share.
