Educational Instruction and Library Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Educational Instruction and Library Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $50,890 a year ($24.47 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Educational Instruction and Library Workers, All Other is $50,890, which is about the same. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 57 by median pay. With 118,590 jobs, it makes up about 7.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($59,060) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Educational Instruction and Library Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($83,130), New Hampshire ($70,990) and Hawaii ($64,450). The lowest was in Georgia ($24,780), Puerto Rico ($31,200) and West Virginia ($37,230). The most jobs were in California (39,440), Georgia (12,240) and Texas (9,020). The occupation is most concentrated in Georgia, where its share of all jobs is 3.3 times the national share.
