Billing and Posting Clerks: pay in the United States, May 2025

Billing and Posting Clerks in the United States earned a median wage of $48,500 a year ($23.32 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Compile, compute, and record billing, accounting, statistical, and other numerical data for billing purposes. Prepare billing invoices for services rendered or for delivery or shipment of goods. Excludes “Medical Records Specialists” (29-2072).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 43-3021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Billing and Posting Clerks is $48,500, which is 5% lower. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 28 by median pay. With 404,060 jobs, it makes up about 26 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Billing and Posting Clerks pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($64,330), California ($56,260) and Massachusetts ($56,110). The lowest was in Guam ($26,900), Puerto Rico ($28,490) and Arkansas ($38,780). The most jobs were in California (41,290), New York (32,770) and Texas (31,150). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 2.0 times the national share.
