Education and Childcare Administrators, Preschool and Daycare: pay in the United States, May 2025

Education and Childcare Administrators, Preschool and Daycare in the United States earned a median wage of $59,300 a year ($28.51 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate academic or nonacademic activities of preschools or childcare centers and programs, including before- and after-school care. Excludes “Preschool Teachers, Except Special Education” (25-2011) and “Childcare Workers” (39-9011).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-9031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Education and Childcare Administrators, Preschool and Daycare is $59,300, which is 16% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 37 by median pay. With 73,660 jobs, it makes up about 4.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Education and Childcare Administrators, Preschool and Daycare pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($96,810), New Jersey ($78,770) and District of Columbia ($77,850). The lowest was in Guam ($33,030), Alabama ($41,780) and Kentucky ($44,530). The most jobs were in California (9,810), Florida (5,980) and Texas (5,480). The occupation is most concentrated in Delaware, where its share of all jobs is 1.8 times the national share.
