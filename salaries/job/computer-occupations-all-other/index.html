Computer Occupations, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Computer Occupations, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $116,580 a year ($56.05 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Computer Occupations, All Other is $116,580, which is 129% higher. Among the 21 occupations of the field Computer and Mathematical, it ranks number 9 by median pay. With 435,370 jobs, it makes up about 28 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Computer Occupations, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($156,590), Maryland ($144,680) and Colorado ($139,580). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($60,470), Tennessee ($76,470) and Guam ($76,770). The most jobs were in California (80,150), Texas (57,020) and Georgia (20,630). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 8.8 times the national share.
