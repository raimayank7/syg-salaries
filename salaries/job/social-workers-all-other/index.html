Social Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Social Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $71,900 a year ($34.57 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Social Workers, All Other is $71,900, which is 41% higher. Among the 17 occupations of the field Community and Social Service, it ranks number 1 by median pay. With 62,930 jobs, it makes up about 4.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Social Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Nevada ($121,830), Hawaii ($116,130) and Rhode Island ($111,590). The lowest was in Guam ($45,260), Puerto Rico ($47,580) and Arkansas ($47,790). The most jobs were in Minnesota (7,270), California (6,430) and Florida (4,800). The occupation is most concentrated in Minnesota, where its share of all jobs is 6.1 times the national share.
