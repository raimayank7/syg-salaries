Therapists, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Therapists, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $77,930 a year ($37.47 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Therapists, All Other is $77,930, which is 53% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 48 by median pay. With 22,640 jobs, it makes up about 1.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($90,420) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Therapists, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($127,010), Illinois ($116,200) and New Jersey ($100,250). The lowest was in Nevada ($38,260), Mississippi ($44,280) and Connecticut ($47,300). The most jobs were in New Jersey (7,090), Texas (1,320) and Maryland (1,310). The occupation is most concentrated in New Jersey, where its share of all jobs is 11.4 times the national share.
