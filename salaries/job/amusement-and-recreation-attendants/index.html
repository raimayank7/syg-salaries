Amusement and Recreation Attendants: pay in the United States, May 2025

Amusement and Recreation Attendants in the United States earned a median wage of $32,150 a year ($15.46 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Perform a variety of attending duties at amusement or recreation facility. May schedule use of recreation facilities, maintain and provide equipment to participants of sporting events or recreational pursuits, or operate amusement concessions and rides.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 39-3091.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Amusement and Recreation Attendants is $32,150, which is 37% lower. Among the 32 occupations of the field Personal Care and Service, it ranks number 32 by median pay. With 397,830 jobs, it makes up about 26 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Amusement and Recreation Attendants pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($45,680), Hawaii ($39,920) and District of Columbia ($37,960). The lowest was in Guam ($21,050), Oklahoma ($22,040) and Puerto Rico ($22,210). The most jobs were in California (56,920), Florida (45,540) and Texas (28,360). The occupation is most concentrated in Hawaii, where its share of all jobs is 2.5 times the national share.
