Occupational Health and Safety Technicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Occupational Health and Safety Technicians in the United States earned a median wage of $61,560 a year ($29.59 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Collect data on work environments for analysis by occupational health and safety specialists. Implement and conduct evaluation of programs designed to limit chemical, physical, biological, and ergonomic risks to workers.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 19-5012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Occupational Health and Safety Technicians is $61,560, which is 21% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 41 by median pay. With 30,590 jobs, it makes up about 2.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($68,120) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Occupational Health and Safety Technicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($104,910), Rhode Island ($84,640) and West Virginia ($81,470). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($35,890), Guam ($39,710) and Montana ($41,420). The most jobs were in Texas (4,420), California (2,650) and Illinois (1,790). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 10.5 times the national share.
