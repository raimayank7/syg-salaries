Computer Science Teachers, Postsecondary: pay in the United States, May 2025

Computer Science Teachers, Postsecondary in the United States earned a median wage of $96,980 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Teach courses in computer science. May specialize in a field of computer science, such as the design and function of computers or operations and research analysis. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 25-1021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Computer Science Teachers, Postsecondary is $96,980, which is 90% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 13 by median pay. With 35,480 jobs, it makes up about 2.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Computer Science Teachers, Postsecondary pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($128,210), Massachusetts ($128,030) and Utah ($124,460). The lowest was in Arkansas ($56,180), Wyoming ($63,900) and Kansas ($67,400). The most jobs were in Texas (3,540), New York (3,170) and California (2,480). The occupation is most concentrated in Massachusetts, where its share of all jobs is 1.8 times the national share.
