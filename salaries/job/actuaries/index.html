Actuaries: pay in the United States, May 2025

Actuaries in the United States earned a median wage of $130,000 a year ($62.50 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Analyze statistical data, such as mortality, accident, sickness, disability, and retirement rates and construct probability tables to forecast risk and liability for payment of future benefits. May ascertain insurance rates required and cash reserves necessary to ensure payment of future benefits.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 15-2011.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Actuaries is $130,000, which is 155% higher. Among the 21 occupations of the field Computer and Mathematical, it ranks number 5 by median pay. With 26,670 jobs, it makes up about 1.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Actuaries pay by state

The highest median pay was in Connecticut ($166,800), District of Columbia ($166,230) and New York ($156,480). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($68,100), Louisiana ($80,860) and Michigan ($100,640). The most jobs were in New York (2,630), Illinois (2,280) and Texas (1,750). The occupation is most concentrated in Connecticut, where its share of all jobs is 4.8 times the national share.
