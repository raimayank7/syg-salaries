Physicians, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Physicians, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $265,930 a year ($127.85 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Physicians, All Other is $265,930, which is 422% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 16 by median pay. With 342,720 jobs, it makes up about 22 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Physicians, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in North Dakota ($454,550), Montana ($438,850) and Maine ($419,410). The lowest was in District of Columbia ($77,430), Illinois ($132,540) and Arkansas ($136,880). The most jobs were in Texas (30,720), California (25,530) and Pennsylvania (23,800). The occupation is most concentrated in Pennsylvania, where its share of all jobs is 1.8 times the national share.
