Bioengineers and Biomedical Engineers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Bioengineers and Biomedical Engineers in the United States earned a median wage of $109,370 a year ($52.58 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Apply knowledge of engineering, biology, chemistry, computer science, and biomechanical principles to the design, development, and evaluation of biological, agricultural, and health systems and products, such as artificial organs, prostheses, instrumentation, medical information systems, and health management and care delivery systems.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 17-2031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Bioengineers and Biomedical Engineers is $109,370, which is 115% higher. Among the 36 occupations of the field Architecture and Engineering, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 23,480 jobs, it makes up about 1.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Bioengineers and Biomedical Engineers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Arizona ($141,230), Idaho ($133,750) and California ($128,310). The lowest was in Arkansas ($67,850), Puerto Rico ($80,210) and Iowa ($81,710). The most jobs were in Massachusetts (4,740), California (2,750) and Texas (2,050). The occupation is most concentrated in Massachusetts, where its share of all jobs is 8.6 times the national share.
