Cooks, Private Household: pay in the United States, May 2025

Cooks, Private Household in the United States earned a median wage of $47,940 a year ($23.05 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Cooks, Private Household is $47,940, which is 6% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Food Preparation and Serving Related, it ranks number 2 by median pay. With 1,100 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($53,680) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.
