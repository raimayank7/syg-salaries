Farm and Home Management Educators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Farm and Home Management Educators in the United States earned a median wage of $60,220 a year ($28.95 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Instruct and advise individuals and families engaged in agriculture, agricultural-related processes, or home management activities. Demonstrate procedures and apply research findings to advance agricultural and home management activities. May develop educational outreach programs. May instruct on either agricultural issues such as agricultural processes and techniques, pest management, and food safety, or on home management issues such as budgeting, nutrition, and child development. Excludes “Dietitians and Nutritionists” (29-1031).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 25-9021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Farm and Home Management Educators is $60,220, which is 18% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 55 by median pay. With 8,220 jobs, it makes up about 0.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Farm and Home Management Educators pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($98,770), Oregon ($80,030) and Maryland ($79,780). The lowest was in Texas ($33,260), Georgia ($33,510) and Pennsylvania ($45,970). The most jobs were in North Carolina (910), Florida (710) and Wisconsin (670). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 9.5 times the national share.
