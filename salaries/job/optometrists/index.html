Optometrists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Optometrists in the United States earned a median wage of $136,570 a year ($65.66 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Diagnose, manage, and treat conditions and diseases of the human eye and visual system. Examine eyes and visual system, diagnose problems or impairments, prescribe corrective lenses, and provide treatment. May prescribe therapeutic drugs to treat specific eye conditions. Ophthalmologists are included in “Ophthalmologists, Except Pediatric” (29-1241).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1041.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Optometrists is $136,570, which is 168% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 27 by median pay. With 42,790 jobs, it makes up about 2.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Optometrists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($170,310), Maryland ($165,840) and North Carolina ($161,560). The lowest was in Oklahoma ($97,350), South Dakota ($102,320) and Idaho ($103,390). The most jobs were in California (6,890), Texas (4,110) and New York (2,390). The occupation is most concentrated in Rhode Island, where its share of all jobs is 1.8 times the national share.
