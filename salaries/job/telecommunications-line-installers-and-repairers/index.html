Telecommunications Line Installers and Repairers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Telecommunications Line Installers and Repairers in the United States earned a median wage of $74,330 a year ($35.73 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Telecommunications Line Installers and Repairers is $74,330, which is 46% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 9 by median pay. With 97,720 jobs, it makes up about 6.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Telecommunications Line Installers and Repairers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Rhode Island ($103,810), Massachusetts ($103,410) and New Jersey ($102,090). The lowest was in Guam ($30,770), Puerto Rico ($31,770) and Louisiana ($48,770). The most jobs were in Texas (9,700), California (8,810) and New York (8,530). The occupation is most concentrated in West Virginia, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.
