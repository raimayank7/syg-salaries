Laborers and Freight, Stock, and Material Movers, Hand: pay in the United States, May 2025

Laborers and Freight, Stock, and Material Movers, Hand in the United States earned a median wage of $40,240 a year ($19.35 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Manually move freight, stock, luggage, or other materials, or perform other general labor. Includes all manual laborers not elsewhere classified. Excludes “Construction Laborers” (47-2061) and “Helpers, Construction Trades” (47-3011 through 47-3019). Excludes “Material Moving Workers” (53-7011 through 53-7199) who use power equipment.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 53-7062.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Laborers and Freight, Stock, and Material Movers, Hand is $40,240, which is 21% lower. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 40 by median pay. With 2,950,280 jobs, it makes up about 190 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Laborers and Freight, Stock, and Material Movers, Hand pay by state

The highest median pay was in Minnesota ($46,280), Washington ($46,190) and District of Columbia ($45,740). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($24,560), Virgin Islands ($31,810) and Louisiana ($35,340). The most jobs were in California (355,500), Texas (216,970) and Illinois (195,390). The occupation is most concentrated in Indiana, where its share of all jobs is 1.7 times the national share.
