Graders and Sorters, Agricultural Products: pay in the United States, May 2025

Graders and Sorters, Agricultural Products in the United States earned a median wage of $35,730 a year ($17.18 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Grade, sort, or classify unprocessed food and other agricultural products by size, weight, color, or condition. Excludes “Agricultural Inspectors” (45-2011).

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Graders and Sorters, Agricultural Products is $35,730, which is 30% lower. Among the 13 occupations of the field Farming, Fishing, and Forestry, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 25,180 jobs, it makes up about 1.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Graders and Sorters, Agricultural Products pay by state

The highest median pay was in Colorado ($48,740), Minnesota ($47,540) and Maine ($45,810). The lowest was in Florida ($27,470), Idaho ($27,680) and Mississippi ($30,140). The most jobs were in California (5,750), Washington (2,830) and Texas (1,950). The occupation is most concentrated in Arkansas, where its share of all jobs is 7.8 times the national share.
