Printing Press Operators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Printing Press Operators in the United States earned a median wage of $45,780 a year ($22.01 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up and operate digital, letterpress, lithographic, flexographic, gravure, or other printing machines. Includes short-run offset printing presses.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-5112.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Printing Press Operators is $45,780, which is 10% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 65 by median pay. With 144,260 jobs, it makes up about 9.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Printing Press Operators pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($116,630), Massachusetts ($53,050) and New Jersey ($50,430). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($26,100), Guam ($32,240) and Alabama ($36,670). The most jobs were in California (12,320), Illinois (9,550) and Texas (9,110). The occupation is most concentrated in Wisconsin, where its share of all jobs is 3.0 times the national share.
