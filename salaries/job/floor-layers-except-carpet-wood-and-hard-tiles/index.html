Floor Layers, Except Carpet, Wood, and Hard Tiles: pay in the United States, May 2025

Floor Layers, Except Carpet, Wood, and Hard Tiles in the United States earned a median wage of $56,460 a year ($27.15 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Floor Layers, Except Carpet, Wood, and Hard Tiles is $56,460, which is 11% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 34 by median pay. With 23,640 jobs, it makes up about 1.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Floor Layers, Except Carpet, Wood, and Hard Tiles pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($92,860), Massachusetts ($79,280) and Hawaii ($77,280). The lowest was in Montana ($37,710), Oklahoma ($38,030) and Alabama ($38,730). The most jobs were in California (4,330), Florida (1,660) and Texas (1,480). The occupation is most concentrated in South Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 2.6 times the national share.
