The median for Electrical, Electronic, and Electromechanical Assemblers, Except Coil Winders, Tapers, and Finishers is $45,850, which is 10% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 64 by median pay. With 246,970 jobs, it makes up about 16 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Electrical, Electronic, and Electromechanical Assemblers, Except Coil Winders, Tapers, and Finishers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($52,480), New Hampshire ($49,020) and Vermont ($48,830). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($24,040), Nevada ($37,110) and New Mexico ($37,210). The most jobs were in California (37,660), Texas (15,440) and Illinois (13,550). The occupation is most concentrated in New Hampshire, where its share of all jobs is 2.7 times the national share.
