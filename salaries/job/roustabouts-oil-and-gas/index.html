Roustabouts, Oil and Gas: pay in the United States, May 2025

Roustabouts, Oil and Gas in the United States earned a median wage of $46,960 a year ($22.58 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Roustabouts, Oil and Gas is $46,960, which is 8% lower. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 54 by median pay. With 44,970 jobs, it makes up about 2.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Roustabouts, Oil and Gas pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($60,810), North Dakota ($60,480) and Montana ($58,800). The lowest was in Louisiana ($44,850), Arkansas ($44,960) and Texas ($45,950). The most jobs were in Texas (24,430), Louisiana (3,460) and Oklahoma (2,730). The occupation is most concentrated in North Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 19.8 times the national share.
