What the occupation covers

Interview persons by telephone, mail, in person, or by other means for the purpose of completing forms, applications, or questionnaires. Ask specific questions, record answers, and assist persons with completing form. May sort, classify, and file forms.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 43-4111.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Interviewers, Except Eligibility and Loan is $45,920, which is 10% lower. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 37 by median pay. With 148,060 jobs, it makes up about 9.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Interviewers, Except Eligibility and Loan pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($56,880), New York ($55,410) and Washington ($51,090). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,810), Alabama ($34,180) and Mississippi ($35,110). The most jobs were in California (22,250), Texas (13,930) and New York (9,260). The occupation is most concentrated in West Virginia, where its share of all jobs is 3.7 times the national share.
