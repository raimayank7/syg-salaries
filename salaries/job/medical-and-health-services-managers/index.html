Medical and Health Services Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Medical and Health Services Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $123,860 a year ($59.55 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Medical and Health Services Managers is $123,860, which is 143% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 17 by median pay. With 597,080 jobs, it makes up about 38 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($140,970) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Medical and Health Services Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($164,120), District of Columbia ($155,140) and Hawaii ($147,630). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($82,580), Arkansas ($94,340) and Virgin Islands ($96,950). The most jobs were in California (75,090), Texas (58,420) and Florida (42,700). The occupation is most concentrated in Rhode Island, where its share of all jobs is 1.8 times the national share.
