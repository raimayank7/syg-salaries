Industrial Production Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Industrial Production Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $126,060 a year ($60.61 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Industrial Production Managers is $126,060, which is 147% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 15 by median pay. With 246,250 jobs, it makes up about 16 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Industrial Production Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Delaware ($153,000), Wyoming ($151,490) and Massachusetts ($148,050). The lowest was in Guam ($100,470), Iowa ($106,510) and Nebraska ($107,200). The most jobs were in California (25,130), Texas (21,200) and Michigan (14,320). The occupation is most concentrated in Michigan, where its share of all jobs is 2.0 times the national share.
