Airfield Operations Specialists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Airfield Operations Specialists in the United States earned a median wage of $56,850 a year ($27.33 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Ensure the safe takeoff and landing of commercial and military aircraft. Duties include coordination between air-traffic control and maintenance personnel, dispatching, using airfield landing and navigational aids, implementing airfield safety procedures, monitoring and maintaining flight records, and applying knowledge of weather information.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 53-2022.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Airfield Operations Specialists is $56,850, which is 12% higher. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 24 by median pay. With 15,190 jobs, it makes up about 1.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($63,610) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Airfield Operations Specialists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Kentucky ($123,290), Washington ($84,050) and Alabama ($78,590). The lowest was in West Virginia ($28,670), South Carolina ($33,590) and Montana ($33,990). The most jobs were in Florida (2,320), New York (2,190) and California (1,660). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 8.9 times the national share.
