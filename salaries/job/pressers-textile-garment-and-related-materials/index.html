Pressers, Textile, Garment, and Related Materials: pay in the United States, May 2025

Pressers, Textile, Garment, and Related Materials in the United States earned a median wage of $35,060 a year ($16.85 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Pressers, Textile, Garment, and Related Materials is $35,060, which is 31% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 104 by median pay. With 26,120 jobs, it makes up about 1.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Pressers, Textile, Garment, and Related Materials pay by state

The highest median pay was in Illinois ($40,230), California ($39,300) and New Hampshire ($38,540). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($21,980), Alabama ($26,860) and Louisiana ($27,170). The most jobs were in California (3,850), Texas (3,490) and Florida (2,130). The occupation is most concentrated in Nevada, where its share of all jobs is 3.1 times the national share.
