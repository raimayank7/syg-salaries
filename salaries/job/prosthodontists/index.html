Prosthodontists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Prosthodontists in the United States earned a median wage of $311,180 a year ($149.61 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Diagnose, treat, rehabilitate, design, and fit prostheses that maintain oral function, health, and appearance for patients with clinical conditions associated with teeth, oral and maxillofacial tissues, or the jaw.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1024.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Prosthodontists is $311,180, which is 510% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 11 by median pay. With 870 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.
