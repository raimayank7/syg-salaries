Computer and Information Research Scientists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Computer and Information Research Scientists in the United States earned a median wage of $140,300 a year ($67.45 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Conduct research into fundamental computer and information science as theorists, designers, or inventors. Develop solutions to problems in the field of computer hardware and software.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 15-1221.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Computer and Information Research Scientists is $140,300, which is 175% higher. Among the 21 occupations of the field Computer and Mathematical, it ranks number 1 by median pay. With 37,200 jobs, it makes up about 2.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Computer and Information Research Scientists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oregon ($206,220), Washington ($202,400) and New York ($181,990). The lowest was in Michigan ($82,590), Indiana ($85,460) and Georgia ($92,250). The most jobs were in California (8,440), Virginia (3,590) and Maryland (2,870). The occupation is most concentrated in Maryland, where its share of all jobs is 4.3 times the national share.
