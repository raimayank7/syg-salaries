Mathematicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Mathematicians in the United States earned a median wage of $126,710 a year ($60.92 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Conduct research in fundamental mathematics or in application of mathematical techniques to science, management, and other fields. Solve problems in various fields using mathematical methods.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 15-2021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Mathematicians is $126,710, which is 149% higher. Among the 21 occupations of the field Computer and Mathematical, it ranks number 7 by median pay. With 2,030 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Mathematicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($162,280), Virginia ($148,030) and Maryland ($131,810). The lowest was in Michigan ($65,510), Colorado ($86,020) and Indiana ($88,050). The most jobs were in Virginia (400), California (250) and Maryland (160). The occupation is most concentrated in Rhode Island, where its share of all jobs is 14.6 times the national share.
