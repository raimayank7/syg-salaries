Dishwashers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Dishwashers in the United States earned a median wage of $34,810 a year ($16.73 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Dishwashers is $34,810, which is 32% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Food Preparation and Serving Related, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 477,450 jobs, it makes up about 31 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Dishwashers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($40,080), District of Columbia ($38,670) and California ($37,870). The lowest was in Guam ($21,030), Puerto Rico ($23,020) and Mississippi ($26,300). The most jobs were in California (74,160), Florida (40,480) and Texas (33,630). The occupation is most concentrated in Nevada, where its share of all jobs is 2.4 times the national share.
