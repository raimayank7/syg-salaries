Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters: pay in the United States, May 2025

Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters in the United States earned a median wage of $63,800 a year ($30.67 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Assemble, install, alter, and repair pipelines or pipe systems that carry water, steam, air, or other liquids or gases. May install heating and cooling equipment and mechanical control systems. Includes sprinkler fitters.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 47-2152.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters is $63,800, which is 25% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 13 by median pay. With 465,840 jobs, it makes up about 30 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($72,170) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($101,020), Illinois ($99,950) and Oregon ($97,050). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($31,620), Guam ($36,470) and Arkansas ($48,660). The most jobs were in California (47,660), Texas (44,090) and Florida (29,260). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 1.6 times the national share.
