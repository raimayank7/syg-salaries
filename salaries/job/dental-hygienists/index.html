Dental Hygienists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Dental Hygienists in the United States earned a median wage of $98,100 a year ($47.16 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Administer oral hygiene care to patients. Assess patient oral hygiene problems or needs and maintain health records. Advise patients on oral health maintenance and disease prevention. May provide advanced care such as providing fluoride treatment or administering topical anesthesia.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1292.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Dental Hygienists is $98,100, which is 92% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 38 by median pay. With 222,740 jobs, it makes up about 14 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Dental Hygienists pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($137,220), Alaska ($132,690) and Washington ($131,160). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($59,320), Alabama ($61,160) and Guam ($62,970). The most jobs were in California (24,500), Texas (16,380) and New York (13,660). The occupation is most concentrated in Alaska, where its share of all jobs is 1.7 times the national share.
