Family Medicine Physicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Family Medicine Physicians in the United States earned a median wage of $244,180 a year ($117.39 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Diagnose, treat, and provide preventive care to individuals and families across the lifespan. May refer patients to specialists when needed for further diagnosis or treatment. Excludes “General Internal Medicine Physicians” (29-1216) and “Pediatricians, General” (29-1221).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1215.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Family Medicine Physicians is $244,180, which is 379% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 19 by median pay. With 107,510 jobs, it makes up about 6.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Family Medicine Physicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Rhode Island ($467,160), Oklahoma ($361,350) and Idaho ($347,160). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($112,990), Alabama ($134,550) and Connecticut ($163,930). The most jobs were in Texas (6,190), Pennsylvania (4,190) and New York (4,000). The occupation is most concentrated in Alaska, where its share of all jobs is 3.4 times the national share.
