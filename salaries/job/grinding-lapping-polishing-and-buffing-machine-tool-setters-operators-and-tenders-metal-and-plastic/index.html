Grinding, Lapping, Polishing, and Buffing Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Grinding, Lapping, Polishing, and Buffing Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $46,550 a year ($22.38 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. What the occupation covers

Set up, operate, or tend grinding and related tools that remove excess material or burrs from surfaces, sharpen edges or corners, or buff, hone, or polish metal or plastic work pieces.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-4033.

The median for Grinding, Lapping, Polishing, and Buffing Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic is $46,550, which is 9% lower. Grinding, Lapping, Polishing, and Buffing Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oregon ($57,290), Minnesota ($51,780) and Massachusetts ($50,910). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($31,400), New Mexico ($38,020) and Alabama ($38,170). The most jobs were in California (7,800), Ohio (5,960) and Michigan (4,180). The occupation is most concentrated in Indiana, where its share of all jobs is 3.0 times the national share.
