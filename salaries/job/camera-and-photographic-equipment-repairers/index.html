Camera and Photographic Equipment Repairers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Camera and Photographic Equipment Repairers in the United States earned a median wage of $52,720 a year ($25.35 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Camera and Photographic Equipment Repairers is $52,720, which is 3% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 33 by median pay. With 1,650 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Camera and Photographic Equipment Repairers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Ohio ($85,070), Virginia ($65,170) and New Jersey ($60,290). The lowest was in Texas ($33,940), North Carolina ($41,140) and Florida ($41,290). The most jobs were in Texas (90), Ohio (80) and Georgia (60). The occupation is most concentrated in Ohio, where its share of all jobs is 1.4 times the national share.
