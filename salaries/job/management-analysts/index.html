Management Analysts: pay in the United States, May 2025

Management Analysts in the United States earned a median wage of $101,860 a year ($48.97 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Conduct organizational studies and evaluations, design systems and procedures, conduct work simplification and measurement studies, and prepare operations and procedures manuals to assist management in operating more efficiently and effectively. Includes program analysts and management consultants. Excludes “Computer Systems Analysts” (15-1211) and “Operations Research Analysts” (15-2031).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 13-1111.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Management Analysts is $101,860, which is 100% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 5 by median pay. With 898,280 jobs, it makes up about 58 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($113,790) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Management Analysts pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($134,720), District of Columbia ($126,140) and Maryland ($121,680). The lowest was in Mississippi ($69,390), Arkansas ($72,950) and Nebraska ($75,360). The most jobs were in California (137,280), Florida (71,060) and New York (69,150). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 4.7 times the national share.
