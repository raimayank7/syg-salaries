Extruding, Forming, Pressing, and Compacting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders: pay in the United States, May 2025

Extruding, Forming, Pressing, and Compacting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders in the United States earned a median wage of $45,760 a year ($22.00 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up, operate, or tend machines, such as glass-forming machines, plodder machines, and tuber machines, to shape and form products such as glassware, food, rubber, soap, brick, tile, clay, wax, tobacco, or cosmetics. Excludes “Shoe Machine Operators and Tenders” (51-6042) and “Paper Goods Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders” (51-9196).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-9041.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Extruding, Forming, Pressing, and Compacting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders is $45,760, which is 10% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 66 by median pay. With 58,770 jobs, it makes up about 3.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Extruding, Forming, Pressing, and Compacting Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders pay by state

The highest median pay was in Maine ($57,990), Pennsylvania ($53,580) and Vermont ($52,340). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($23,330), Kentucky ($36,180) and New Mexico ($36,710). The most jobs were in California (4,210), Tennessee (4,210) and Ohio (3,970). The occupation is most concentrated in Arkansas, where its share of all jobs is 4.4 times the national share.
