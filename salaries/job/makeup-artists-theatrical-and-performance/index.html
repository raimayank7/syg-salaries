Makeup Artists, Theatrical and Performance: pay in the United States, May 2025

Makeup Artists, Theatrical and Performance in the United States earned a median wage of $97,150 a year ($46.71 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Makeup Artists, Theatrical and Performance is $97,150, which is 91% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Personal Care and Service, it ranks number 1 by median pay. With 2,340 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Makeup Artists, Theatrical and Performance pay by state

The highest median pay was in Georgia ($143,230), California ($140,870) and Louisiana ($129,580). The lowest was in Florida ($82,400), District of Columbia ($95,730) and New York ($97,150). The most jobs were in California (830), Florida (250) and Georgia (200). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 5.9 times the national share.
