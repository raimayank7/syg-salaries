Training and Development Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Training and Development Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $133,000 a year ($63.94 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Training and Development Managers is $133,000, which is 161% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 14 by median pay. With 48,050 jobs, it makes up about 3.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($148,370) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Training and Development Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Delaware ($172,340), New York ($171,400) and Washington ($163,360). The lowest was in Mississippi ($88,030), Puerto Rico ($94,310) and Montana ($98,950). The most jobs were in California (6,680), Texas (6,190) and Florida (3,550). The occupation is most concentrated in Maryland, where its share of all jobs is 1.5 times the national share.
