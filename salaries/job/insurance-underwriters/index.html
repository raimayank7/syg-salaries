Insurance Underwriters: pay in the United States, May 2025

Insurance Underwriters in the United States earned a median wage of $81,370 a year ($39.12 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Insurance Underwriters is $81,370, which is 60% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 14 by median pay. With 105,420 jobs, it makes up about 6.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($93,700) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Insurance Underwriters pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($106,640), New Hampshire ($103,940) and Nevada ($101,440). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($46,990), Mississippi ($59,250) and Alabama ($62,220). The most jobs were in Texas (9,980), Florida (7,300) and Georgia (7,160). The occupation is most concentrated in Georgia, where its share of all jobs is 2.2 times the national share.
