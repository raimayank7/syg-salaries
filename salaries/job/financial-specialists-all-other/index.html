Financial Specialists, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Financial Specialists, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $81,100 a year ($38.99 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Financial Specialists, All Other is $81,100, which is 59% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 15 by median pay. With 132,130 jobs, it makes up about 8.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($93,810) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Financial Specialists, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($125,110), Maine ($109,060) and New York ($107,490). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($39,270), Kansas ($51,160) and Louisiana ($59,860). The most jobs were in California (14,290), Texas (13,550) and New York (9,750). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 4.1 times the national share.
