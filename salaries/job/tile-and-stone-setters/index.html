Tile and Stone Setters: pay in the United States, May 2025

Tile and Stone Setters in the United States earned a median wage of $55,690 a year ($26.77 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Tile and Stone Setters is $55,690, which is 9% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 35 by median pay. With 35,850 jobs, it makes up about 2.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Tile and Stone Setters pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($81,150), Hawaii ($79,270) and Washington ($77,920). The lowest was in Arkansas ($38,980), Mississippi ($39,130) and North Dakota ($42,620). The most jobs were in California (8,250), Florida (4,070) and New York (2,990). The occupation is most concentrated in Nevada, where its share of all jobs is 4.1 times the national share.
