Logisticians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Logisticians in the United States earned a median wage of $82,320 a year ($39.58 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Analyze and coordinate the ongoing logistical functions of a firm or organization. Responsible for the entire life cycle of a product, including acquisition, distribution, internal allocation, delivery, and final disposal of resources. Excludes “Transportation, Storage, and Distribution Managers” (11-3071) and “Project Management Specialists” (13-1082).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 13-1081.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Logisticians is $82,320, which is 61% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 13 by median pay. With 251,040 jobs, it makes up about 16 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Logisticians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($107,250), District of Columbia ($104,770) and Maryland ($102,700). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($54,380), Nebraska ($65,370) and Guam ($65,440). The most jobs were in California (32,940), Texas (21,030) and Florida (15,020). The occupation is most concentrated in Georgia, where its share of all jobs is 1.6 times the national share.
