Directors, Religious Activities and Education: pay in the United States, May 2025

Directors, Religious Activities and Education in the United States earned a median wage of $52,100 a year ($25.05 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Coordinate or design programs and conduct outreach to promote the religious education or activities of a denominational group. May provide counseling, guidance, and leadership relative to marital, health, financial, and religious problems.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 21-2021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Directors, Religious Activities and Education is $52,100, which is 2% higher. Among the 17 occupations of the field Community and Social Service, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 22,160 jobs, it makes up about 1.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($62,100) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Directors, Religious Activities and Education pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alabama ($79,310), Texas ($75,420) and Georgia ($72,910). The lowest was in Louisiana ($29,540), Arkansas ($33,920) and South Carolina ($36,120). The most jobs were in New York (3,550), California (3,200) and Oregon (1,860). The occupation is most concentrated in Montana, where its share of all jobs is 8.8 times the national share.
