Rock Splitters, Quarry: pay in the United States, May 2025

Rock Splitters, Quarry in the United States earned a median wage of $48,740 a year ($23.44 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Separate blocks of rough dimension stone from quarry mass using jackhammers, wedges, or chop saws.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 47-5051.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Rock Splitters, Quarry is $48,740, which is 4% lower. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 49 by median pay. With 3,320 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Rock Splitters, Quarry pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($66,000), Vermont ($65,420) and Wisconsin ($62,000). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,810), Idaho ($35,880) and Georgia ($42,320). The most jobs were in Kentucky (310), Tennessee (300) and California (290). The occupation is most concentrated in Montana, where its share of all jobs is 8.7 times the national share.
