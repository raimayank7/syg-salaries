Cooling and Freezing Equipment Operators and Tenders: pay in the United States, May 2025

Cooling and Freezing Equipment Operators and Tenders in the United States earned a median wage of $41,330 a year ($19.87 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Operate or tend equipment such as cooling and freezing units, refrigerators, batch freezers, and freezing tunnels, to cool or freeze products, food, blood plasma, and chemicals.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-9193.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Cooling and Freezing Equipment Operators and Tenders is $41,330, which is 19% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 84 by median pay. With 6,900 jobs, it makes up about 0.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Cooling and Freezing Equipment Operators and Tenders pay by state

The highest median pay was in Nebraska ($68,170), Iowa ($62,520) and Indiana ($55,730). The lowest was in Michigan ($31,700), Alabama ($35,280) and Virginia ($35,370). The most jobs were in Pennsylvania (760), Georgia (540) and Texas (510). The occupation is most concentrated in Mississippi, where its share of all jobs is 7.0 times the national share.
