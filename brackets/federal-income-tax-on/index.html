These are the amounts of tax on taxable income, which is the income left after the standard deduction or itemized deductions. To start from your pay instead, use the calculator and keep the standard deduction ticked.

Marginal and average rates

The tax grows faster than income only because the rate steps up from bracket to bracket. For a single filer in 2026, $100,000 of taxable income is taxed at $16,712 overall, which is 16.7% on average, while the marginal rate on the next dollar is 22%. At $300,000 the average rate is 24.6% and the marginal rate 35%. The marginal rate is the one that matters for the tax on extra income; the average rate is what you pay on the whole.
