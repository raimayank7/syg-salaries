Federal income tax on $100,000 of taxable income

On taxable income of $100,000 a single filer owes $16,712 in 2026 (16.7% of it) and $16,542 in 2027, before credits. The marginal rate, the rate on the next dollar, is 22% in 2026.

What this tells you

  • Taxable income is after the standard deduction (single 2026: $16,100). A gross income of $116,100 becomes $100,000 of taxable income for a single filer.
  • The 2027 tax is $170 less than 2026 for a single filer, because the bracket limits were raised for inflation.
  • This is income tax only; credits, Social Security, Medicare and state tax are separate.

Common questions

How much federal income tax on $100,000 of taxable income?

For a single filer in 2026: $16,712; for married filing jointly: $11,504 (before credits).
