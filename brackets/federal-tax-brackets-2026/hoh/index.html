Head of household tax brackets, tax year 2026

These are the federal income tax brackets for Head of household (Heads of households) in tax year 2026. The standard deduction is $24,150. The top rate of 37% applies to taxable income over $640,600.

Taxable incomeRate on that partTax on the amount below it
Up to $17,70010%$0
$17,701 to $67,45012%$1,770
$67,451 to $105,70022%$7,740
$105,701 to $201,75024%$16,155
$201,751 to $256,20032%$39,207
$256,201 to $640,60035%$56,631
Over $640,60037%$191,171

An example

With an income of $75,000 and the standard deduction of $24,150, taxable income is $50,850. The tax is $5,748, which is 7.7% of the income and the marginal rate is 12%.

Other 2026 numbers

  • Maximum 0% capital gains rate amount: $66,200; maximum 15% rate amount: $579,600 (above that the rate is 20%).
  • AMT exemption: $90,100 (the figure for unmarried individuals).
  • Additional standard deduction for age 65 or older or blind: $1,650 ($2,050 if you are unmarried and not a surviving spouse).

Common questions

What is the 2026 standard deduction for head of household?

$24,150 (IRS).

What is the top rate?

37% on taxable income over $640,600.
