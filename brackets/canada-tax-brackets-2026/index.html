How to read these tables

  • Each rate applies only to the slice of taxable income inside its bracket.
  • The rates are before credits. The basic personal amount and other non-refundable credits reduce the tax, and are not in the sources read for this site.
  • See CPP and EI for 2026 and the calculator.

Common questions

What are the 2026 federal tax rates in Canada?

14% on the first $58,523 of taxable income, 20.5% to $117,045, 26% to $181,440, 29% to $258,482 and 33% above (CRA).

Does Quebec use these tables?

No. The CRA page lists the other provinces and territories; Quebec has its own provincial income tax, which is not shown here.
