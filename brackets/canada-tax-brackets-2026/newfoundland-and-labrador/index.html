Newfoundland and Labrador income tax brackets, 2026

These are the Newfoundland and Labrador provincial/territorial income tax rates for income earned in 2026, from the Canada Revenue Agency. They are added to federal tax. The lowest rate is 8.7% and the highest is 21.8%.

Good to know

  • The amounts are the tax on the brackets alone. The basic personal amount and other non-refundable credits lower the tax, and are not in the sources read for this site.
  • The province that counts is the one you lived in on 31 December 2026 (CRA).
  • Some provinces and territories add surtaxes or premiums, which are not shown here.
