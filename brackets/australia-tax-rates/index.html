Common questions

What are the Australian resident tax rates for 2026-27?

Nil up to $18,200; 15c for each $1 over $18,200 to $45,000; $4,020 plus 30c over $45,000 to $135,000; $31,020 plus 37c over $135,000 to $190,000; $51,370 plus 45c over $190,000 (ATO).

What is the Medicare levy?

An amount paid in addition to tax: 2% of taxable income (ATO), with reductions and exemptions depending on circumstances.
