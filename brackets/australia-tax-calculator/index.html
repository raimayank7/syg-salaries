Tax Brackets

Australia tax calculator, 2026-27

Resident tax and the 2% Medicare levy on a taxable income. Offsets, HELP and the levy surcharge are not included.

How it works

Income tax is worked out on the ATO resident brackets: nil on the first $18,200, then 15c (16c in 2025-26) for each dollar to $45,000, 30c to $135,000, 37c to $190,000 and 45c above. The Medicare levy is 2% of taxable income. The result is the tax and levy for a resident for the full year with the full tax-free threshold. It does not subtract offsets (for example the low income tax offset), HELP repayments or the Medicare levy reduction, and it does not add the Medicare levy surcharge, so a low income may really pay less and a high income without private hospital cover may pay more. Super is paid by the employer on top of the salary and is not part of this result.

The rate tables have the brackets.

Sources

  • Australian Taxation Office (ato.gov.au), "Tax rates - Australian resident" (page last updated 13 August 2026) and "Medicare levy" (last updated 30 April 2026), saved and read on 13 November 2026. The tables were read from the printed page.
  • Each Australian table was checked: the "plus" amount of every bracket equals the tax on the brackets below it.

Figures from the ATO, read on 13 November 2026, for Australian residents with the full tax-free threshold. Offsets, HELP, Medicare levy reduction and surcharge are not included. Not tax advice.
