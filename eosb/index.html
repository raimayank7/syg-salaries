The rule in four lines

  • At least 1 year of continuous service is needed.
  • 21 days of basic salary for each of the first 5 years, then 30 days for each year after that; part years count in proportion.
  • The total can never exceed 2 years of wages.
  • Only the last basic salary counts, not allowances.

Common questions

How is gratuity calculated in the UAE?

For an expatriate worker with at least 1 year of continuous service: 21 days of basic salary for each of the first 5 years and 30 days for each year after that, in proportion for part years, and never more than 2 years of wages (u.ae, Article 51 of Decree-Law 33 of 2021).

Is gratuity paid on the full salary?

No. The basis is the last basic salary; housing, transport, utilities and furniture allowances are not included.

When must the employer pay it?

Within 14 days of the end of the contract, together with other dues. The employer may deduct amounts the worker owes.

Do I get gratuity if I worked less than one year?

No. One year of continuous service is the minimum, and days of unpaid absence do not count towards it.
