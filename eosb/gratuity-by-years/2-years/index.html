UAE gratuity after 2 years of service

An expatriate worker in the UAE private sector who completes 2 years with the same employer is entitled to 42 days of basic salary as end-of-service gratuity, which is 1.40 months of basic salary.

How the 42 days are made up

The first 2 years each earn 21 days, which is 42 days.

One more year

With 3 years of service the gratuity would be 63 days; on a basic salary of AED 5,000 that is AED 10,500, which is AED 3,500 more than now.

Points to remember

  • Only the last basic salary counts, not housing, transport or other allowances.
  • Unpaid absence days are taken off the service count.
  • The employer must pay within 14 days of the end of the contract, and may deduct amounts the worker owes.

Common questions

How much gratuity after 2 years in the UAE?

42 days of basic salary, which is 1.40 months. On a basic salary of AED 5,000 that is AED 7,000.

Does the gratuity include allowances?

No: only the last basic salary counts (u.ae).
