How to use this table

Find the row nearest to your last basic salary, not your total pay: housing, transport and similar allowances do not count. Each row uses the rule from u.ae: 21 days per year for the first 5 years and 30 days after, a day being basic salary ÷ 30. Tap a salary for the full year-by-year table.

What the numbers show

  • Doubling the basic salary doubles the gratuity, because every figure is a number of days of the basic salary.
  • The step from 5 to 10 years is larger than from 0 to 5 years in each row, because years after the fifth earn 30 days instead of 21.
  • A person whose package has a low basic salary and large allowances gets a smaller gratuity than the package suggests. Check the basic salary in the contract.
  • Any figure here is an estimate for a full-time expatriate worker with continuous service and no unpaid absence.

Sources

  • UAE Government portal u.ae, "End of service benefits for workers in the private sector" (u.ae), read on 13 November 2026. It quotes Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 and Article 30 of Cabinet Resolution No. 1 of 2022. Everything about the 21 and 30 days, the two-year cap, the basic salary, the 14-day payment deadline, part-time workers and the Savings Scheme comes from that page.
  • The only assumption: a "day" of salary is the monthly basic salary divided by 30. The page does not state the divisor; check how your employer or MOHRE counts it.

Rules read on 13 November 2026 from the UAE Government portal. They apply to expatriate workers in the private sector; Emiratis follow the pension and social security legislation. Laws change and your contract may give more.
