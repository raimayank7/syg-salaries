UAE gratuity on a basic salary of AED 4,000

If your last basic salary is AED 4,000 a month, one day of salary is AED 133. The gratuity after 5 years is AED 14,000 (105 days), after 10 years AED 34,000 (255 days) and after 20 years AED 74,000 (555 days).

What to check

  • The figure is based on the last basic salary on your contract, not the total package. If your contract shows a basic of AED 4,000 and allowances on top, only the basic counts.
  • If your basic salary rose during your service, the gratuity uses the last one for all the years.
  • Unpaid absence days are taken off the service count, and gratuity is paid in proportion for part years once the first year is complete.
  • The most you can receive is 24 months of basic salary, which for this salary is AED 96,000.

Common questions

What is the gratuity on a basic salary of AED 4,000?

After 5 years AED 14,000; after 10 years AED 34,000; after 20 years AED 74,000.

Does a higher total salary change it?

No. Only the basic part counts, so the allowances in a AED 8,000 package do not add to the gratuity.
