EPF Wage Ceiling

PF balance projection

An estimate of the PF account balance when the same wage keeps being contributed. The rate filled in, 8.25%, was the interest rate of FY 2025-26 (EPFO FAQ Q47); change it, because the rate for later years is decided every year.

What is in the estimate

  • Each month the employee share (12%) and the employer EPF share (3.67%) go into the PF account; the employer's 8.33% EPS share goes to the pension fund and is not in the balance.
  • Interest is worked out each month on the running balance and added once a year, a simplification of EPFO's method (the FAQ says "compounded at monthly running balance").
  • The wage is assumed to stay the same and the rate is assumed constant, so the result is only a guide.

Sources

  • Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), "Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs): Revision of EPFO Statutory Wage Ceiling from ₹15,000 per month to ₹25,000 per month" (13 pages; notification S.O. 5109(E) dated 17 September 2026, effective 17 September 2026), saved from the EPFO website and read on 13 November 2026. Question numbers (Q1 to Q48) are quoted.
  • The wage ceiling revision is also reported in Press Information Bureau releases (PRID 2313829 and 2314111), read on 13 November 2026.
  • Not read: the text of the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, the EPS and EDLI schemes, and any EPFO circular issued after the FAQs.
