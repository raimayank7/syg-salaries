EPF Wage Ceiling

Who is covered by the ₹25,000 wage ceiling?

The wage ceiling is the largest monthly wage counted for mandatory coverage under EPF, EPS and EDLI. It rose from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 from 17 September 2026 (FAQ Q1, Q2). These are the answers EPFO gives on who is covered.

Questions and answers

Who benefits from the higher ceiling? (Q6, Q19, Q20)

Employees with wages above ₹15,000 and up to ₹25,000 a month, who meet the other statutory conditions, come within mandatory coverage; EPFO estimates more than 51 lakh additional employees. An employee joining at ₹20,000 is now enrolled in EPF and EPS.

Is my gross salary compared with ₹25,000? (Q24 to Q27)

No. The test is PF wages as defined in section 2(88) of the Code on Social Security, 2020, not gross salary. With a gross salary of ₹50,000 but EPF wages of ₹25,000 you are covered under all three schemes (Q27); with EPF wages of ₹30,000 you are not required to be covered but may opt in with your employer's consent (Q26).

What about employees who were excluded on 17 September 2026? (Q28, Q29)

Those with EPF wages below ₹25,000 are to be made members of EPF, EPS and EDLI from 17 September 2026, and the employer contributes from that date.

Do I have to apply? (Q30, Q31)

No. Enrolling the members and starting compliance is the employer's statutory responsibility, and EPS membership is automatic for those newly brought in.

Must everyone contribute on ₹25,000? (Q21, Q22)

No. Contributions follow the wages; where wages exceed ₹25,000 the contribution may be limited to the ceiling, and employees already contributing on higher wages need not reduce it to ₹25,000.
