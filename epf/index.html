The rules in short

  • Employee: 12% of PF wages. Employer: 12% of PF wages, of which 8.33% goes to the pension scheme (EPS) and 3.67% to the PF account (EPF).
  • The employer also pays 0.5% for EDLI insurance and 0.5% as admin charges, with a minimum admin charge of ₹500 a month for an establishment with a contributing member.
  • Wages above ₹25,000: the contribution is on ₹25,000 unless the employee already contributes on higher wages (Q21, Q22), and EPS membership is open only to employees whose wages did not exceed the ceiling when they joined or when the new ceiling started (Q13).
  • For the month of September 2026 the two ceilings apply to two parts of the month: see September 2026.

Common questions

What is the EPF wage ceiling now?

₹25,000 a month, raised from ₹15,000 with effect from 17 September 2026 (notification S.O. 5109(E), EPFO FAQs Q1 and Q2).

How much PF is deducted on a wage of ₹20,000?

Employee 12% = ₹2,400. The employer also pays 12%, of which 8.33% (₹1,666) goes to EPS and 3.67% (₹734) to EPF, plus 0.5% EDLI and 0.5% admin charges (EPFO FAQs Q12 and Q13).

Does the PF apply to my whole gross salary?

No. PF is worked out on PF wages as defined in the Code on Social Security, 2020, not on the whole gross salary (EPFO FAQs Q24 and Q25).
