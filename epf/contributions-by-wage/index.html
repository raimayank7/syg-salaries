EPF Wage Ceiling

PF contribution by wage, from October 2026

Employee and employer contributions at ten PF wages. Five rows (₹10,000, ₹15,000, ₹20,000, ₹25,000, ₹35,000) are the rows of the EPFO FAQ table; the others use the same rules.

Reading the table

  • Up to ₹25,000 the contribution grows with the wage; above it, it stops growing for a member contributing on the ceiling.
  • At ₹35,000 there is no EPS share in the table because EPS membership is for employees whose wages were within the ceiling at the relevant date, so the whole 12% of ₹25,000 (₹3,000) goes to EPF.
  • Amounts are rounded to whole rupees as in the EPFO table.
