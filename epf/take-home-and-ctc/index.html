EPF Wage Ceiling

Take-home pay, CTC and the new ceiling

Many employees ask whether the higher ceiling reduces their pay. EPFO's FAQs give a direct answer and some figures.

For how a CTC turns into in-hand salary see the in-hand salary calculator of this family.

Questions and answers

Will take-home pay go down? (Q16, Q17)

Where PF is now required on a higher wage the employee contribution rises: at a PF wage of ₹20,000, 12% is ₹2,400 against ₹1,800 on the old ₹15,000 ceiling. EPFO says it moves money from the take-home pocket to the PF account pocket, matched by the employer.

Does the ceiling change my CTC? (Q14, Q15)

CTC is not a statutory concept for PF; contributions follow the statutory wages. The employer's contribution cannot simply be treated as an employee deduction by describing it as part of CTC.

What does the employer pay in addition? (Q32, Q39)

A matching 12% (3.67% EPF and 8.33% EPS) on wages up to ₹25,000. For a wage of ₹20,000 the employer contribution is ₹2,400 instead of ₹1,800. EPFO notes an incentive of up to ₹3,000 a month for every additional employment created under PMVBRY.

What about the first employee month in September 2026? (Q10, Q11)

The extra employee share can be recovered in the next payroll cycle for take-home purposes, but the ECR and remittance for September must be made on time.
