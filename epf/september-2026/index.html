EPF Wage Ceiling

EPF for September 2026: two ceilings in one month

The wage ceiling rose to ₹25,000 on 17 September 2026, so September is worked out in two parts: 16 days (1 to 16 September) on the old ceiling of ₹15,000 and 14 days (17 to 30 September) on the new one. EPFO's FAQ Q7 gives three scenarios for a wage of ₹20,000; the calculator below reproduces them and extends them to other wages.

How the parts are worked out

  • Period 1 (1 to 16 September) is 16/30 of the month, with a ceiling of ₹15,000; period 2 (17 to 30 September) is 14/30, with a ceiling of ₹25,000.
  • Scenario A: the employee was excluded until 16 September, so only 14/30 of the wage counts, all of it at the new rates.
  • Scenario B: an EPF and EDLI member who already paid on the full wage; the employer pays 12% to EPF on period 1 and 3.67% on period 2, and EPS starts on period 2.
  • Scenario C: a member of all three schemes on the ₹15,000 cap, whose wages for period 1 are ₹15,000 × 16/30.

Common questions

Does an employer file two returns for September 2026?

No. One ECR covers September 2026, with the contribution worked out for 1 to 16 September on the old ceiling of ₹15,000 and 17 to 30 September on the new ceiling of ₹25,000 (EPFO FAQs Q8 and Q9). The return is ordinarily due by 15 October 2026.

Can the extra employee share be taken from the October salary?

Employers may defer recovery of the employee share for newly covered employees to the next payroll for take-home pay purposes without prior approval, but the ECR and the full remittance for September must still be made on time (Q10, Q11).
