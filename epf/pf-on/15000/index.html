PF contribution on a wage of ₹15,000

With a monthly PF wage of ₹15,000, the employee contribution is ₹1,800 (12%). The employer pays ₹550 to the PF account and ₹1,250 to the pension scheme, so ₹2,350 a month goes into the employee's PF account and ₹1,250 to the pension fund. The employer also pays ₹75 for EDLI and ₹75 as admin charges. The total remitted is ₹3,750.

Before and after the ceiling change

This wage is within the old ceiling of ₹15,000, so the contribution does not change.

Notes

  • The wage is within the ceiling of ₹25,000, so contributions are on the whole wage.
  • Amounts are rounded to whole rupees, as in the EPFO table. Admin charges have a minimum of ₹500 a month per establishment.
  • This is the position from the wage month of October 2026; September 2026 is worked out in two parts (see how).

Common questions

How much PF on a wage of ₹15,000?

Employee ₹1,800; employer ₹550 to EPF and ₹1,250 to EPS; EDLI ₹75 and admin ₹75 (rules of the EPFO wage ceiling FAQs).
