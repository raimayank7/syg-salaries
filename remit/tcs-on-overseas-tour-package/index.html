TCS on an overseas tour package

Section 394(1), Table serial 8 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (read on 13 November 2026) makes the seller of an "overseas tour programme package", including expenses for travel, hotel stay, boarding or lodging or similar related expenditure, collect 5% of the amount or aggregate of amounts up to ten lakh rupees and 20% of the amount or aggregate above ten lakh rupees. There is no tax-free first slice, unlike remittances.

No double collection

Section 394(4)(a): if the seller has collected tax on the package, the bank does not collect again under the remittance rule on that amount.

Common questions

Is there a threshold for tour packages?

No. Serial 8 of the table collects 5% on the amount or aggregate up to ₹10 lakh and 20% on the part above ₹10 lakh, from the first rupee.
