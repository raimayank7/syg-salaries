TCS on foreign remittance

Section 394(1), Table serial 7 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (read on 13 November 2026) makes the authorised dealer (your bank) collect tax on a remittance under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme "of an amount or aggregate of the amounts exceeding ten lakh rupees": 5% for education or medical treatment and 20% for other purposes. The Act prints the rates in the next column without the words "on the excess"; this site applies them to the part of the year’s total above ₹10 lakh, This is this site’s reading of the table, not a sentence of the Act (serial 8 does say it for its 20% band). Check with your bank.

Common questions

What counts as the Liberalised Remittance Scheme?

The Act’s table speaks of a "remittance under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme" and does not define the scheme; the limits on how much an individual may remit come from foreign exchange rules, which were not read for this site.
