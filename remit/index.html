Tax on money sent abroad from India

Work out the tax collected at source on a Liberalised Remittance Scheme payment or an overseas tour package under section 394 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, read on 13 November 2026. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Worked examples

  • Sending ₹15 lakh abroad in a year for travel or a gift after nothing earlier: the part above ₹10 lakh is ₹5 lakh, and the tax at 20% is ₹1,00,000.
  • Sending ₹15 lakh for education: the same ₹5 lakh at 5% is ₹25,000.
  • Buying a ₹12 lakh overseas tour package: 5% of ₹10 lakh and 20% of ₹2 lakh, ₹90,000.

Common questions

How much tax is collected when I send money abroad?

Under section 394 of the Income-tax Act, 2025, the authorised dealer collects 5% of the part above ₹10 lakh in the year if the purpose is education or medical treatment and 20% for any other purpose, as this site reads the table. An education loan from a financial institution is exempt.

Is the tax collected on the first ₹10 lakh?

No collection applies to remittances up to ₹10 lakh in the year under serial 7. For an overseas tour package, serial 8 collects 5% on amounts up to ₹10 lakh and 20% above.

Can the rate be lower?

Section 395(3) lets the buyer apply to the Assessing Officer for a certificate for collection of tax at a lower rate, if total income justifies it.

Source

  • The Income-tax Act, 2025, as published in the Gazette of India Extraordinary: section 394 (tax collection at source, Table serials 7 and 8 and sub-sections (4) and (5)) and section 395(3) (certificate for collection at a lower rate). The enacted text was read on 13 November 2026.
