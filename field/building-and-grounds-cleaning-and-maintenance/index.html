The field as a whole

Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance holds 4,548,260 of the 155,495,730 jobs in the national figures, about 3 in every 100. Its median pay of $37,680 a year is 26% below the median for all occupations ($50,980), which places it number 19 of the 22 fields. Across the whole field, the lowest-paid tenth earned less than $29,010 and the highest-paid tenth more than $57,420; the middle half earned between $33,930 and $46,170. The average was $40,880, 8% above the median.

Inside the field

Inside the field, the best-paid occupation with a published yearly median earns 1.6 times the lowest ($58,430 against $35,510). 1 of the 10 occupations with a yearly median are above the national median of $50,980, and 9 are below it. The occupation in the middle of the field's list is Pest Control Workers, at $45,250. Jobs are unevenly spread: the three largest occupations hold 88% of the field's jobs, while the smallest, Grounds Maintenance Workers, All Other, has 13,630. Pay stretches furthest within Grounds Maintenance Workers, All Other, where the highest-paid tenth earn over $74,820 and the lowest-paid tenth under $31,650.

Reading the field

Pay also moves with place. For the field's largest occupation, Janitors and Cleaners, Except Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners, the state medians run from $21,890 in Puerto Rico to $45,580 in Massachusetts; its own page has every state. The Bureau of Labor Statistics sorts every occupation into one field by the work done, not by the industry of the employer, so an accountant at a hospital is counted under business and financial operations, not health care.
