Puducherry professional tax

Act No. 5 of 2017, the Puducherry Municipalities (Amendment) Act (Gazette of Puducherry, 17 May 2017, read on 14 November 2026), substituted Schedule-IV of the Puducherry Municipalities Act, 1973. Persons are assessed on a scale determined by the Municipal Council, subject to a maximum half-yearly tax for each class of half-yearly income. This page shows those maximums, not what a council charges.

Limits of this page

The amounts are ceilings: the scale each municipal council sets was not found and can be lower. Who is liable, how it is collected and any amendment after May 2017 were not read. Check your municipality’s notice.

Common questions

What is the profession tax in Puducherry?

Schedule-IV of the Puducherry Municipalities Act, 1973 as substituted in 2017 sets a maximum half-yearly tax by half-yearly income: nil up to ₹99,999; ₹250 for ₹1,00,000 to ₹2,00,000; ₹500 up to ₹3,00,000; ₹750 up to ₹4,00,000; ₹1,000 up to ₹5,00,000; ₹1,250 above ₹5,00,000.

Are these the rates?

They are ceilings. The Act says persons are assessed on a scale determined by the Municipal Council from time to time, subject to the maximum for each class.

From when?

The Amendment Act, assented to on 9 May 2017, came into force on its publication in the Gazette on 17 May 2017.

Source

  • The Gazette of Puducherry, Part II Extraordinary, No. 28, 17 May 2017: the Puducherry Municipalities (Amendment) Act, 2017 (Act No. 5 of 2017), section 2 (Schedule-IV, Profession Tax), read on 14 November 2026.
