Tripura professional tax on salary

The Tripura Finance Department (Taxes & Excise) notification of 25 July 2018 (Tripura Gazette Extraordinary No. 443; corrigendum in Gazette No. 1031 of 30 October 2018; read on 14 November 2026) set the tax on salary and wage earners by monthly gross income: nil up to ₹7,500; ₹150 a month (₹1,800 a year) from ₹7,501 to ₹15,000; ₹208 a month (₹2,496 a year) above ₹15,000. It took effect on the date of publication.

Limits of this page

Only entry 1 (salary and wage earners) is worked. The gazette pages are scans that were read by eye. Professionals, dealers, traders and others pay under other entries. Later changes were not looked for.

Common questions

How much professional tax does a salaried person pay in Tripura?

The notification of 25 July 2018: nothing up to ₹7,500 a month; ₹150 a month (₹1,800 a year) above ₹7,500 and up to ₹15,000; ₹208 a month (₹2,496 a year) above ₹15,000.

What did the October 2018 corrigendum change?

It made the middle band start at ₹7,501 (the first printing said ₹7,500 and above, which overlapped the nil band).

From when does it apply?

From the date the notification was published in the Gazette, 25 July 2018. Later changes were not looked for.
