Telangana professional tax on salary

The Telangana Commercial Taxes Department’s “PT Schedule” page (read on 14 November 2026) shows the First Schedule of the Telangana Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Act, 1987 “as amended upto date”. For salary and wage earners by monthly pay: nil up to ₹15,000; ₹150 a month from ₹15,001 to ₹20,000; ₹200 a month above ₹20,000 (₹2,400 a year).

Limits of this page

This is the department’s consolidated web page, not the amending gazette or the text of the Act, and it carries no date or amendment number. Only entry 1 (salary and wages) is worked; professionals, businesses and the other entries of the schedule are not. Check your employer’s notice.

Common questions

How much professional tax does a salaried person pay in Telangana?

The First Schedule shown by the Commercial Taxes Department: nothing up to ₹15,000 a month; ₹150 a month from ₹15,001 to ₹20,000; ₹200 a month above ₹20,000, which is ₹2,400 a year.

Is this from the gazette?

No. It is the department’s own consolidated page of the First Schedule “as amended upto date”, not the amending notification or the Act text, and it gives no date.

Is there a different amount in February?

The page shows no separate February rate, so none is applied.
