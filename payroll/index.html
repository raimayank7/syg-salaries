Gratuity calculator, Code on Social Security 2020

Fifteen days’ wages for each completed year, from section 53 of the Code on Social Security, 2020, read on 13 November 2026. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Worked example

Wages of ₹50,000 a month and 10 years 7 months of service count as 11 years (part of a year beyond six months counts as a year). Fifteen days’ wages are ₹50,000 ÷ 26 × 15 = ₹28,846, and for 11 years the gratuity is ₹3,17,308.

Common questions

What is the gratuity formula?

Section 53(2) of the Code on Social Security, 2020 pays fifteen days’ wages for every completed year of service or part of a year in excess of six months, on the wages last drawn. For a monthly-rated employee, Explanation 3 says fifteen days’ wages are the monthly wages divided by 26 and multiplied by 15.

How many years of service are needed?

Five years of continuous service for retirement or resignation (section 53(1)). The Code says five years is not needed on death, disablement or expiry of a fixed term, and the government says fixed-term employees qualify after one year, on a proportionate basis.

Is there a maximum?

Section 53(3) says the gratuity shall not exceed the amount notified by the Central Government. The Code itself names no figure, so this page lets you enter the notified ceiling.
