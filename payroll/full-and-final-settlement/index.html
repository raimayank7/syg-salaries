India Payroll Rules

When wages and the final settlement are due

Section 17 of the Code on Wages, 2019 sets the time limits for paying wages (read on 13 November 2026). The appropriate Government may provide another time limit under section 17(3).

The limits

Wage periodWages must be paid
DailyAt the end of the shift
WeeklyOn the last working day of the week, before the weekly holiday
FortnightlyBefore the end of the second day after the fortnight
MonthlyBefore the expiry of the seventh day of the next month
Removal, dismissal, retrenchment, resignation, closureWithin two working days

Example

An employee who resigns on Friday 13 November 2026, with Sunday as the only weekly off, is owed the wages by Monday 16 November 2026.

What is not in these Codes

Leave encashment and notice pay are not set by the Code on Wages or the Code on Social Security texts read for this site: they come from the contract, the establishment’s policy or other law, so no calculator is offered. Gratuity is a separate payment under the gratuity rules.

Common questions

When must the final wages be paid?

Section 17(2) of the Code on Wages: within two working days of removal, dismissal, retrenchment or resignation, or of unemployment due to closure of the establishment.

When must monthly wages be paid?

Section 17(1)(iv): before the expiry of the seventh day of the succeeding month. Daily wages are paid at the end of the shift, weekly wages on the last working day of the week, and fortnightly wages before the end of the second day after the fortnight.
