UPI Limits and Rules

UPI biometric limit: ₹10,000 per transaction

UPI lets you confirm a payment with a fingerprint or face on your phone instead of typing the UPI PIN. NPCI circular OC 226 of 7 October 2025 introduced that option, and circular OC 226A of 3 July 2026 raises the per-transaction limit for it from ₹5,000 to ₹10,000, effective 7 August 2026 (read on 13 November 2026).

What it means

  • A payment of up to ₹10,000 can be authorised with an on-device biometric where your bank and app support it.
  • Above that amount you use the UPI PIN as before.
  • The circular tells issuer banks, PSP banks and UPI apps to tell customers about the new limit, and says existing validations and compliance requirements continue to apply.
  • It does not change any other UPI limit.

Common questions

What is the UPI limit with fingerprint or face unlock?

NPCI circular OC 226A says the per-transaction limit for on-device biometric authentication in UPI is raised from ₹5,000 to ₹10,000, effective 7 August 2026.
