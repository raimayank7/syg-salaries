UPI Limits and Rules

UPI transaction limits by category

NPCI circular OC 185B of 28 August 2025 raised the per-transaction limit for payments to verified merchants in eleven categories. The table is Annexure I of that circular, read on 13 November 2026. The limits applied from 15 September 2025.

How to read it

  • The enhanced limits are for merchants categorised as "Verified Merchant", and acquiring banks must give them only to merchants that follow NPCI’s UPI guidelines.
  • Member banks may keep their own, lower internal limits within NPCI’s ceilings, so your app or bank can show less than the table.
  • The cumulative limit is kept by the issuing bank, which is why your 24-hour total can stop a payment even when each one is within the per-transaction limit.
  • NPCI’s earlier circular OC 185A (24 August 2024) had already raised the limit to ₹5 lakh for tax-payment categories.
  • The limit for person-to-person payments is not changed by this circular and is not stated in it.

Common questions

What is the highest UPI limit?

NPCI circular OC 185B sets ₹5 lakh per transaction for most of the categories in its table, with a cumulative limit of ₹10 lakh in 24 hours for capital market, insurance, e-marketplace, travel and collections payments. It applies to verified merchants in those categories, not to every payment.

Does the higher limit apply to payments to friends?

No. The circular says the per-transaction limit for person-to-person payments continues as per the existing guidelines and states no figure.
