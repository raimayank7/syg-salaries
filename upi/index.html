UPI money debited but not received?

RBI sets a time limit for banks to reverse a failed payment and pays ₹100 a day for each day beyond it. Work out what is due. Rules from the RBI circular of 20 September 2019, read on 13 November 2026. Nothing you type leaves your device.

Common questions

How long does a bank have to reverse a failed UPI transfer?

RBI’s circular says that when money is debited but the beneficiary is not credited, the beneficiary bank must auto-reverse latest on T + 1 day, where T is the day of the transaction. For a UPI payment to a merchant with no confirmation, the auto-reversal is due within T + 5 days.

What compensation do I get for a delay?

₹100 for each day of delay beyond the time limit, credited to your account. RBI says it is paid on its own, without waiting for a complaint or claim.

Where do I complain if I am not given redress?

RBI says customers who do not get the redress described in the time limits can complain under the Reserve Bank Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2021.
