US federal take-home pay, 2026

This page works out what an employee keeps of a salary after federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare in 2026. State and local tax is not included, so the real take-home is lower where a state taxes wages. Figures from the IRS and the Social Security Administration, read on 13 November 2026.

What is not included

State and local tax, state disability and family-leave contributions, tax credits, and pre-tax deductions such as a 401(k) or health insurance. Wages are the only income. The 0.9% Additional Medicare tax is applied as employers withhold it, on wages over $200,000 for every filing status; on the tax return the threshold depends on the filing status.

Common questions

How much federal tax on $75,000 in 2026?

A single filer taking the standard deduction of $16,100 has taxable income of $58,900 and owes $7,670 in federal income tax, plus $4,650 Social Security and $1,088 Medicare.

Why is state tax missing?

States set their own rates, and this page has not read the state revenue pages, so it shows the federal part only. Nine states charge no tax on wages, but the list was not checked against each state’s own page here.

Sources

  • IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32: tax year 2026 rate schedules and standard deduction ($16,100 single, $24,150 head of household, $32,200 married filing jointly), as already checked on the US Tax Brackets section, read on 13 November 2026.
  • Social Security Administration, "Contribution and Benefit Base" (ssa.gov): $184,500 for 2026; the tax is 6.2% for employees and Medicare 1.45%.
  • IRS, "Topic no. 751, Social Security and Medicare withholding rates" (irs.gov): 0.9% Additional Medicare tax on wages over $200,000.
