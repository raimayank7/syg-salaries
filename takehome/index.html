Assumptions

A steady yearly salary from one job in the 2026-27 tax year, standard tax code, National Insurance category A, no pension contribution, no benefits in kind and no other income. Student loan is taken as 9% of earnings above the yearly threshold of the plan (HMRC works it per pay period). Scottish bands are used only where the page says Scotland. Take-home pay on your payslip can differ.

Common questions

How much is £40,000 after tax in the UK?

In the 2026-27 tax year, with no pension or student loan: Income Tax £5,486, National Insurance £2,194, so you keep £32,320 a year, £2,693.30 a month.

What is the Personal Allowance?

The amount of income you do not pay Income Tax on: £12,570 for the standard allowance in 2026-27 (GOV.UK). It reduces by £1 for every £2 of adjusted net income above £100,000 and is nil at £125,140.

What National Insurance does an employee pay?

For category A in 2026-27: nothing up to £12,570 a year, 8% between £12,570 and £50,270 and 2% above (GOV.UK).
