Canada take-home pay by province, 2026

This calculator follows the Canada Revenue Agency’s own payroll method for 2026: federal tax and provincial or territorial tax on income after the additional CPP contributions, less the basic personal amount, CPP and EI credits, plus CPP and EI themselves. Figures from the CRA T4032 tables, read on 13 November 2026.

What is not included

Quebec and Manitoba; RRSP and other deductions; other credits; self-employment. The federal basic personal amount is scaled down in a straight line between taxable incomes of $181,440 and $258,482, the usual CRA rule, which was not checked in the worksheet. The second additional CPP contribution is treated like the first, as a deduction from income.

Common questions

How much tax on $90,000 in Ontario in 2026?

On employment income of $90,000 the calculation gives about $11,252 federal tax, $5,782 Ontario tax with surtax and health premium, $4,646 CPP and $1,123 EI, leaving $67,197.

Why are Quebec and Manitoba missing?

Quebec has its own system with its own pages. Manitoba is left out because two CRA pages give different 2026 brackets (47,000 and 100,000 against 47,564 and 101,200) and its basic personal amount is reduced above $200,000 by a worksheet that was not read.
