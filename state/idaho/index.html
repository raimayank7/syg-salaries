How pay is spread in Idaho

By median pay, Idaho is number 35 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $29,300 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $103,240. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $36,660 to $72,320. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $61,170, 28% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 857,440 jobs counted here are 0.6% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Idaho are Office and Administrative Support (106,250 jobs, 13%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (73,900 jobs, 9%), Sales and Related (71,980 jobs, 9%) and Transportation and Material Moving (71,900 jobs, 9%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Forest and Conservation Technicians (12.1 times the national share), Agricultural Technicians (7.8 times the national share) and Food Science Technicians (7.0 times the national share). The table on this page lists 637 occupations; for 19 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 175 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 15 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 123 pay less and 37 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Food Batchmakers ($49,570 here, $42,290 nationally), Parts Salespersons ($44,580 here, $38,630 nationally) and Physicians, All Other ($299,340 here, $265,930 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Lawyers ($103,400 here, $159,670 nationally), Computer Systems Analysts ($75,310 here, $105,850 nationally) and Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers ($51,370 here, $69,990 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Idaho

What is the median salary in Idaho?

The median pay of all employees in Idaho was $47,970 a year ($23.06 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Idaho?

Fast Food and Counter Workers, with 25,810 jobs and a median pay of $28,940 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Idaho?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Airline Pilots, Copilots, and Flight Engineers ($507,640), Anesthesiologists ($449,990) and General Internal Medicine Physicians ($355,390).
