How pay is spread in Illinois

By median pay, Illinois is number 20 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $32,900 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $127,770. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $37,960 to $83,060. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $71,130, 37% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 6,098,130 jobs counted here are 3.9% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Illinois are Office and Administrative Support (670,150 jobs, 11%), Transportation and Material Moving (630,030 jobs, 10%), Management (533,700 jobs, 9%) and Sales and Related (518,130 jobs, 9%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Mathematical Science Occupations, All Other (9.1 times the national share), Nuclear Technicians (3.1 times the national share) and Mixing and Blending Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders (2.8 times the national share). The table on this page lists 759 occupations; for 10 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 452 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 193 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 141 pay less and 118 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Insulation Workers, Mechanical ($100,400 here, $58,340 nationally), Operating Engineers and Other Construction Equipment Operators ($97,740 here, $59,850 nationally) and Structural Iron and Steel Workers ($101,850 here, $62,780 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Physicians, All Other ($132,540 here, $265,930 nationally), Transportation Inspectors ($47,990 here, $92,100 nationally) and General Internal Medicine Physicians ($140,680 here, $256,560 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Illinois

What is the median salary in Illinois?

The median pay of all employees in Illinois was $51,960 a year ($24.98 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Illinois?

Laborers and Freight, Stock, and Material Movers, Hand, with 195,390 jobs and a median pay of $40,550 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Illinois?
