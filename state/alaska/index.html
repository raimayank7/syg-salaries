How pay is spread in Alaska

By median pay, Alaska is number 4 of the 54 states and territories in the figures. The lowest-paid tenth of employees earned less than $34,930 a year and the highest-paid tenth more than $128,720. The middle half, between the 25% and 75% lines, earned from $43,470 to $92,910. For the whole country those outer lines were $31,200 and $128,560. The average (mean) pay was $74,720, 22% above the median, because high earners lift an average more than a median. The 325,570 jobs counted here are 0.2% of all jobs in the national figures.

The kinds of work

Adding up the occupations with a published job count, the largest fields of work in Alaska are Office and Administrative Support (39,560 jobs, 12%), Transportation and Material Moving (29,580 jobs, 9%), Food Preparation and Serving Related (27,570 jobs, 9%) and Management (24,560 jobs, 8%). Some occupations are far more common here than elsewhere. Set against their share of all jobs in the country, the most concentrated are Gambling Service Workers, All Other (23.7 times the national share), Zoologists and Wildlife Biologists (17.2 times the national share) and Tour and Travel Guides (14.8 times the national share). The table on this page lists 528 occupations; for 17 of them the Bureau released a job count but no pay figure.

Same job, different pay

Among the 78 occupations with at least 1,000 jobs and a yearly median in the state, 60 pay more than the same occupation does nationally, 10 pay less and 8 are within 2%. The widest gaps above the national median are for Service Unit Operators, Oil and Gas ($106,480 here, $58,160 nationally), Substitute Teachers, Short-Term ($66,920 here, $41,670 nationally) and Plumbers, Pipefitters, and Steamfitters ($93,920 here, $63,800 nationally). The widest gaps below it are for Waiters and Waitresses ($26,310 here, $35,230 nationally), Financial Managers ($127,030 here, $166,570 nationally) and Commercial Pilots ($100,120 here, $123,220 nationally). A gap in pay is not a gap in living standard: prices, housing costs and taxes differ between states, and these statistics do not adjust for them.

Questions about pay in Alaska

What is the median salary in Alaska?

The median pay of all employees in Alaska was $61,000 a year ($29.33 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. The national median was $50,980.

What is the most common job in Alaska?

Retail Salespersons, with 7,890 jobs and a median pay of $37,290 a year.

What are the highest-paying jobs in Alaska?

The three highest medians published as a figure are Emergency Medicine Physicians ($452,620), Obstetricians and Gynecologists ($421,450) and General Internal Medicine Physicians ($366,080). Occupations shown as "$239,200 or more" are paid above the highest value the Bureau reports.
