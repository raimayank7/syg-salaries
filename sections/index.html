Common questions

Where did section 80C go in the Income-tax Act, 2025?

The department's navigator maps old section 80C to section 123 of the new Act, and the Act's section 123 allows a deduction for the sums listed in Schedule XV up to ₹1,50,000 (read from the Act).

What happened to TDS section 194C?

The old TDS sections are combined into sections 392 and 393. A payment to a contractor, old section 194C, is under section 393(1) [Table: Sl. No. 6(i)] from 1 April 2026; rates are unchanged (CBDT FAQs, Q6.2 and Q6.3).

Which Act applies to FY 2025-26?

The 1961 Act. Income earned in FY 2025-26 is governed by it and assessed in AY 2026-27; the 2025 Act applies to income from 1 April 2026 (CBDT FAQs, Q1.12).
