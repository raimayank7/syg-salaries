What is not shown

The last page of the guide has rows with form names instead of numbers (for example "Form 1 (Declaration by Unit of IFSC)") whose columns do not read cleanly from the PDF; they are left out here. The guide itself says forms of the 1961 Act stay on the e-filing portal under a separate tab for earlier years.

Read on 13 November 2026. The Income-tax Act, 2025 came into force on 1 April 2026 (section 1(3)); the 1961 Act continues to apply to tax years before that (section 536 and the CBDT's FAQs). The mapping comes from the department's navigator, which was drawn up for the Bill, and each new number was checked against the enacted Act; rows that could not be checked are marked.
