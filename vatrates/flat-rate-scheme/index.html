UK VAT

VAT Flat Rate Scheme rates

Under the Flat Rate Scheme you pay HMRC a fixed percentage of your VAT-inclusive turnover and keep the difference from the VAT you charged. GOV.UK lists the percentages below (read on 13 November 2026); you cannot reclaim VAT on purchases except capital assets over £2,000.

Worked example

GOV.UK’s example: a photographer bills £1,000 plus 20% VAT, £1,200 in all. The flat rate is 11%, so the payment is 11% of £1,200, which is £132.00.

Common questions

Who can join the Flat Rate Scheme?

GOV.UK says you may be able to join if your VAT turnover is £150,000 or less, excluding VAT. You apply to HMRC.

What is a limited cost business?

GOV.UK says it is one whose goods cost less than either 2% of turnover or £1,000 a year (if that is more). It pays a higher flat rate of 16.5%.

Is there a first-year discount?

Yes, GOV.UK says you get a 1% discount if you are in your first year as a VAT-registered business.
