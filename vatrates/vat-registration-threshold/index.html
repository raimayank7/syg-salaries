UK VAT

UK VAT registration threshold

GOV.UK says businesses have to register for VAT if their VAT taxable turnover is more than £90,000, and can choose to register below that (read on 13 November 2026). The deregistration threshold was not read for this site: see GOV.UK.

What registering does to your prices

Once registered you add VAT to your sales at the right rate, keep records, send VAT returns, usually every 3 months, and pay HMRC the difference between the VAT you charged and the VAT you paid on business purchases. At a 20% rate a £100 price becomes £120, so a customer who cannot reclaim VAT sees a rise of one fifth unless you absorb it.

Common questions

What is the VAT registration threshold?

GOV.UK says a business must register when its VAT taxable turnover is more than £90,000, and may register if it is less.

What is VAT taxable turnover?

The total of everything you sell that is not exempt from VAT, at the standard, reduced or zero rate. GOV.UK has a separate page on how to work it out.
