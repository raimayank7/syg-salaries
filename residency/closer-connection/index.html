US Tax Residency Test

Closer connection exception

Even if you meet the substantial presence test you can still be treated as a nonresident of the United States for tax if you qualify for one of two exceptions listed on the IRS page (read on 13 November 2026): the closer connection exception, and a version available only to students. The conditions are on the IRS pages for those exceptions; this site has not read them and does not test them.

The calculator therefore tells you only whether the day count is met. Residency can also depend on a green card and on a tax treaty, which are outside this site.

Common questions

Can I be a nonresident even if I meet the test?

Yes, if you qualify for the closer connection exception, or for the version for students; the IRS pages on those exceptions give the conditions.
