Model Makers, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Model Makers, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $63,340 a year ($30.45 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up and operate machines, such as lathes, milling and engraving machines, and jig borers to make working models of metal or plastic objects. Includes template makers.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-4061.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Model Makers, Metal and Plastic is $63,340, which is 24% higher. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 13 by median pay. With 2,610 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Model Makers, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($92,960), Florida ($79,930) and New York ($77,710). The lowest was in North Carolina ($45,760), Pennsylvania ($52,990) and Utah ($53,650). The most jobs were in Michigan (710), California (260) and Texas (230). The occupation is most concentrated in Michigan, where its share of all jobs is 9.6 times the national share.
