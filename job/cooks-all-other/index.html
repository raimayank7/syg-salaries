Cooks, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Cooks, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $37,690 a year ($18.12 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Cooks, All Other is $37,690, which is 26% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Food Preparation and Serving Related, it ranks number 4 by median pay. With 19,350 jobs, it makes up about 1.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Cooks, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($69,570), Washington ($52,510) and Nevada ($51,220). The lowest was in Iowa ($26,060), Louisiana ($28,930) and Arkansas ($30,780). The most jobs were in Texas (4,650), California (3,990) and Tennessee (2,060). The occupation is most concentrated in Tennessee, where its share of all jobs is 5.0 times the national share.
