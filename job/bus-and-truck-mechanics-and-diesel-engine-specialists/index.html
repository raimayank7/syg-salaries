Bus and Truck Mechanics and Diesel Engine Specialists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Bus and Truck Mechanics and Diesel Engine Specialists in the United States earned a median wage of $61,770 a year ($29.70 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul buses and trucks, or maintain and repair any type of diesel engines. Includes mechanics working primarily with automobile or marine diesel engines.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 49-3031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Bus and Truck Mechanics and Diesel Engine Specialists is $61,770, which is 21% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 22 by median pay. With 289,960 jobs, it makes up about 19 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Bus and Truck Mechanics and Diesel Engine Specialists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Hawaii ($80,080), Washington ($77,900) and Alaska ($75,580). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($40,110), West Virginia ($46,610) and Alabama ($50,470). The most jobs were in Texas (29,870), California (20,940) and Florida (14,900). The occupation is most concentrated in North Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 2.2 times the national share.
