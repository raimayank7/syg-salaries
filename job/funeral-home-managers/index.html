Funeral Home Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Funeral Home Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $78,790 a year ($37.88 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate the services or resources of funeral homes. Includes activities such as determining prices for services or merchandise and managing the facilities of funeral homes. Excludes “Morticians, Undertakers, and Funeral Arrangers” (39-4031).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-9171.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Funeral Home Managers is $78,790, which is 55% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 32 by median pay. With 13,910 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($92,840) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Funeral Home Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Rhode Island ($122,150), Pennsylvania ($104,400) and New York ($103,530). The lowest was in Kentucky ($47,040), Arkansas ($60,260) and Nevada ($60,750). The most jobs were in Texas (1,230), Florida (1,050) and New York (820). The occupation is most concentrated in Rhode Island, where its share of all jobs is 2.6 times the national share.
