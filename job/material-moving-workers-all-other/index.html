Material Moving Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Material Moving Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $41,800 a year ($20.10 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Material Moving Workers, All Other is $41,800, which is 18% lower. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 37 by median pay. With 23,480 jobs, it makes up about 1.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Material Moving Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($71,640), New Jersey ($64,870) and Colorado ($61,240). The lowest was in Ohio ($33,420), Wisconsin ($35,580) and Arkansas ($35,650). The most jobs were in California (9,120), Texas (2,080) and Wisconsin (1,180). The occupation is most concentrated in California, where its share of all jobs is 3.3 times the national share.
