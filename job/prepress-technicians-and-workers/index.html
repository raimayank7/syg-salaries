Prepress Technicians and Workers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Prepress Technicians and Workers in the United States earned a median wage of $48,690 a year ($23.41 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Prepress Technicians and Workers is $48,690, which is 4% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 43 by median pay. With 23,840 jobs, it makes up about 1.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Prepress Technicians and Workers pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($100,980), Maryland ($60,770) and Massachusetts ($57,250). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($33,030), Alabama ($35,020) and West Virginia ($35,320). The most jobs were in Minnesota (2,880), Texas (2,320) and California (2,020). The occupation is most concentrated in Minnesota, where its share of all jobs is 6.4 times the national share.
