First-Line Supervisors of Landscaping, Lawn Service, and Groundskeeping Workers: pay in the United States, May 2025

First-Line Supervisors of Landscaping, Lawn Service, and Groundskeeping Workers in the United States earned a median wage of $58,430 a year ($28.09 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Directly supervise and coordinate activities of workers engaged in landscaping or groundskeeping activities. Work may involve reviewing contracts to ascertain service, machine, and workforce requirements; answering inquiries from potential customers regarding methods, material, and price ranges; and preparing estimates according to labor, material, and machine costs.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 37-1012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for First-Line Supervisors of Landscaping, Lawn Service, and Groundskeeping Workers is $58,430, which is 15% higher. Among the 10 occupations of the field Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance, it ranks number 1 by median pay. With 130,760 jobs, it makes up about 8.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

First-Line Supervisors of Landscaping, Lawn Service, and Groundskeeping Workers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Delaware ($74,340), Washington ($71,930) and New York ($70,760). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($37,510), Guam ($40,100) and Mississippi ($47,130). The most jobs were in California (14,850), Florida (12,020) and Texas (11,260). The occupation is most concentrated in Colorado, where its share of all jobs is 1.8 times the national share.
