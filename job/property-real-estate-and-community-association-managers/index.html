Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $69,990 a year ($33.65 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate the selling, buying, leasing, or governance activities of commercial, industrial, or residential real estate properties. Includes managers of homeowner and condominium associations, rented or leased housing units, buildings, or land (including rights-of-way).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-9141.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers is $69,990, which is 37% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 33 by median pay. With 311,180 jobs, it makes up about 20 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($83,710) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Property, Real Estate, and Community Association Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($119,320), Massachusetts ($103,410) and Colorado ($100,080). The lowest was in Arkansas ($47,760), South Dakota ($49,490) and Louisiana ($49,990). The most jobs were in California (50,040), Texas (45,350) and Florida (30,160). The occupation is most concentrated in Virgin Islands, where its share of all jobs is 2.2 times the national share.
