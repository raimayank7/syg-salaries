Human Resources Assistants, Except Payroll and Timekeeping: pay in the United States, May 2025

Human Resources Assistants, Except Payroll and Timekeeping in the United States earned a median wage of $50,610 a year ($24.33 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Compile and keep personnel records. Record data for each employee, such as address, weekly earnings, absences, amount of sales or production, supervisory reports, and date of and reason for termination. May prepare reports for employment records, file employment records, or search employee files and furnish information to authorized persons.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 43-4161.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Human Resources Assistants, Except Payroll and Timekeeping is $50,610, which is about the same. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 18 by median pay. With 90,220 jobs, it makes up about 5.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Human Resources Assistants, Except Payroll and Timekeeping pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($59,050), California ($57,590) and Washington ($57,390). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($29,080), Guam ($35,990) and Alabama ($42,140). The most jobs were in California (11,490), New York (8,800) and Texas (5,890). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 2.5 times the national share.
