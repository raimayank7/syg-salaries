Pharmacy Technicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Pharmacy Technicians in the United States earned a median wage of $45,750 a year ($22.00 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Prepare medications under the direction of a pharmacist. May measure, mix, count out, label, and record amounts and dosages of medications according to prescription orders.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-2052.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Pharmacy Technicians is $45,750, which is 10% lower. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 67 by median pay. With 471,680 jobs, it makes up about 30 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Pharmacy Technicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($58,900), California ($55,110) and Oregon ($54,900). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($29,520), Guam ($36,010) and West Virginia ($38,210). The most jobs were in Texas (41,740), California (39,860) and Florida (38,460). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 2.0 times the national share.
