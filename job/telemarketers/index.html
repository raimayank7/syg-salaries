Telemarketers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Telemarketers in the United States earned a median wage of $35,450 a year ($17.04 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Telemarketers is $35,450, which is 30% lower. Among the 22 occupations of the field Sales and Related, it ranks number 20 by median pay. With 58,430 jobs, it makes up about 3.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Telemarketers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Kansas ($48,010), Colorado ($47,670) and California ($46,000). The lowest was in Mississippi ($21,540), North Dakota ($23,440) and Oklahoma ($26,880). The most jobs were in Florida (9,760), California (3,880) and Texas (3,660). The occupation is most concentrated in Nevada, where its share of all jobs is 3.8 times the national share.
