Public Relations Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Public Relations Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $146,910 a year ($70.63 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Public Relations Managers is $146,910, which is 188% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 11 by median pay. With 74,850 jobs, it makes up about 4.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($164,760) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Public Relations Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($197,600), New York ($180,880) and Virginia ($176,340). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($72,290), West Virginia ($82,380) and Louisiana ($94,380). The most jobs were in California (9,400), Texas (8,190) and New York (7,710). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 21.9 times the national share.
