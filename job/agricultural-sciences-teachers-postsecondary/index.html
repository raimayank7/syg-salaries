Agricultural Sciences Teachers, Postsecondary: pay in the United States, May 2025

Agricultural Sciences Teachers, Postsecondary in the United States earned a median wage of $98,700 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Teach courses in the agricultural sciences. Includes teachers of agronomy, dairy sciences, fisheries management, horticultural sciences, poultry sciences, range management, and agricultural soil conservation. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research. Excludes “Forestry and Conservation Science Teachers, Postsecondary” (25-1043).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 25-1041.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Agricultural Sciences Teachers, Postsecondary is $98,700, which is 94% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 10 by median pay. With 8,920 jobs, it makes up about 0.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Agricultural Sciences Teachers, Postsecondary pay by state

The highest median pay was in Michigan ($136,130), New Jersey ($131,380) and Maryland ($129,920). The lowest was in Ohio ($46,160), Florida ($62,420) and Oklahoma ($64,080). The most jobs were in Texas (1,170), North Carolina (500) and Oklahoma (410). The occupation is most concentrated in Wyoming, where its share of all jobs is 5.7 times the national share.
