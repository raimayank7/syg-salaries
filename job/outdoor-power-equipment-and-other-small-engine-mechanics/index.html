Outdoor Power Equipment and Other Small Engine Mechanics: pay in the United States, May 2025

Outdoor Power Equipment and Other Small Engine Mechanics in the United States earned a median wage of $47,880 a year ($23.02 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul small engines used to power lawn mowers, chain saws, recreational sporting equipment, and related equipment.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Outdoor Power Equipment and Other Small Engine Mechanics is $47,880, which is 6% lower. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 43 by median pay. With 36,060 jobs, it makes up about 2.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Outdoor Power Equipment and Other Small Engine Mechanics pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($60,190), Hawaii ($58,110) and Massachusetts ($57,830). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($23,730), Arkansas ($37,610) and Mississippi ($37,900). The most jobs were in Florida (3,450), California (2,470) and Texas (2,270). The occupation is most concentrated in Maine, where its share of all jobs is 2.0 times the national share.
