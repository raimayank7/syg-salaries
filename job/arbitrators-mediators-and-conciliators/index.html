Arbitrators, Mediators, and Conciliators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Arbitrators, Mediators, and Conciliators in the United States earned a median wage of $75,530 a year ($36.31 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Arbitrators, Mediators, and Conciliators is $75,530, which is 48% higher. Among the 8 occupations of the field Legal, it ranks number 4 by median pay. With 9,210 jobs, it makes up about 0.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($91,010) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Arbitrators, Mediators, and Conciliators pay by state

The highest median pay was in Illinois ($162,410), District of Columbia ($110,300) and New Mexico ($110,180). The lowest was in Oklahoma ($36,780), Louisiana ($46,100) and Texas ($50,940). The most jobs were in New York (1,050), Pennsylvania (310) and Wisconsin (260). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 3.6 times the national share.
