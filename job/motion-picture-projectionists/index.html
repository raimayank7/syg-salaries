Motion Picture Projectionists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Motion Picture Projectionists in the United States earned a median wage of $38,270 a year ($18.40 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Motion Picture Projectionists is $38,270, which is 25% lower. Among the 32 occupations of the field Personal Care and Service, it ranks number 15 by median pay. With 1,480 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($51,480) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Motion Picture Projectionists pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($82,260), Illinois ($78,990) and California ($74,840). The lowest was in Tennessee ($22,750), Iowa ($23,210) and Missouri ($29,140). The most jobs were in New York (270), California (190) and Iowa (100). The occupation is most concentrated in Iowa, where its share of all jobs is 6.7 times the national share.
