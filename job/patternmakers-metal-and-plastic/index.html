Patternmakers, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Patternmakers, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $58,000 a year ($27.89 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Lay out, machine, fit, and assemble castings and parts to metal or plastic foundry patterns, core boxes, or match plates.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-4062.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Patternmakers, Metal and Plastic is $58,000, which is 14% higher. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 20 by median pay. With 1,470 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Patternmakers, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in Ohio ($97,690), Minnesota ($74,900) and Massachusetts ($65,210). The lowest was in North Carolina ($47,050), Texas ($49,390) and Wisconsin ($52,150). The most jobs were in Michigan (250), Wisconsin (230) and Pennsylvania (160). The occupation is most concentrated in Wisconsin, where its share of all jobs is 8.4 times the national share.
