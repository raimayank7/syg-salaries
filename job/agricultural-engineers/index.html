Agricultural Engineers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Agricultural Engineers in the United States earned a median wage of $98,590 a year ($47.40 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Apply knowledge of engineering technology and biological science to agricultural problems concerned with power and machinery, electrification, structures, soil and water conservation, and processing of agricultural products.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 17-2021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Agricultural Engineers is $98,590, which is 93% higher. Among the 36 occupations of the field Architecture and Engineering, it ranks number 19 by median pay. With 1,480 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Agricultural Engineers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Ohio ($119,250), Minnesota ($115,940) and Iowa ($96,290). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($40,690), Arkansas ($57,740) and Pennsylvania ($80,300). The most jobs were in Indiana (150), Missouri (120) and Oklahoma (120). The occupation is most concentrated in Oklahoma, where its share of all jobs is 7.5 times the national share.
