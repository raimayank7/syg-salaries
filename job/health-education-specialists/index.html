Health Education Specialists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Health Education Specialists in the United States earned a median wage of $64,070 a year ($30.80 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Provide and manage health education programs that help individuals, families, and their communities maximize and maintain healthy lifestyles. Use data to identify community needs prior to planning, implementing, monitoring, and evaluating programs designed to encourage healthy lifestyles, policies, and environments. May link health systems, health providers, insurers, and patients to address individual and population health needs. May serve as resource to assist individuals, other health professionals, or the community, and may administer fiscal resources for health education programs. Excludes “Community Health Workers” (21-1094).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 21-1091.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Health Education Specialists is $64,070, which is 26% higher. Among the 17 occupations of the field Community and Social Service, it ranks number 6 by median pay. With 65,690 jobs, it makes up about 4.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($73,280) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Health Education Specialists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Maryland ($104,470), District of Columbia ($103,330) and Georgia ($99,400). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($39,650), Florida ($46,360) and Tennessee ($49,630). The most jobs were in California (12,520), Texas (5,320) and New York (4,150). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 2.3 times the national share.
