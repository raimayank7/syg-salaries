Etchers and Engravers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Etchers and Engravers in the United States earned a median wage of $43,310 a year ($20.82 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Engrave or etch metal, wood, rubber, or other materials. Includes such workers as etcher-circuit processors, pantograph engravers, and silk screen etchers. Photoengravers are included in “Prepress Technicians and Workers” (51-5111).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-9194.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Etchers and Engravers is $43,310, which is 15% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 75 by median pay. With 7,750 jobs, it makes up about 0.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Etchers and Engravers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Vermont ($65,380), Rhode Island ($63,250) and Arizona ($59,940). The lowest was in South Carolina ($28,300), Alabama ($31,030) and Texas ($31,180). The most jobs were in California (1,040), Texas (630) and New York (520). The occupation is most concentrated in Vermont, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.
