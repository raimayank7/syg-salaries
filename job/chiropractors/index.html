Chiropractors: pay in the United States, May 2025

Chiropractors in the United States earned a median wage of $79,200 a year ($38.08 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Assess, treat, and care for patients by manipulation of spine and musculoskeletal system. May provide spinal adjustment or address sacral or pelvic misalignment.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Chiropractors is $79,200, which is 55% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 47 by median pay. With 39,630 jobs, it makes up about 2.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($90,520) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Chiropractors pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Jersey ($134,990), New York ($120,950) and Maine ($107,300). The lowest was in Utah ($53,900), Kansas ($57,600) and Georgia ($61,660). The most jobs were in Florida (3,220), Texas (2,840) and California (2,760). The occupation is most concentrated in North Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 3.3 times the national share.
