Surgeons, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Surgeons, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $414,010 a year ($199.04 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Surgeons, All Other is $414,010, which is 712% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 4 by median pay. With 25,140 jobs, it makes up about 1.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Surgeons, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in North Dakota ($604,890), Minnesota ($568,150) and Ohio ($554,220). The lowest was in California ($131,990), Connecticut ($161,680) and Kentucky ($162,160). The most jobs were in New York (4,540), Florida (2,260) and Texas (1,710). The occupation is most concentrated in Hawaii, where its share of all jobs is 4.4 times the national share.
