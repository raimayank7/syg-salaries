Sales Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Sales Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $148,270 a year ($71.28 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate the actual distribution or movement of a product or service to the customer. Coordinate sales distribution by establishing sales territories, quotas, and goals and establish training programs for sales representatives. Analyze sales statistics gathered by staff to determine sales potential and inventory requirements and monitor the preferences of customers.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-2022.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Sales Managers is $148,270, which is 191% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 9 by median pay. With 637,080 jobs, it makes up about 41 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($164,350) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Sales Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($217,640), Massachusetts ($191,200) and Virginia ($191,130). The lowest was in Guam ($60,740), Puerto Rico ($78,960) and West Virginia ($94,830). The most jobs were in California (112,440), Texas (76,010) and Florida (46,480). The occupation is most concentrated in California, where its share of all jobs is 1.5 times the national share.
