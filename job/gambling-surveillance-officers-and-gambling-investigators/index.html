Gambling Surveillance Officers and Gambling Investigators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Gambling Surveillance Officers and Gambling Investigators in the United States earned a median wage of $43,370 a year ($20.85 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Observe gambling operation for irregular activities such as cheating or theft by either employees or patrons. Investigate potential threats to gambling assets such as money, chips, and gambling equipment. Act as oversight and security agent for management and customers.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 33-9031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Gambling Surveillance Officers and Gambling Investigators is $43,370, which is 15% lower. Among the 24 occupations of the field Protective Service, it ranks number 19 by median pay. With 9,520 jobs, it makes up about 0.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Gambling Surveillance Officers and Gambling Investigators pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($61,770), Connecticut ($56,300) and Oregon ($50,120). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,950), South Dakota ($33,820) and Louisiana ($33,910). The most jobs were in Oklahoma (1,310), California (990) and Nevada (820). The occupation is most concentrated in Oklahoma, where its share of all jobs is 12.5 times the national share.
