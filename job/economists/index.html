Economists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Economists in the United States earned a median wage of $124,720 a year ($59.96 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Conduct research, prepare reports, or formulate plans to address economic problems related to the production and distribution of goods and services or monetary and fiscal policy. May collect and process economic and statistical data using sampling techniques and econometric methods. Excludes “Market Research Analysts and Marketing Specialists” (13-1161).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 19-3011.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Economists is $124,720, which is 145% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 6 by median pay. With 17,790 jobs, it makes up about 1.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($142,130) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Economists pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($167,590), New Jersey ($167,550) and Delaware ($159,120). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($70,750), Kentucky ($71,400) and Louisiana ($73,050). The most jobs were in District of Columbia (3,000), Maryland (1,130) and Massachusetts (1,030). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 37.3 times the national share.
