Shoe Machine Operators and Tenders: pay in the United States, May 2025

Shoe Machine Operators and Tenders in the United States earned a median wage of $35,650 a year ($17.14 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Shoe Machine Operators and Tenders is $35,650, which is 30% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 103 by median pay. With 3,280 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Shoe Machine Operators and Tenders pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($48,420), California ($47,730) and Maine ($39,850). The lowest was in Texas ($26,560), Minnesota ($37,600) and Wisconsin ($37,970). The most jobs were in Maine (530), Texas (500) and Minnesota (370). The occupation is most concentrated in Maine, where its share of all jobs is 39.2 times the national share.
