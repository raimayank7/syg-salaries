Food Service Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Food Service Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $69,390 a year ($33.36 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities of an organization or department that serves food and beverages. Excludes “Chefs and Head Cooks” (35-1011).

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Food Service Managers is $69,390, which is 36% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 35 by median pay. With 238,430 jobs, it makes up about 15 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Food Service Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($95,190), Massachusetts ($92,240) and Hawaii ($87,800). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($42,230), Guam ($44,770) and West Virginia ($54,800). The most jobs were in California (33,180), Texas (25,990) and Florida (18,500). The occupation is most concentrated in Virgin Islands, where its share of all jobs is 2.8 times the national share.
