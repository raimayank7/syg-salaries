Dentists, All Other Specialists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Dentists, All Other Specialists in the United States earned a median wage of $224,990 a year ($108.17 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Dentists, All Other Specialists is $224,990, which is 341% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 21 by median pay. With 5,230 jobs, it makes up about 0.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($247,930) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Dentists, All Other Specialists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Texas ($286,510), Oregon ($256,510) and Illinois ($250,880). The lowest was in Connecticut ($126,410), Colorado ($186,640) and Ohio ($192,050). The most jobs were in Texas (730), Minnesota (580) and Florida (380). The occupation is most concentrated in Minnesota, where its share of all jobs is 5.8 times the national share.
