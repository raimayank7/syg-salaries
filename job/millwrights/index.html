Millwrights: pay in the United States, May 2025

Millwrights in the United States earned a median wage of $65,700 a year ($31.59 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Millwrights is $65,700, which is 29% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 15 by median pay. With 40,330 jobs, it makes up about 2.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Millwrights pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Jersey ($107,540), New Mexico ($90,230) and Michigan ($89,190). The lowest was in Oklahoma ($50,950), Virginia ($51,430) and Arkansas ($56,310). The most jobs were in Texas (3,540), Michigan (2,900) and California (2,230). The occupation is most concentrated in Mississippi, where its share of all jobs is 4.2 times the national share.
