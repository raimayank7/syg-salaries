Procurement Clerks: pay in the United States, May 2025

Procurement Clerks in the United States earned a median wage of $50,580 a year ($24.32 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Compile information and records to draw up purchase orders for procurement of materials and services. Excludes “Wholesale and Retail Buyers, Except Farm Products” (13-1022).

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Procurement Clerks is $50,580, which is about the same. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 19 by median pay. With 55,810 jobs, it makes up about 3.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Procurement Clerks pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($62,880), Massachusetts ($60,110) and New Hampshire ($58,830). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($30,940), Guam ($38,100) and Tennessee ($38,710). The most jobs were in California (7,080), Tennessee (3,900) and Texas (3,740). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 6.1 times the national share.
