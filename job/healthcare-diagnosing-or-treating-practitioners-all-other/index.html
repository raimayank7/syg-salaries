Healthcare Diagnosing or Treating Practitioners, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Healthcare Diagnosing or Treating Practitioners, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $115,210 a year ($55.39 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Healthcare Diagnosing or Treating Practitioners, All Other is $115,210, which is 126% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 32 by median pay. With 28,630 jobs, it makes up about 1.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($127,660) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Healthcare Diagnosing or Treating Practitioners, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Maryland ($164,670), District of Columbia ($151,990) and New York ($127,360). The lowest was in Rhode Island ($81,280), Arkansas ($81,330) and New Hampshire ($82,550). The most jobs were in Maryland (5,840), Georgia (3,130) and California (1,600). The occupation is most concentrated in Maryland, where its share of all jobs is 11.5 times the national share.
