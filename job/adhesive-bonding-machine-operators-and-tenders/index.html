Adhesive Bonding Machine Operators and Tenders: pay in the United States, May 2025

Adhesive Bonding Machine Operators and Tenders in the United States earned a median wage of $46,460 a year ($22.34 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Operate or tend bonding machines that use adhesives to join items for further processing or to form a completed product. Processes include joining veneer sheets into plywood; gluing paper; or joining rubber and rubberized fabric parts, plastic, simulated leather, or other materials. Excludes “Shoe Machine Operators and Tenders” (51-6042).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-9191.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Adhesive Bonding Machine Operators and Tenders is $46,460, which is 9% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 58 by median pay. With 11,500 jobs, it makes up about 0.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Adhesive Bonding Machine Operators and Tenders pay by state

The highest median pay was in Utah ($65,500), Oklahoma ($59,980) and New Jersey ($59,170). The lowest was in Mississippi ($31,250), Maryland ($33,540) and Florida ($34,650). The most jobs were in North Carolina (1,590), Ohio (940) and Oregon (900). The occupation is most concentrated in Oregon, where its share of all jobs is 6.2 times the national share.
