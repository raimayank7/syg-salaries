Historians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Historians in the United States earned a median wage of $76,750 a year ($36.90 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Research, analyze, record, and interpret the past as recorded in sources, such as government and institutional records, newspapers and other periodicals, photographs, interviews, films, electronic media, and unpublished manuscripts, such as personal diaries and letters.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 19-3093.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Historians is $76,750, which is 51% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 31 by median pay. With 3,450 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Historians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($121,710), Maryland ($120,580) and District of Columbia ($118,290). The lowest was in Utah ($33,840), Wisconsin ($45,810) and Mississippi ($46,010). The most jobs were in New York (410), District of Columbia (250) and California (210). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 16.3 times the national share.
