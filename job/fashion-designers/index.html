Fashion Designers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Fashion Designers in the United States earned a median wage of $80,960 a year ($38.92 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Fashion Designers is $80,960, which is 59% higher. Among the 41 occupations of the field Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media, it ranks number 7 by median pay. With 21,450 jobs, it makes up about 1.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($91,230) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Fashion Designers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($103,520), New Jersey ($101,610) and New Hampshire ($100,160). The lowest was in Texas ($40,040), Indiana ($40,740) and Minnesota ($42,110). The most jobs were in California (6,470), New York (5,760) and Texas (1,850). The occupation is most concentrated in New York, where its share of all jobs is 4.3 times the national share.
