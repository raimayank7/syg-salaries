Computer and Information Systems Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Computer and Information Systems Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $175,140 a year ($84.20 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate activities in such fields as electronic data processing, information systems, systems analysis, and computer programming. Excludes “Computer Occupations” (15-1211 through 15-1299).

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Computer and Information Systems Managers is $175,140, which is 244% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 2 by median pay. With 670,570 jobs, it makes up about 43 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Computer and Information Systems Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($218,290), Massachusetts ($214,960) and New York ($214,300). The lowest was in Virgin Islands ($87,900), Guam ($92,560) and Puerto Rico ($99,780). The most jobs were in California (94,720), Texas (73,060) and New York (48,050). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 1.9 times the national share.
