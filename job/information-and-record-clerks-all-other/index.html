Information and Record Clerks, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Information and Record Clerks, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $49,500 a year ($23.80 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Information and Record Clerks, All Other is $49,500, which is 3% lower. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 24 by median pay. With 134,920 jobs, it makes up about 8.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Information and Record Clerks, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($70,490), California ($58,680) and Rhode Island ($58,470). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($27,560), Wisconsin ($32,470) and North Carolina ($33,880). The most jobs were in California (18,010), Texas (16,770) and Colorado (13,960). The occupation is most concentrated in Colorado, where its share of all jobs is 5.6 times the national share.
