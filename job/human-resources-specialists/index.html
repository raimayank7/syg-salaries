Human Resources Specialists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Human Resources Specialists in the United States earned a median wage of $75,940 a year ($36.51 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Recruit, screen, interview, or place individuals within an organization. May perform other activities in multiple human resources areas. Excludes “Compensation, Benefits, and Job Analysis Specialists” (13-1141) and “Training and Development Specialists” (13-1151).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 13-1071.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Human Resources Specialists is $75,940, which is 49% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 24 by median pay. With 912,430 jobs, it makes up about 59 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Human Resources Specialists pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($110,970), Massachusetts ($85,630) and Washington ($84,550). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($40,900), Arkansas ($55,010) and Guam ($59,900). The most jobs were in California (104,000), Texas (84,930) and Florida (62,730). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 1.7 times the national share.
