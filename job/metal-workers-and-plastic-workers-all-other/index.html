Metal Workers and Plastic Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Metal Workers and Plastic Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $45,950 a year ($22.09 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Metal Workers and Plastic Workers, All Other is $45,950, which is 10% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 63 by median pay. With 15,900 jobs, it makes up about 1.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Metal Workers and Plastic Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Hawaii ($78,000), Washington ($65,760) and Oklahoma ($65,040). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($21,840), New Jersey ($33,790) and Florida ($36,040). The most jobs were in California (1,970), Georgia (1,730) and Texas (1,410). The occupation is most concentrated in New Hampshire, where its share of all jobs is 11.3 times the national share.
