Gas Plant Operators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Gas Plant Operators in the United States earned a median wage of $87,820 a year ($42.22 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Gas Plant Operators is $87,820, which is 72% higher. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 5 by median pay. With 18,030 jobs, it makes up about 1.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Gas Plant Operators pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($130,220), New Jersey ($110,900) and Oregon ($109,760). The lowest was in Kentucky ($63,070), Tennessee ($66,560) and Florida ($67,170). The most jobs were in Texas (2,550), Pennsylvania (1,370) and Oklahoma (970). The occupation is most concentrated in North Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 15.6 times the national share.
