Civil Engineering Technologists and Technicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Civil Engineering Technologists and Technicians in the United States earned a median wage of $64,950 a year ($31.23 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Apply theory and principles of civil engineering in planning, designing, and overseeing construction and maintenance of structures and facilities under the direction of engineering staff or physical scientists.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Civil Engineering Technologists and Technicians is $64,950, which is 27% higher. Among the 36 occupations of the field Architecture and Engineering, it ranks number 33 by median pay. With 68,520 jobs, it makes up about 4.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Civil Engineering Technologists and Technicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oregon ($85,390), California ($83,490) and Washington ($79,580). The lowest was in Guam ($37,230), Puerto Rico ($42,390) and Mississippi ($46,430). The most jobs were in California (7,250), Texas (4,470) and Illinois (4,190). The occupation is most concentrated in Wyoming, where its share of all jobs is 2.5 times the national share.
