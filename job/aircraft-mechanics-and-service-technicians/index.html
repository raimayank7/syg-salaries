Aircraft Mechanics and Service Technicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Aircraft Mechanics and Service Technicians in the United States earned a median wage of $79,870 a year ($38.40 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Diagnose, adjust, repair, or overhaul aircraft engines and assemblies, such as hydraulic and pneumatic systems. Excludes “Avionics Technicians” (49-2091).

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Aircraft Mechanics and Service Technicians is $79,870, which is 57% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 6 by median pay. With 138,090 jobs, it makes up about 8.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Aircraft Mechanics and Service Technicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Jersey ($105,370), New York ($102,800) and Maryland ($100,500). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($45,100), West Virginia ($45,470) and Wyoming ($56,680). The most jobs were in Texas (16,850), Florida (14,850) and California (12,590). The occupation is most concentrated in Oklahoma, where its share of all jobs is 5.6 times the national share.
