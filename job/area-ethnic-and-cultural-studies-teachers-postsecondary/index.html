Area, Ethnic, and Cultural Studies Teachers, Postsecondary: pay in the United States, May 2025

Area, Ethnic, and Cultural Studies Teachers, Postsecondary in the United States earned a median wage of $85,020 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Teach courses pertaining to the culture and development of an area, an ethnic group, or any other group, such as Latin American studies, women’s studies, or urban affairs. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 25-1062.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Area, Ethnic, and Cultural Studies Teachers, Postsecondary is $85,020, which is 67% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 17 by median pay. With 11,300 jobs, it makes up about 0.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($98,330) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Area, Ethnic, and Cultural Studies Teachers, Postsecondary pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Hampshire ($125,450), Kansas ($107,120) and California ($105,220). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($46,660), Alabama ($63,090) and South Carolina ($64,500). The most jobs were in New York (1,520), Texas (830) and California (770). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 4.6 times the national share.
