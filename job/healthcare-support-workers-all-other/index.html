Healthcare Support Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Healthcare Support Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $48,430 a year ($23.28 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Healthcare Support Workers, All Other is $48,430, which is 5% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Healthcare Support, it ranks number 4 by median pay. With 109,740 jobs, it makes up about 7.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Healthcare Support Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($73,590), Washington ($62,040) and Alaska ($61,300). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($26,660), Louisiana ($34,040) and Mississippi ($34,900). The most jobs were in California (23,590), Texas (10,320) and Florida (5,550). The occupation is most concentrated in Hawaii, where its share of all jobs is 3.2 times the national share.
