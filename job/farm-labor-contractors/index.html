Farm Labor Contractors: pay in the United States, May 2025

Farm Labor Contractors in the United States earned a median wage of $58,460 a year ($28.11 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Farm Labor Contractors is $58,460, which is 15% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 30 by median pay. With 310 jobs, it makes up about 0.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Farm Labor Contractors pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($85,710), Florida ($79,590) and Minnesota ($50,730). The lowest was in Minnesota ($50,730), Florida ($79,590) and California ($85,710).
