Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners: pay in the United States, May 2025

Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners in the United States earned a median wage of $34,980 a year ($16.82 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Post information enabling patrons to wager on various races and sporting events. Assist in the operation of games such as keno and bingo. May operate random number-generating equipment and announce the numbers for patrons. Receive, verify, and record patrons’ wagers. Scan and process winning tickets presented by patrons and pay out winnings for those wagers.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 39-3012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners is $34,980, which is 31% lower. Among the 32 occupations of the field Personal Care and Service, it ranks number 26 by median pay. With 8,950 jobs, it makes up about 0.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Gambling and Sports Book Writers and Runners pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($54,740), New Jersey ($48,260) and Massachusetts ($47,720). The lowest was in West Virginia ($19,740), Montana ($22,030) and Louisiana ($26,620). The most jobs were in California (1,480), Nevada (1,440) and Montana (1,050). The occupation is most concentrated in Montana, where its share of all jobs is 35.6 times the national share.
