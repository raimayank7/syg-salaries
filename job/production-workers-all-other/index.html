Production Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Production Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $40,110 a year ($19.29 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Production Workers, All Other is $40,110, which is 21% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 88 by median pay. With 251,700 jobs, it makes up about 16 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($44,380) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Production Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($109,470), Indiana ($48,260) and Maryland ($47,640). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($23,170), Arkansas ($36,030) and Rhode Island ($36,130). The most jobs were in California (28,090), Georgia (22,440) and Tennessee (20,150). The occupation is most concentrated in Tennessee, where its share of all jobs is 3.8 times the national share.
