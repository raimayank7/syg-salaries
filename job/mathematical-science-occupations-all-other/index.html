Mathematical Science Occupations, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Mathematical Science Occupations, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $81,490 a year ($39.18 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Mathematical Science Occupations, All Other is $81,490, which is 60% higher. Among the 21 occupations of the field Computer and Mathematical, it ranks number 19 by median pay. With 3,720 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($91,550) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Mathematical Science Occupations, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($180,500), North Carolina ($101,850) and Texas ($93,090). The lowest was in Oklahoma ($36,820), Maryland ($42,850) and Iowa ($49,020). The most jobs were in Illinois (1,320), Washington (320) and Virginia (180). The occupation is most concentrated in Alaska, where its share of all jobs is 11.2 times the national share.
