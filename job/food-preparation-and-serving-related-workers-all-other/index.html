Food Preparation and Serving Related Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Food Preparation and Serving Related Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $35,840 a year ($17.23 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Food Preparation and Serving Related Workers, All Other is $35,840, which is 30% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Food Preparation and Serving Related, it ranks number 8 by median pay. With 84,630 jobs, it makes up about 5.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Food Preparation and Serving Related Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Nebraska ($46,170), Hawaii ($43,540) and Oregon ($39,080). The lowest was in Guam ($21,600), West Virginia ($21,640) and Louisiana ($21,820). The most jobs were in California (30,730), North Carolina (4,670) and Texas (4,030). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 7.4 times the national share.
