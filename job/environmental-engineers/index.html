Environmental Engineers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Environmental Engineers in the United States earned a median wage of $107,110 a year ($51.50 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Research, design, plan, or perform engineering duties in the prevention, control, and remediation of environmental hazards using various engineering disciplines. Work may include waste treatment, site remediation, or pollution control technology.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 17-2081.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Environmental Engineers is $107,110, which is 110% higher. Among the 36 occupations of the field Architecture and Engineering, it ranks number 13 by median pay. With 38,340 jobs, it makes up about 2.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Environmental Engineers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Louisiana ($130,510), California ($130,410) and Oregon ($127,100). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($77,560), West Virginia ($83,200) and Arkansas ($85,280). The most jobs were in California (4,550), New York (3,170) and Texas (1,940). The occupation is most concentrated in Alaska, where its share of all jobs is 3.3 times the national share.
