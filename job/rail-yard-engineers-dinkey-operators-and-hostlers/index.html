Rail Yard Engineers, Dinkey Operators, and Hostlers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Rail Yard Engineers, Dinkey Operators, and Hostlers in the United States earned a median wage of $60,600 a year ($29.14 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Rail Yard Engineers, Dinkey Operators, and Hostlers is $60,600, which is 19% higher. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 17 by median pay. With 3,920 jobs, it makes up about 0.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($68,450) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Rail Yard Engineers, Dinkey Operators, and Hostlers pay by state

The highest median pay was in West Virginia ($91,380), Florida ($80,360) and New Jersey ($71,030). The lowest was in Arkansas ($38,980), Alabama ($48,900) and Nevada ($48,970). The most jobs were in California (190), Indiana (190) and New Jersey (130). The occupation is most concentrated in Indiana, where its share of all jobs is 2.3 times the national share.
