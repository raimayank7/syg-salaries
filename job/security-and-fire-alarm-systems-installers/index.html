Security and Fire Alarm Systems Installers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Security and Fire Alarm Systems Installers in the United States earned a median wage of $60,070 a year ($28.88 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Install, program, maintain, and repair security and fire alarm wiring and equipment. Ensure that work is in accordance with relevant codes. Excludes “Electricians” (47-2111) who do a broad range of electrical wiring.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 49-2098.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Security and Fire Alarm Systems Installers is $60,070, which is 18% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 27 by median pay. With 86,340 jobs, it makes up about 5.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Security and Fire Alarm Systems Installers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($79,870), Massachusetts ($78,530) and Rhode Island ($76,320). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($29,280), Guam ($37,450) and Mississippi ($47,160). The most jobs were in Texas (10,260), Florida (8,320) and California (8,180). The occupation is most concentrated in Florida, where its share of all jobs is 1.5 times the national share.
