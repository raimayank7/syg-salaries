Layout Workers, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Layout Workers, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $63,870 a year ($30.71 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Lay out reference points and dimensions on metal or plastic stock or workpieces, such as sheets, plates, tubes, structural shapes, castings, or machine parts, for further processing. Includes shipfitters.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-4192.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Layout Workers, Metal and Plastic is $63,870, which is 25% higher. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 5,970 jobs, it makes up about 0.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Layout Workers, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in Hawaii ($87,570), Washington ($85,300) and Kansas ($79,240). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($25,170), Alabama ($37,590) and New Mexico ($38,560). The most jobs were in Virginia (1,080), Kansas (610) and Washington (500). The occupation is most concentrated in Kansas, where its share of all jobs is 11.1 times the national share.
