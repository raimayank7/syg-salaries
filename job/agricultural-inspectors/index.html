Agricultural Inspectors: pay in the United States, May 2025

Agricultural Inspectors in the United States earned a median wage of $49,940 a year ($24.01 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Inspect agricultural commodities, processing equipment, and facilities, and fish and logging operations, to ensure compliance with regulations and laws governing health, quality, and safety.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 45-2011.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Agricultural Inspectors is $49,940, which is 2% lower. Among the 13 occupations of the field Farming, Fishing, and Forestry, it ranks number 5 by median pay. With 14,410 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Agricultural Inspectors pay by state

The highest median pay was in Minnesota ($77,150), New York ($73,920) and Ohio ($69,280). The lowest was in Wyoming ($36,670), Nevada ($39,270) and Alabama ($39,300). The most jobs were in California (2,340), Georgia (1,390) and Texas (590). The occupation is most concentrated in Wyoming, where its share of all jobs is 5.6 times the national share.
