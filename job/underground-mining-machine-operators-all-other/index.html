Underground Mining Machine Operators, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Underground Mining Machine Operators, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $70,130 a year ($33.72 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Underground Mining Machine Operators, All Other is $70,130, which is 38% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 9 by median pay. With 3,180 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Underground Mining Machine Operators, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($106,840), Montana ($84,410) and Minnesota ($83,120). The lowest was in Pennsylvania ($54,480), Nevada ($59,810) and Virginia ($61,990). The most jobs were in Minnesota (930), West Virginia (240) and Utah (180). The occupation is most concentrated in West Virginia, where its share of all jobs is 17.1 times the national share.
