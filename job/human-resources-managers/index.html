Human Resources Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Human Resources Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $149,280 a year ($71.77 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate human resources activities and staff of an organization. Excludes managers who primarily focus on compensation and benefits (11-3111) and training and development (11-3131).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-3121.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Human Resources Managers is $149,280, which is 193% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 7 by median pay. With 220,660 jobs, it makes up about 14 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($164,230) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Human Resources Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($184,350), Massachusetts ($181,780) and New York ($176,650). The lowest was in Guam ($77,180), Virgin Islands ($77,490) and Puerto Rico ($78,370). The most jobs were in California (27,450), Texas (23,220) and New York (17,920). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 2.3 times the national share.
