Managers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Managers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $141,900 a year ($68.22 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Managers, All Other is $141,900, which is 178% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 622,190 jobs, it makes up about 40 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Managers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($182,950), District of Columbia ($175,720) and California ($174,560). The lowest was in Indiana ($79,900), Puerto Rico ($82,250) and Arkansas ($87,520). The most jobs were in California (99,580), Texas (61,410) and Florida (37,820). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 6.6 times the national share.
