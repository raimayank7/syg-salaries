Neurologists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Neurologists in the United States earned a median wage of $248,560 a year ($119.50 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Neurologists is $248,560, which is 388% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 18 by median pay. With 10,590 jobs, it makes up about 0.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Neurologists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Vermont ($434,730), Arizona ($409,610) and Minnesota ($381,670). The lowest was in West Virginia ($63,500), New York ($155,990) and Indiana ($220,210). The most jobs were in New York (2,810), California (1,090) and Ohio (940). The occupation is most concentrated in Alaska, where its share of all jobs is 4.5 times the national share.
