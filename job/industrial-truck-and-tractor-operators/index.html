Industrial Truck and Tractor Operators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Industrial Truck and Tractor Operators in the United States earned a median wage of $46,420 a year ($22.32 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Operate industrial trucks or tractors equipped to move materials around a warehouse, storage yard, factory, construction site, or similar location. Excludes “Logging Equipment Operators” (45-4022).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 53-7051.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Industrial Truck and Tractor Operators is $46,420, which is 9% lower. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 32 by median pay. With 774,420 jobs, it makes up about 50 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Industrial Truck and Tractor Operators pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($83,720), New Mexico ($62,150) and Delaware ($60,350). The lowest was in Guam ($34,870), Virgin Islands ($35,230) and Puerto Rico ($36,090). The most jobs were in California (89,620), Texas (88,780) and Georgia (50,260). The occupation is most concentrated in Delaware, where its share of all jobs is 2.2 times the national share.
