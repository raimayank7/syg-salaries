Transit and Railroad Police: pay in the United States, May 2025

Transit and Railroad Police in the United States earned a median wage of $90,230 a year ($43.38 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Transit and Railroad Police is $90,230, which is 77% higher. Among the 24 occupations of the field Protective Service, it ranks number 4 by median pay. With 4,390 jobs, it makes up about 0.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Transit and Railroad Police pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($109,650), New Jersey ($106,290) and Maryland ($99,420). The lowest was in Georgia ($57,690), Missouri ($67,840) and Florida ($69,810). The most jobs were in New Jersey (340), Georgia (240) and California (100). The occupation is most concentrated in New Jersey, where its share of all jobs is 2.8 times the national share.
