Cardiovascular Technologists and Technicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Cardiovascular Technologists and Technicians in the United States earned a median wage of $74,310 a year ($35.73 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Conduct tests on pulmonary or cardiovascular systems of patients for diagnostic, therapeutic, or research purposes. May conduct or assist in electrocardiograms, cardiac catheterizations, pulmonary functions, lung capacity, and similar tests.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-2031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Cardiovascular Technologists and Technicians is $74,310, which is 46% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 51 by median pay. With 62,960 jobs, it makes up about 4.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Cardiovascular Technologists and Technicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Utah ($113,510), Hawaii ($111,730) and Massachusetts ($105,580). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($34,160), Mississippi ($38,000) and Oklahoma ($44,900). The most jobs were in Texas (6,140), Florida (5,640) and California (5,570). The occupation is most concentrated in Utah, where its share of all jobs is 1.8 times the national share.
