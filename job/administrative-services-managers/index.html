Administrative Services Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Administrative Services Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $114,130 a year ($54.87 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate one or more administrative services of an organization, such as records and information management, mail distribution, and other office support services. Medical records administrators are included in “Medical and Health Services Managers” (11-9111). Excludes “Facilities Managers” (11-3013) and “Purchasing Managers” (11-3061).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-3012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Administrative Services Managers is $114,130, which is 124% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 19 by median pay. With 263,960 jobs, it makes up about 17 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($129,870) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Administrative Services Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Colorado ($157,000), New Jersey ($144,460) and New York ($142,280). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($63,930), Guam ($64,320) and Virgin Islands ($74,990). The most jobs were in California (41,240), Texas (22,980) and Florida (15,350). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 10.9 times the national share.
