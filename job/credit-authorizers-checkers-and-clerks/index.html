Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks: pay in the United States, May 2025

Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks in the United States earned a median wage of $50,080 a year ($24.08 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Authorize credit charges against customers’ accounts. Investigate history and credit standing of individuals or business establishments applying for credit. May interview applicants to obtain personal and financial data, determine credit worthiness, process applications, and notify customers of acceptance or rejection of credit.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 43-4041.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks is $50,080, which is about the same. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 22 by median pay. With 12,030 jobs, it makes up about 0.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Credit Authorizers, Checkers, and Clerks pay by state

The highest median pay was in Texas ($62,930), Minnesota ($61,000) and Washington ($59,840). The lowest was in Georgia ($30,490), Puerto Rico ($33,700) and Alabama ($35,990). The most jobs were in Texas (1,180), Florida (930) and New York (730). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 2.6 times the national share.
