Editors: pay in the United States, May 2025

Editors in the United States earned a median wage of $77,920 a year ($37.46 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Editors is $77,920, which is 53% higher. Among the 41 occupations of the field Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media, it ranks number 8 by median pay. With 91,690 jobs, it makes up about 5.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($90,060) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Editors pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($101,480), California ($98,030) and Connecticut ($85,950). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($39,640), Texas ($45,950) and Nebraska ($46,320). The most jobs were in New York (16,800), California (14,830) and Texas (6,460). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 8.0 times the national share.
