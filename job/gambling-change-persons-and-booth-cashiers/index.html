Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers in the United States earned a median wage of $36,220 a year ($17.41 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Exchange coins, tokens, and chips for patrons’ money. May issue payoffs and obtain customer’s signature on receipt. May operate a booth in the slot machine area and furnish change persons with money bank at the start of the shift, or count and audit money in drawers. Excludes “Cashiers” (41-2011).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 41-2012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers is $36,220, which is 29% lower. Among the 22 occupations of the field Sales and Related, it ranks number 19 by median pay. With 21,530 jobs, it makes up about 1.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Gambling Change Persons and Booth Cashiers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Arizona ($63,400), Texas ($62,400) and North Dakota ($49,830). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,050), West Virginia ($22,080) and Louisiana ($23,390). The most jobs were in California (4,360), Nevada (3,080) and Michigan (1,210). The occupation is most concentrated in Nevada, where its share of all jobs is 14.3 times the national share.
