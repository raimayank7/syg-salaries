Merchandise Displayers and Window Trimmers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Merchandise Displayers and Window Trimmers in the United States earned a median wage of $39,390 a year ($18.94 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Merchandise Displayers and Window Trimmers is $39,390, which is 23% lower. Among the 41 occupations of the field Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media, it ranks number 39 by median pay. With 165,220 jobs, it makes up about 11 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Merchandise Displayers and Window Trimmers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($48,780), California ($47,010) and Alaska ($46,530). The lowest was in Virgin Islands ($27,520), Puerto Rico ($29,400) and Mississippi ($33,040). The most jobs were in California (17,840), Texas (14,730) and Florida (14,360). The occupation is most concentrated in Virgin Islands, where its share of all jobs is 3.8 times the national share.
