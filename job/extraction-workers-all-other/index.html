Extraction Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Extraction Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $57,010 a year ($27.41 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Extraction Workers, All Other is $57,010, which is 12% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 33 by median pay. With 5,540 jobs, it makes up about 0.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Extraction Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Pennsylvania ($79,520), Colorado ($77,890) and Idaho ($71,050). The lowest was in Ohio ($44,550), Utah ($45,140) and Georgia ($45,760). The most jobs were in Louisiana (1,650), Texas (1,070) and West Virginia (470). The occupation is most concentrated in Wyoming, where its share of all jobs is 36.9 times the national share.
