Speech-Language Pathologists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Speech-Language Pathologists in the United States earned a median wage of $97,870 a year ($47.05 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Assess and treat persons with speech, language, voice, and fluency disorders. May select alternative communication systems and teach their use. May perform research related to speech and language problems.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1127.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Speech-Language Pathologists is $97,870, which is 92% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 39 by median pay. With 183,390 jobs, it makes up about 12 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Speech-Language Pathologists pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($118,970), Colorado ($110,750) and District of Columbia ($109,380). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($49,800), South Dakota ($65,680) and Louisiana ($69,610). The most jobs were in Texas (19,120), California (16,930) and New York (15,310). The occupation is most concentrated in Arkansas, where its share of all jobs is 1.6 times the national share.
