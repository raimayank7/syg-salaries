Furniture Finishers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Furniture Finishers in the United States earned a median wage of $44,540 a year ($21.41 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Furniture Finishers is $44,540, which is 13% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 69 by median pay. With 14,480 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Furniture Finishers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Connecticut ($59,830), Massachusetts ($56,300) and New Hampshire ($56,050). The lowest was in Tennessee ($32,160), Alabama ($34,950) and Texas ($36,350). The most jobs were in Florida (1,470), Indiana (1,280) and California (1,010). The occupation is most concentrated in Indiana, where its share of all jobs is 4.3 times the national share.
