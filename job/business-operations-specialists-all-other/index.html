Business Operations Specialists, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $83,050 a year ($39.93 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Business Operations Specialists, All Other is $83,050, which is 63% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 11 by median pay. With 1,087,090 jobs, it makes up about 70 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($93,970) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Business Operations Specialists, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($108,160), Maryland ($101,500) and Washington ($100,360). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($46,700), Arkansas ($61,240) and Louisiana ($63,220). The most jobs were in California (155,000), Texas (104,270) and Georgia (67,450). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 7.7 times the national share.
