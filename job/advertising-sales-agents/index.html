Advertising Sales Agents: pay in the United States, May 2025

Advertising Sales Agents in the United States earned a median wage of $64,820 a year ($31.16 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Advertising Sales Agents is $64,820, which is 27% higher. Among the 22 occupations of the field Sales and Related, it ranks number 8 by median pay. With 91,700 jobs, it makes up about 5.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($80,670) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Advertising Sales Agents pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($101,320), New York ($98,360) and Massachusetts ($80,500). The lowest was in Tennessee ($40,910), Alaska ($43,840) and Puerto Rico ($44,160). The most jobs were in New York (13,570), California (9,620) and Florida (6,420). The occupation is most concentrated in New York, where its share of all jobs is 2.4 times the national share.
