Rail Transportation Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Rail Transportation Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $56,360 a year ($27.10 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Rail Transportation Workers, All Other is $56,360, which is 11% higher. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 26 by median pay. With 1,120 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Rail Transportation Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($86,650), Georgia ($78,500) and California ($60,360). The lowest was in Texas ($43,150), Tennessee ($48,660) and California ($60,360). The most jobs were in California (230), Texas (120) and Tennessee (110). The occupation is most concentrated in Tennessee, where its share of all jobs is 4.8 times the national share.
