Gambling Service Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Gambling Service Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $36,310 a year ($17.46 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Gambling Service Workers, All Other is $36,310, which is 29% lower. Among the 32 occupations of the field Personal Care and Service, it ranks number 20 by median pay. With 14,140 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($41,380) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Gambling Service Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Iowa ($52,260), Maryland ($49,490) and Mississippi ($48,100). The lowest was in West Virginia ($19,030), Puerto Rico ($23,060) and Montana ($23,400). The most jobs were in Minnesota (2,960), California (1,650) and Nevada (1,650). The occupation is most concentrated in Alaska, where its share of all jobs is 23.7 times the national share.
