Food and Tobacco Roasting, Baking, and Drying Machine Operators and Tenders: pay in the United States, May 2025

Food and Tobacco Roasting, Baking, and Drying Machine Operators and Tenders in the United States earned a median wage of $44,810 a year ($21.54 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Operate or tend food or tobacco roasting, baking, or drying equipment, including hearth ovens, kiln driers, roasters, char kilns, and vacuum drying equipment.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Food and Tobacco Roasting, Baking, and Drying Machine Operators and Tenders is $44,810, which is 12% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 67 by median pay. With 20,370 jobs, it makes up about 1.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Food and Tobacco Roasting, Baking, and Drying Machine Operators and Tenders pay by state

The highest median pay was in North Dakota ($77,300), Maryland ($60,170) and Colorado ($59,590). The lowest was in Florida ($35,340), Montana ($35,360) and New Mexico ($35,480). The most jobs were in Texas (1,800), California (1,630) and North Carolina (1,510). The occupation is most concentrated in Idaho, where its share of all jobs is 3.8 times the national share.
