Occupational Health and Safety Specialists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Occupational Health and Safety Specialists in the United States earned a median wage of $90,150 a year ($43.34 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Review, evaluate, and analyze work environments and design programs and procedures to control, eliminate, and prevent disease or injury caused by chemical, physical, and biological agents or ergonomic factors. May conduct inspections and enforce adherence to laws and regulations governing the health and safety of individuals. May be employed in the public or private sector.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 19-5011.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Occupational Health and Safety Specialists is $90,150, which is 77% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 23 by median pay. With 140,610 jobs, it makes up about 9.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Occupational Health and Safety Specialists pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($111,530), Massachusetts ($103,970) and Washington ($102,920). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($45,630), Guam ($68,030) and Arkansas ($73,130). The most jobs were in Texas (17,920), California (16,230) and New York (6,840). The occupation is most concentrated in Wyoming, where its share of all jobs is 2.3 times the national share.
