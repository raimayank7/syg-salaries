Commercial and Industrial Designers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Commercial and Industrial Designers in the United States earned a median wage of $83,910 a year ($40.34 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Design and develop manufactured products, such as cars, home appliances, and children’s toys. Combine artistic talent with research on product use, marketing, and materials to create the most functional and appealing product design.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 27-1021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Commercial and Industrial Designers is $83,910, which is 65% higher. Among the 41 occupations of the field Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media, it ranks number 6 by median pay. With 33,490 jobs, it makes up about 2.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($93,260) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Commercial and Industrial Designers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Kansas ($104,870), Michigan ($102,160) and Massachusetts ($101,720). The lowest was in South Dakota ($60,110), Utah ($62,620) and New Mexico ($62,710). The most jobs were in California (5,300), Michigan (2,930) and New York (2,400). The occupation is most concentrated in Michigan, where its share of all jobs is 3.1 times the national share.
