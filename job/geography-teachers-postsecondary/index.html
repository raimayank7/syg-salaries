Geography Teachers, Postsecondary: pay in the United States, May 2025

Geography Teachers, Postsecondary in the United States earned a median wage of $97,590 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Teach courses in geography. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 25-1064.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Geography Teachers, Postsecondary is $97,590, which is 91% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 3,330 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Geography Teachers, Postsecondary pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($146,040), Oregon ($113,500) and Vermont ($107,790). The lowest was in Nebraska ($72,300), Mississippi ($75,130) and New Jersey ($78,540). The most jobs were in California (280), Pennsylvania (220) and New York (200). The occupation is most concentrated in Vermont, where its share of all jobs is 6.2 times the national share.
