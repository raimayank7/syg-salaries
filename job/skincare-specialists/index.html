Skincare Specialists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Skincare Specialists in the United States earned a median wage of $45,330 a year ($21.79 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Provide skincare treatments to face and body to enhance an individual’s appearance. Includes electrologists and laser hair removal specialists.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 39-5094.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Skincare Specialists is $45,330, which is 11% lower. Among the 32 occupations of the field Personal Care and Service, it ranks number 9 by median pay. With 72,890 jobs, it makes up about 4.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($51,850) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Skincare Specialists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($79,320), District of Columbia ($77,480) and Wyoming ($62,400). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($23,440), Idaho ($34,130) and Arkansas ($36,520). The most jobs were in California (11,360), Texas (6,160) and Florida (5,270). The occupation is most concentrated in Nevada, where its share of all jobs is 2.1 times the national share.
