Drafters, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Drafters, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $63,520 a year ($30.54 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Drafters, All Other is $63,520, which is 25% higher. Among the 36 occupations of the field Architecture and Engineering, it ranks number 34 by median pay. With 14,470 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Drafters, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Jersey ($77,840), California ($73,390) and Wisconsin ($73,040). The lowest was in Alabama ($47,320), Arkansas ($48,020) and Oklahoma ($49,920). The most jobs were in Texas (2,450), California (1,750) and Louisiana (1,200). The occupation is most concentrated in Louisiana, where its share of all jobs is 6.6 times the national share.
