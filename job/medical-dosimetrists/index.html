Medical Dosimetrists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Medical Dosimetrists in the United States earned a median wage of $147,470 a year ($70.90 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Generate radiation treatment plans, develop radiation dose calculations, communicate and supervise the treatment plan implementation, and consult with members of radiation oncology team.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-2036.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Medical Dosimetrists is $147,470, which is 189% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 25 by median pay. With 3,410 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Medical Dosimetrists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($189,280), Oregon ($183,990) and New Jersey ($170,650). The lowest was in West Virginia ($45,350), Mississippi ($115,490) and Missouri ($129,610). The most jobs were in Texas (330), Florida (320) and New York (300). The occupation is most concentrated in West Virginia, where its share of all jobs is 3.7 times the national share.
