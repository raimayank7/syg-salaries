Dancers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Dancers in the United States earned a median wage of $24.80 an hour in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $24.51 an hour. The median for Dancers is $24.80, which is about the same. Among the 41 occupations of the field Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media, it ranks number 32 by median pay. With 8,130 jobs, it makes up about 0.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Dancers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Nevada ($38.75), Texas ($35.31) and Oregon ($30.80). The lowest was in Minnesota ($16.75), Puerto Rico ($20.24) and Florida ($20.47). The most jobs were in California (1,870), New York (1,170) and Hawaii (480). The occupation is most concentrated in Hawaii, where its share of all jobs is 14.8 times the national share.
