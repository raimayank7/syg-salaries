Helpers, Construction Trades, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Helpers, Construction Trades, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $42,670 a year ($20.51 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Helpers, Construction Trades, All Other is $42,670, which is 16% lower. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 58 by median pay. With 24,770 jobs, it makes up about 1.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Helpers, Construction Trades, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($61,750), New York ($54,480) and Missouri ($51,630). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,910), Utah ($28,120) and Hawaii ($30,900). The most jobs were in Texas (5,060), California (2,990) and Florida (2,280). The occupation is most concentrated in Louisiana, where its share of all jobs is 7.1 times the national share.
