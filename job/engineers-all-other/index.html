Engineers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Engineers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $122,930 a year ($59.10 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Engineers, All Other is $122,930, which is 141% higher. Among the 36 occupations of the field Architecture and Engineering, it ranks number 7 by median pay. With 154,070 jobs, it makes up about 9.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Engineers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Mexico ($162,070), Alabama ($152,550) and District of Columbia ($151,920). The lowest was in Kansas ($76,100), Arkansas ($76,940) and Puerto Rico ($81,370). The most jobs were in California (28,610), Texas (10,820) and Florida (8,600). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 5.1 times the national share.
