Web and Digital Interface Designers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Web and Digital Interface Designers in the United States earned a median wage of $104,000 a year ($50.00 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Design digital user interfaces or websites. Develop and test layouts, interfaces, functionality, and navigation menus to ensure compatibility and usability across browsers or devices. May use web framework applications as well as client-side code and processes. May evaluate web design following web and accessibility standards, and may analyze web use metrics and optimize websites for marketability and search engine ranking. May design and test interfaces that facilitate the human-computer interaction and maximize the usability of digital devices, websites, and software with a focus on aesthetics and design. May create graphics used in websites and manage website content and links. Excludes “Special Effects Artists and Animators” (27-1014) and “Graphic Designers” (27-1024).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 15-1255.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Web and Digital Interface Designers is $104,000, which is 104% higher. Among the 21 occupations of the field Computer and Mathematical, it ranks number 14 by median pay. With 113,330 jobs, it makes up about 7.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($117,490) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Web and Digital Interface Designers pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($136,110), Washington ($130,480) and New York ($120,990). The lowest was in Guam ($31,200), Mississippi ($48,550) and Puerto Rico ($48,700). The most jobs were in California (27,090), New York (16,560) and Washington (11,580). The occupation is most concentrated in Washington, where its share of all jobs is 4.5 times the national share.
