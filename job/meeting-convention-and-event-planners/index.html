Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners: pay in the United States, May 2025

Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners in the United States earned a median wage of $61,160 a year ($29.41 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners is $61,160, which is 20% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 29 by median pay. With 142,860 jobs, it makes up about 9.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Meeting, Convention, and Event Planners pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($76,970), Massachusetts ($75,740) and New York ($74,730). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($28,400), West Virginia ($35,760) and Mississippi ($44,680). The most jobs were in California (20,650), Texas (10,730) and Florida (10,480). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 3.7 times the national share.
