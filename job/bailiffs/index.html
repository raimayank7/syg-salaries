Bailiffs: pay in the United States, May 2025

Bailiffs in the United States earned a median wage of $56,600 a year ($27.21 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Bailiffs is $56,600, which is 11% higher. Among the 24 occupations of the field Protective Service, it ranks number 14 by median pay. With 17,310 jobs, it makes up about 1.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Bailiffs pay by state

The highest median pay was in Connecticut ($111,890), Alaska ($90,600) and Massachusetts ($86,730). The lowest was in North Dakota ($27,560), Kentucky ($30,760) and South Carolina ($34,280). The most jobs were in New York (3,830), Ohio (1,640) and Illinois (960). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 7.9 times the national share.
