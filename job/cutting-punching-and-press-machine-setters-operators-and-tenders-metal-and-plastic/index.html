Cutting, Punching, and Press Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Cutting, Punching, and Press Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $46,330 a year ($22.27 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up, operate, or tend machines to saw, cut, shear, slit, punch, crimp, notch, bend, or straighten metal or plastic material.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Cutting, Punching, and Press Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic is $46,330, which is 9% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 61 by median pay. With 171,200 jobs, it makes up about 11 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Cutting, Punching, and Press Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oregon ($53,680), Maine ($51,820) and Washington ($51,160). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($24,090), New Mexico ($39,430) and Florida ($39,440). The most jobs were in Michigan (22,060), Ohio (13,350) and Texas (13,290). The occupation is most concentrated in Michigan, where its share of all jobs is 4.5 times the national share.
