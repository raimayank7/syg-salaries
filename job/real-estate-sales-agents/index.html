Real Estate Sales Agents: pay in the United States, May 2025

Real Estate Sales Agents in the United States earned a median wage of $52,830 a year ($25.40 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Rent, buy, or sell property for clients. Perform duties such as study property listings, interview prospective clients, accompany clients to property site, discuss conditions of sale, and draw up real estate contracts. Includes agents who represent buyer.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 41-9022.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Real Estate Sales Agents is $52,830, which is 4% higher. Among the 22 occupations of the field Sales and Related, it ranks number 10 by median pay. With 193,370 jobs, it makes up about 12 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($69,510) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Real Estate Sales Agents pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($123,770), New York ($102,990) and New Jersey ($99,990). The lowest was in Arkansas ($38,180), Mississippi ($39,550) and Kansas ($39,770). The most jobs were in Florida (26,790), Texas (22,250) and California (17,500). The occupation is most concentrated in Florida, where its share of all jobs is 2.2 times the national share.
