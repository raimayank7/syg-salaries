Career/Technical Education Teachers, Postsecondary: pay in the United States, May 2025

Career/Technical Education Teachers, Postsecondary in the United States earned a median wage of $63,820 a year ($30.68 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Teach vocational courses intended to provide occupational training below the baccalaureate level in subjects such as construction, mechanics/repair, manufacturing, transportation, or cosmetology, primarily to students who have graduated from or left high school. Teaching takes place in public or private schools whose primary business is academic or vocational education. Excludes “Training and Development Specialists” (13-1151), “Adult Basic Education, Adult Secondary Education, and English as a Second Language Instructors” (25-3011), and postsecondary teachers classified elsewhere in the 25-1000 minor group. Flight instructors are included with “Aircraft Pilots and Flight Engineers” (53-2010).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 25-1194.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Career/Technical Education Teachers, Postsecondary is $63,820, which is 25% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 51 by median pay. With 114,110 jobs, it makes up about 7.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($72,060) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Career/Technical Education Teachers, Postsecondary pay by state

The highest median pay was in Wisconsin ($82,620), South Carolina ($79,610) and Massachusetts ($78,780). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($42,470), Kansas ($48,790) and Arkansas ($50,120). The most jobs were in Texas (12,620), California (11,040) and Florida (7,600). The occupation is most concentrated in Alaska, where its share of all jobs is 4.7 times the national share.
