Athletic Trainers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Athletic Trainers in the United States earned a median wage of $62,520 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Evaluate and treat musculoskeletal injuries or illnesses. Provide preventive, therapeutic, emergency, and rehabilitative care. Excludes “Physical Therapists” (29-1123).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-9091.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Athletic Trainers is $62,520, which is 23% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 59 by median pay. With 30,500 jobs, it makes up about 2.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Athletic Trainers pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($78,490), Hawaii ($76,320) and New Jersey ($76,210). The lowest was in Alabama ($46,940), West Virginia ($50,510) and Iowa ($55,430). The most jobs were in Texas (3,130), Ohio (2,100) and Pennsylvania (1,870). The occupation is most concentrated in Vermont, where its share of all jobs is 2.3 times the national share.
