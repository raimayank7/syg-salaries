Gambling Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Gambling Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $93,220 a year ($44.82 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Gambling Managers is $93,220, which is 83% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 28 by median pay. With 5,030 jobs, it makes up about 0.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($103,050) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Gambling Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Texas ($106,670), Washington ($106,450) and Kentucky ($105,340). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($54,640), Nebraska ($61,360) and West Virginia ($74,270). The most jobs were in Nevada (790), California (620) and Illinois (310). The occupation is most concentrated in Nevada, where its share of all jobs is 15.7 times the national share.
