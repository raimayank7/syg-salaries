Lathe and Turning Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Lathe and Turning Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $50,620 a year ($24.34 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up, operate, or tend lathe and turning machines to turn, bore, thread, form, or face metal or plastic materials, such as wire, rod, or bar stock.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-4034.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Lathe and Turning Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic is $50,620, which is about the same. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 33 by median pay. With 16,710 jobs, it makes up about 1.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Lathe and Turning Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in Colorado ($71,670), Maine ($64,800) and Minnesota ($61,000). The lowest was in Arkansas ($39,060), Tennessee ($40,730) and Oregon ($41,640). The most jobs were in California (2,600), Texas (2,150) and Michigan (1,540). The occupation is most concentrated in Connecticut, where its share of all jobs is 3.8 times the national share.
