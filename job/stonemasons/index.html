Stonemasons: pay in the United States, May 2025

Stonemasons in the United States earned a median wage of $57,390 a year ($27.59 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Build stone structures, such as piers, walls, and abutments. Lay walks, curbstones, or special types of masonry for vats, tanks, and floors.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 47-2022.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Stonemasons is $57,390, which is 13% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 30 by median pay. With 7,820 jobs, it makes up about 0.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Stonemasons pay by state

The highest median pay was in Wisconsin ($82,740), Nevada ($79,600) and Washington ($78,010). The lowest was in Mississippi ($40,700), Oklahoma ($40,930) and Arizona ($42,130). The most jobs were in California (1,250), Texas (420) and Washington (350). The occupation is most concentrated in Maine, where its share of all jobs is 7.3 times the national share.
