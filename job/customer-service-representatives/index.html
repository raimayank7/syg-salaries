Customer Service Representatives: pay in the United States, May 2025

Customer Service Representatives in the United States earned a median wage of $44,770 a year ($21.53 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Interact with customers to provide basic or scripted information in response to routine inquiries about products and services. May handle and resolve general complaints. Excludes individuals whose duties are primarily installation, sales, repair, and technical support.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 43-4051.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Customer Service Representatives is $44,770, which is 12% lower. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 41 by median pay. With 2,595,750 jobs, it makes up about 167 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Customer Service Representatives pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($50,330), California ($49,560) and Massachusetts ($49,120). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($26,700), Guam ($30,350) and Mississippi ($36,380). The most jobs were in Texas (337,170), Florida (228,000) and California (176,850). The occupation is most concentrated in Arizona, where its share of all jobs is 1.6 times the national share.
