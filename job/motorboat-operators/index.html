Motorboat Operators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Motorboat Operators in the United States earned a median wage of $47,520 a year ($22.85 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Motorboat Operators is $47,520, which is 7% lower. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 31 by median pay. With 2,480 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($56,270) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Motorboat Operators pay by state

The highest median pay was in Hawaii ($76,250), Illinois ($68,380) and Washington ($63,650). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($26,630), California ($34,570) and Tennessee ($42,360). The most jobs were in Hawaii (330), Texas (270) and California (190). The occupation is most concentrated in Hawaii, where its share of all jobs is 32.6 times the national share.
