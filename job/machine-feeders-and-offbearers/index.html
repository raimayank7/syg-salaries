Machine Feeders and Offbearers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Machine Feeders and Offbearers in the United States earned a median wage of $41,220 a year ($19.82 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Machine Feeders and Offbearers is $41,220, which is 19% lower. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 38 by median pay. With 42,330 jobs, it makes up about 2.7 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Machine Feeders and Offbearers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Wisconsin ($55,120), Wyoming ($51,280) and Minnesota ($48,740). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,400), Maine ($32,310) and Rhode Island ($32,770). The most jobs were in California (4,830), Texas (3,100) and Ohio (2,930). The occupation is most concentrated in South Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 5.3 times the national share.
