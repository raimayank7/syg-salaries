Multiple Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Multiple Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $47,180 a year ($22.68 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Multiple Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic is $47,180, which is 7% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 53 by median pay. With 124,590 jobs, it makes up about 8.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Multiple Machine Tool Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in West Virginia ($76,080), Kentucky ($54,960) and Missouri ($53,160). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($28,770), Mississippi ($36,330) and Rhode Island ($38,720). The most jobs were in Ohio (11,170), Texas (10,540) and Michigan (9,350). The occupation is most concentrated in Iowa, where its share of all jobs is 3.6 times the national share.
