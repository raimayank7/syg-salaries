Purchasing Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Purchasing Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $148,080 a year ($71.19 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate the activities of buyers, purchasing officers, and related workers involved in purchasing materials, products, and services. Includes wholesale or retail trade merchandising managers and procurement managers.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-3061.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Purchasing Managers is $148,080, which is 190% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 10 by median pay. With 84,320 jobs, it makes up about 5.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Purchasing Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($175,720), New Jersey ($174,520) and Colorado ($171,250). The lowest was in Guam ($80,290), Puerto Rico ($89,600) and Louisiana ($109,630). The most jobs were in Texas (9,640), California (7,470) and New York (6,930). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.
