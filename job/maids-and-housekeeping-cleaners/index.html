Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners: pay in the United States, May 2025

Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners in the United States earned a median wage of $35,510 a year ($17.07 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Perform any combination of light cleaning duties to maintain private households or commercial establishments, such as hotels and hospitals, in a clean and orderly manner. Duties may include making beds, replenishing linens, cleaning rooms and halls, and vacuuming.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 37-2012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners is $35,510, which is 30% lower. Among the 10 occupations of the field Building and Grounds Cleaning and Maintenance, it ranks number 10 by median pay. With 860,670 jobs, it makes up about 55 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Maids and Housekeeping Cleaners pay by state

The highest median pay was in Hawaii ($51,600), Nevada ($46,550) and California ($42,660). The lowest was in Guam ($21,470), Puerto Rico ($22,550) and Mississippi ($25,360). The most jobs were in California (103,430), Florida (79,550) and Texas (63,110). The occupation is most concentrated in Virgin Islands, where its share of all jobs is 3.3 times the national share.
