Avionics Technicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Avionics Technicians in the United States earned a median wage of $82,280 a year ($39.56 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Install, inspect, test, adjust, or repair avionics equipment, such as radar, radio, navigation, and missile control systems in aircraft or space vehicles.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 49-2091.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Avionics Technicians is $82,280, which is 61% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 5 by median pay. With 18,830 jobs, it makes up about 1.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Avionics Technicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Pennsylvania ($117,470), New Jersey ($100,250) and Maryland ($97,490). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($37,660), Kentucky ($42,440) and Arkansas ($54,160). The most jobs were in Washington (3,400), Florida (2,400) and Georgia (1,820). The occupation is most concentrated in Washington, where its share of all jobs is 7.9 times the national share.
