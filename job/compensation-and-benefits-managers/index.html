Compensation and Benefits Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Compensation and Benefits Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $149,230 a year ($71.75 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate compensation and benefits activities of an organization. Job analysis and position description managers are included in “Human Resources Managers” (11-3121).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-3111.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Compensation and Benefits Managers is $149,230, which is 193% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 8 by median pay. With 22,940 jobs, it makes up about 1.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Compensation and Benefits Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($199,190), Connecticut ($197,970) and Massachusetts ($184,070). The lowest was in Mississippi ($89,250), Idaho ($90,020) and Puerto Rico ($97,500). The most jobs were in California (2,970), New York (2,260) and Texas (2,230). The occupation is most concentrated in Connecticut, where its share of all jobs is 1.9 times the national share.

How much do Compensation and Benefits Managers earn in the United States?
