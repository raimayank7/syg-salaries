Communications Equipment Operators, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Communications Equipment Operators, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $54,680 a year ($26.29 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Communications Equipment Operators, All Other is $54,680, which is 7% higher. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 11 by median pay. With 1,200 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($62,130) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Communications Equipment Operators, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Missouri ($87,030), California ($79,450) and Texas ($58,410). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($21,840), North Carolina ($46,430) and Ohio ($47,360). The most jobs were in California (450), Puerto Rico (240) and Texas (110). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 33.3 times the national share.
