Education Administrators, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $95,200 a year ($45.77 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Education Administrators, All Other is $95,200, which is 87% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 26 by median pay. With 55,130 jobs, it makes up about 3.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Education Administrators, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($140,670), Guam ($129,480) and Maryland ($125,500). The lowest was in Montana ($68,580), Idaho ($71,690) and Hawaii ($75,130). The most jobs were in California (11,480), Texas (4,450) and New York (3,060). The occupation is most concentrated in Guam, where its share of all jobs is 8.1 times the national share.
