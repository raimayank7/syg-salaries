Orderlies: pay in the United States, May 2025

Orderlies in the United States earned a median wage of $38,290 a year ($18.41 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Transport patients to areas such as operating rooms or x-ray rooms using wheelchairs, stretchers, or moveable beds. May maintain stocks of supplies or clean and transport equipment. Excludes “Nursing Assistants” (31-1131). Psychiatric orderlies are included in “Psychiatric Aides” (31-1133).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 31-1132.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Orderlies is $38,290, which is 25% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Healthcare Support, it ranks number 13 by median pay. With 52,440 jobs, it makes up about 3.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Orderlies pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($49,920), Washington ($48,690) and Vermont ($48,120). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,330), Louisiana ($27,330) and Mississippi ($29,790). The most jobs were in California (4,870), New York (4,680) and Florida (4,440). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 2.0 times the national share.
