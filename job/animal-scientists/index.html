Animal Scientists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Animal Scientists in the United States earned a median wage of $68,940 a year ($33.14 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Animal Scientists is $68,940, which is 35% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 37 by median pay. With 3,100 jobs, it makes up about 0.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($88,350) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Animal Scientists pay by state

The highest median pay was in Texas ($132,830), Minnesota ($88,640) and Wisconsin ($84,310). The lowest was in Illinois ($47,930), Nebraska ($55,300) and Michigan ($60,010). The most jobs were in Wisconsin (280), Nebraska (210) and Maryland (190). The occupation is most concentrated in Nebraska, where its share of all jobs is 10.5 times the national share.

How much do Animal Scientists earn in the United States?
