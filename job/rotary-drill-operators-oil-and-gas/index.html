Rotary Drill Operators, Oil and Gas: pay in the United States, May 2025

Rotary Drill Operators, Oil and Gas in the United States earned a median wage of $67,890 a year ($32.64 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up or operate a variety of drills to remove underground oil and gas, or remove core samples for testing during oil and gas exploration. Excludes “Earth Drillers, Except Oil and Gas” (47-5023).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 47-5012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Rotary Drill Operators, Oil and Gas is $67,890, which is 33% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 12,600 jobs, it makes up about 0.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Rotary Drill Operators, Oil and Gas pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($85,470), New Mexico ($82,780) and Colorado ($79,830). The lowest was in Arkansas ($37,700), Kentucky ($38,520) and Louisiana ($49,410). The most jobs were in Texas (5,980), Oklahoma (1,700) and Louisiana (910). The occupation is most concentrated in Oklahoma, where its share of all jobs is 12.3 times the national share.
