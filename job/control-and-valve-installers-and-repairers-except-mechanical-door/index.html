Control and Valve Installers and Repairers, Except Mechanical Door: pay in the United States, May 2025

Control and Valve Installers and Repairers, Except Mechanical Door in the United States earned a median wage of $74,340 a year ($35.74 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Install, repair, and maintain mechanical regulating and controlling devices, such as electric meters, gas regulators, thermostats, safety and flow valves, and other mechanical governors.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 49-9012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Control and Valve Installers and Repairers, Except Mechanical Door is $74,340, which is 46% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 8 by median pay. With 48,240 jobs, it makes up about 3.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Control and Valve Installers and Repairers, Except Mechanical Door pay by state

The highest median pay was in Rhode Island ($110,900), Alaska ($109,450) and Connecticut ($104,690). The lowest was in Mississippi ($48,670), South Carolina ($50,800) and Puerto Rico ($52,660). The most jobs were in Texas (11,630), California (4,310) and New York (4,170). The occupation is most concentrated in Wyoming, where its share of all jobs is 3.4 times the national share.
