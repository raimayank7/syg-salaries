Electrical and Electronics Repairers, Commercial and Industrial Equipment: pay in the United States, May 2025

Electrical and Electronics Repairers, Commercial and Industrial Equipment in the United States earned a median wage of $74,090 a year ($35.62 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Repair, test, adjust, or install electronic equipment, such as industrial controls, transmitters, and antennas. Excludes “Avionics Technicians” (49-2091), “Electrical and Electronics Installers and Repairers, Transportation Equipment” (49-2093), and “Electronic Equipment Installers and Repairers, Motor Vehicles” (49-2096).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 49-2094.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Electrical and Electronics Repairers, Commercial and Industrial Equipment is $74,090, which is 45% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 10 by median pay. With 65,010 jobs, it makes up about 4.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Electrical and Electronics Repairers, Commercial and Industrial Equipment pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($99,200), North Dakota ($98,700) and Alaska ($96,390). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,580), Illinois ($59,950) and Missouri ($60,950). The most jobs were in Texas (13,540), California (5,830) and Florida (3,650). The occupation is most concentrated in North Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.
