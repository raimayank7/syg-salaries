Architecture Teachers, Postsecondary: pay in the United States, May 2025

Architecture Teachers, Postsecondary in the United States earned a median wage of $96,870 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Teach courses in architecture and architectural design, such as architectural environmental design, interior architecture/design, and landscape architecture. Includes both teachers primarily engaged in teaching and those who do a combination of teaching and research.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 25-1031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Architecture Teachers, Postsecondary is $96,870, which is 90% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 14 by median pay. With 7,700 jobs, it makes up about 0.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Architecture Teachers, Postsecondary pay by state

The highest median pay was in Puerto Rico ($126,700), New York ($126,430) and Connecticut ($122,880). The lowest was in New Mexico ($69,820), South Carolina ($76,900) and Nebraska ($77,540). The most jobs were in New York (1,460), Texas (770) and California (530). The occupation is most concentrated in Rhode Island, where its share of all jobs is 4.1 times the national share.
