Carpet Installers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Carpet Installers in the United States earned a median wage of $50,340 a year ($24.20 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Lay and install carpet from rolls or blocks on floors. Install padding and trim flooring materials. Excludes “Floor Layers, Except Carpet, Wood, and Hard Tiles” (47-2042).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 47-2041.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Carpet Installers is $50,340, which is about the same. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 41 by median pay. With 13,780 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($56,990) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Carpet Installers pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Jersey ($115,180), Minnesota ($85,910) and Alaska ($76,790). The lowest was in South Carolina ($33,810), West Virginia ($36,220) and Mississippi ($36,590). The most jobs were in California (2,270), Texas (810) and Nevada (710). The occupation is most concentrated in Nevada, where its share of all jobs is 5.2 times the national share.
