Roof Bolters, Mining in the United States earned a median wage of $78,540 a year ($37.76 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Roof Bolters, Mining is $78,540, which is 54% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 3 by median pay. With 2,160 jobs, it makes up about 0.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Roof Bolters, Mining pay by state

The highest median pay was in Wyoming ($116,570), Illinois ($81,860) and Colorado ($81,270). The lowest was in Alabama ($67,020), Kentucky ($72,510) and Virginia ($78,260). The most jobs were in West Virginia (970), Illinois (200) and Kentucky (120). The occupation is most concentrated in West Virginia, where its share of all jobs is 99.8 times the national share.
