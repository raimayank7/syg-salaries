Marketing Managers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Marketing Managers in the United States earned a median wage of $166,790 a year ($80.19 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, direct, or coordinate marketing policies and programs, such as determining the demand for products and services offered by a firm and its competitors, and identify potential customers. Develop pricing strategies with the goal of maximizing the firm’s profits or share of the market while ensuring the firm’s customers are satisfied. Oversee product development or monitor trends that indicate the need for new products and services.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 11-2021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Marketing Managers is $166,790, which is 227% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 5 by median pay. With 395,240 jobs, it makes up about 25 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Marketing Managers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Massachusetts ($212,020), California ($193,620) and Virginia ($187,820). The lowest was in Guam ($79,890), Virgin Islands ($97,760) and West Virginia ($98,750). The most jobs were in California (54,660), New York (54,000) and Texas (44,680). The occupation is most concentrated in New York, where its share of all jobs is 2.2 times the national share.
