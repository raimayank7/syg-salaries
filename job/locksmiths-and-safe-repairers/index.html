Locksmiths and Safe Repairers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Locksmiths and Safe Repairers in the United States earned a median wage of $51,320 a year ($24.68 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Locksmiths and Safe Repairers is $51,320, which is about the same. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 36 by median pay. With 15,040 jobs, it makes up about 1.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Locksmiths and Safe Repairers pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($76,740), Connecticut ($69,280) and Hawaii ($64,490). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($27,220), Arkansas ($35,770) and Oklahoma ($38,680). The most jobs were in California (2,060), Florida (1,440) and Texas (1,420). The occupation is most concentrated in Alaska, where its share of all jobs is 2.6 times the national share.
