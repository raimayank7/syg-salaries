Social Scientists and Related Workers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Social Scientists and Related Workers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $101,110 a year ($48.61 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Social Scientists and Related Workers, All Other is $101,110, which is 98% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 15 by median pay. With 37,100 jobs, it makes up about 2.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Social Scientists and Related Workers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Virginia ($140,670), Maryland ($131,810) and District of Columbia ($128,440). The lowest was in Maine ($73,920), Puerto Rico ($76,400) and North Dakota ($76,400). The most jobs were in New York (3,410), Virginia (3,380) and California (2,970). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 14.8 times the national share.
