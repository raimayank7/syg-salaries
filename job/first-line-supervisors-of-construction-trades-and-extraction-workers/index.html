First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers: pay in the United States, May 2025

First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers in the United States earned a median wage of $79,920 a year ($38.42 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers is $79,920, which is 57% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 2 by median pay. With 812,210 jobs, it makes up about 52 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

First-Line Supervisors of Construction Trades and Extraction Workers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($109,570), Illinois ($105,750) and New Jersey ($105,510). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($37,860), Guam ($51,370) and Arkansas ($61,230). The most jobs were in Texas (90,010), California (71,750) and Florida (69,830). The occupation is most concentrated in Wyoming, where its share of all jobs is 2.2 times the national share.
