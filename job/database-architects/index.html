Database Architects: pay in the United States, May 2025

Database Architects in the United States earned a median wage of $139,500 a year ($67.07 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Design strategies for enterprise databases, data warehouse systems, and multidimensional networks. Set standards for database operations, programming, query processes, and security. Model, design, and construct large relational databases or data warehouses. Create and optimize data models for warehouse infrastructure and workflow. Integrate new systems with existing warehouse structure and refine system performance and functionality. Excludes “Database Administrators” (15-1242).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 15-1243.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Database Architects is $139,500, which is 174% higher. Among the 21 occupations of the field Computer and Mathematical, it ranks number 2 by median pay. With 67,140 jobs, it makes up about 4.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Database Architects pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($170,160), Massachusetts ($161,650) and Virginia ($160,360). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($95,620), Mississippi ($98,420) and Arkansas ($104,070). The most jobs were in Texas (8,270), California (6,210) and Virginia (5,260). The occupation is most concentrated in Virginia, where its share of all jobs is 3.0 times the national share.
