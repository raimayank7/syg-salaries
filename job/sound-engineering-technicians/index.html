Sound Engineering Technicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Sound Engineering Technicians in the United States earned a median wage of $73,130 a year ($35.16 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Assemble and operate equipment to record, synchronize, mix, edit, or reproduce sound, including music, voices, or sound effects, for theater, video, film, television, podcasts, sporting events, and other productions. Excludes “Audio and Video Technicians” (27-4011).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 27-4014.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Sound Engineering Technicians is $73,130, which is 43% higher. Among the 41 occupations of the field Arts, Design, Entertainment, Sports, and Media, it ranks number 16 by median pay. With 13,080 jobs, it makes up about 0.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($81,390) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Sound Engineering Technicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in New Jersey ($115,190), District of Columbia ($106,630) and New York ($87,510). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($35,180), Utah ($36,400) and South Carolina ($45,240). The most jobs were in California (4,060), New York (2,050) and Texas (830). The occupation is most concentrated in California, where its share of all jobs is 2.6 times the national share.
