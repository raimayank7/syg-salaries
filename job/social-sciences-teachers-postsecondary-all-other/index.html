Social Sciences Teachers, Postsecondary, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Social Sciences Teachers, Postsecondary, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $72,990 a year in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Social Sciences Teachers, Postsecondary, All Other is $72,990, which is 43% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 39 by median pay. With 16,580 jobs, it makes up about 1.1 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($88,390) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Social Sciences Teachers, Postsecondary, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($132,920), Rhode Island ($126,630) and Minnesota ($102,430). The lowest was in Arkansas ($37,490), Pennsylvania ($48,710) and Kansas ($51,540). The most jobs were in California (2,760), New York (1,840) and Illinois (1,700). The occupation is most concentrated in Illinois, where its share of all jobs is 2.6 times the national share.
