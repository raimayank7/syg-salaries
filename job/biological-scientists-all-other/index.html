Biological Scientists, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Biological Scientists, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $98,920 a year ($47.56 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Biological Scientists, All Other is $98,920, which is 94% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 18 by median pay. With 55,850 jobs, it makes up about 3.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Biological Scientists, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Maryland ($121,680), California ($113,530) and Delaware ($113,400). The lowest was in Missouri ($63,290), Louisiana ($69,590) and South Dakota ($73,920). The most jobs were in California (11,880), Maryland (4,970) and Massachusetts (2,930). The occupation is most concentrated in Maryland, where its share of all jobs is 5.0 times the national share.
