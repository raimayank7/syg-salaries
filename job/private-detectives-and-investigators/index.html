Private Detectives and Investigators: pay in the United States, May 2025

Private Detectives and Investigators in the United States earned a median wage of $51,220 a year ($24.62 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Private Detectives and Investigators is $51,220, which is about the same. Among the 24 occupations of the field Protective Service, it ranks number 16 by median pay. With 35,580 jobs, it makes up about 2.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($60,150) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Private Detectives and Investigators pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oregon ($76,480), Connecticut ($75,010) and District of Columbia ($72,720). The lowest was in Mississippi ($39,060), Oklahoma ($39,680) and Iowa ($41,110). The most jobs were in California (4,190), Florida (3,660) and Texas (3,220). The occupation is most concentrated in Louisiana, where its share of all jobs is 1.9 times the national share.
