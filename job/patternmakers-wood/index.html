Patternmakers, Wood: pay in the United States, May 2025

Patternmakers, Wood in the United States earned a median wage of $49,630 a year ($23.86 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan, lay out, and construct wooden unit or sectional patterns used in forming sand molds for castings.
