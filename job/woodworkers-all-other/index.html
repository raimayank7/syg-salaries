Woodworkers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Woodworkers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $44,530 a year ($21.41 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Woodworkers, All Other is $44,530, which is 13% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 70 by median pay. With 5,490 jobs, it makes up about 0.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Woodworkers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Virginia ($62,730), Pennsylvania ($61,880) and New York ($58,300). The lowest was in Tennessee ($34,790), Texas ($35,160) and Illinois ($35,970). The most jobs were in Tennessee (930), Oregon (640) and California (540). The occupation is most concentrated in Oregon, where its share of all jobs is 9.2 times the national share.
