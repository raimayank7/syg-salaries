Legal Secretaries and Administrative Assistants: pay in the United States, May 2025

Legal Secretaries and Administrative Assistants in the United States earned a median wage of $55,570 a year ($26.72 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Perform secretarial duties using legal terminology, procedures, and documents. Prepare legal papers and correspondence, such as summonses, complaints, motions, and subpoenas. May also assist with legal research.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 43-6012.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Legal Secretaries and Administrative Assistants is $55,570, which is 9% higher. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 9 by median pay. With 156,280 jobs, it makes up about 10 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Legal Secretaries and Administrative Assistants pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($99,280), New York ($72,230) and Connecticut ($66,220). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($31,000), Kansas ($36,620) and Missouri ($38,560). The most jobs were in California (27,470), Texas (13,380) and Florida (10,920). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 2.8 times the national share.
