Food Science Technicians: pay in the United States, May 2025

Food Science Technicians in the United States earned a median wage of $52,130 a year ($25.06 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Work with food scientists or technologists to perform standardized qualitative and quantitative tests to determine physical or chemical properties of food or beverage products. Includes technicians who assist in research and development of production technology, quality control, packaging, processing, and use of foods.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 19-4013.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Food Science Technicians is $52,130, which is 2% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 47 by median pay. With 14,600 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Food Science Technicians pay by state

The highest median pay was in Virginia ($65,360), Kentucky ($64,770) and Washington ($62,070). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($36,050), South Carolina ($39,480) and Kansas ($39,930). The most jobs were in California (3,990), Texas (900) and Oregon (820). The occupation is most concentrated in Idaho, where its share of all jobs is 7.0 times the national share.
