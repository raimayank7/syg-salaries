Entertainment and Recreation Managers, Except Gambling: pay in the United States, May 2025

Entertainment and Recreation Managers, Except Gambling in the United States earned a median wage of $79,520 a year ($38.23 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Entertainment and Recreation Managers, Except Gambling is $79,520, which is 56% higher. Among the 37 occupations of the field Management, it ranks number 31 by median pay. With 37,980 jobs, it makes up about 2.4 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($89,750) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Entertainment and Recreation Managers, Except Gambling pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($113,930), Minnesota ($107,660) and New York ($98,390). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($43,260), West Virginia ($47,310) and Arkansas ($49,640). The most jobs were in California (7,000), Illinois (3,260) and Texas (2,880). The occupation is most concentrated in Vermont, where its share of all jobs is 3.6 times the national share.
