Social Science Research Assistants: pay in the United States, May 2025

Social Science Research Assistants in the United States earned a median wage of $61,990 a year ($29.81 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Assist social scientists in laboratory, survey, and other social science research. May help prepare findings for publication and assist in laboratory analysis, quality control, or data management. Excludes “Teaching Assistants, Postsecondary” (25-9044).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 19-4061.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Social Science Research Assistants is $61,990, which is 22% higher. Among the 48 occupations of the field Life, Physical, and Social Science, it ranks number 40 by median pay. With 30,640 jobs, it makes up about 2.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Social Science Research Assistants pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oklahoma ($69,090), Delaware ($68,230) and Kansas ($67,740). The lowest was in Nevada ($26,560), Iowa ($40,940) and South Dakota ($44,820). The most jobs were in California (5,300), New York (3,040) and North Carolina (2,930). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 11.2 times the national share.
