Elevator and Escalator Installers and Repairers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Elevator and Escalator Installers and Repairers in the United States earned a median wage of $109,910 a year ($52.84 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Elevator and Escalator Installers and Repairers is $109,910, which is 116% higher. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 1 by median pay. With 23,790 jobs, it makes up about 1.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Elevator and Escalator Installers and Repairers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Maine ($143,690), Nevada ($141,950) and California ($141,180). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($38,190), Kentucky ($42,670) and Arkansas ($61,530). The most jobs were in New York (3,470), Florida (2,790) and Texas (2,030). The occupation is most concentrated in New York, where its share of all jobs is 2.3 times the national share.
