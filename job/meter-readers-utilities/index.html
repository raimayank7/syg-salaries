Meter Readers, Utilities in the United States earned a median wage of $48,150 a year ($23.15 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Meter Readers, Utilities is $48,150, which is 6% lower. Among the 54 occupations of the field Office and Administrative Support, it ranks number 29 by median pay. With 19,430 jobs, it makes up about 1.2 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($55,380) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Meter Readers, Utilities pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($79,040), Pennsylvania ($78,450) and Nevada ($76,120). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($30,440), Mississippi ($37,120) and Arkansas ($37,340). The most jobs were in Texas (1,360), Ohio (1,070) and New York (930). The occupation is most concentrated in Mississippi, where its share of all jobs is 4.1 times the national share.
