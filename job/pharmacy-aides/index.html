Pharmacy Aides: pay in the United States, May 2025

Pharmacy Aides in the United States earned a median wage of $37,680 a year ($18.12 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Record drugs delivered to the pharmacy, store incoming merchandise, and inform the supervisor of stock needs. May operate cash register and accept prescriptions for filling.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 31-9095.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Pharmacy Aides is $37,680, which is 26% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Healthcare Support, it ranks number 15 by median pay. With 39,530 jobs, it makes up about 2.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Pharmacy Aides pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($59,960), Utah ($51,600) and Hawaii ($46,250). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($28,100), Mississippi ($28,380) and Tennessee ($30,170). The most jobs were in California (8,440), Texas (2,900) and New York (2,360). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 4.6 times the national share.
