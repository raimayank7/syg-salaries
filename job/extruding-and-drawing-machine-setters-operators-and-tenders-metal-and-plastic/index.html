Extruding and Drawing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Extruding and Drawing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $47,720 a year ($22.94 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up, operate, or tend machines to extrude or draw thermoplastic or metal materials into tubes, rods, hoses, wire, bars, or structural shapes.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-4021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Extruding and Drawing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic is $47,720, which is 6% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 52 by median pay. With 60,840 jobs, it makes up about 3.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Extruding and Drawing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in Rhode Island ($61,350), Vermont ($59,220) and North Carolina ($53,330). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($26,030), Idaho ($34,700) and Oklahoma ($38,820). The most jobs were in Ohio (5,020), Texas (4,070) and California (3,870). The occupation is most concentrated in Indiana, where its share of all jobs is 3.0 times the national share.
