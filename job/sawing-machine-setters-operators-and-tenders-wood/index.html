Sawing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Wood: pay in the United States, May 2025

Sawing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Wood in the United States earned a median wage of $42,770 a year ($20.56 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up, operate, or tend wood sawing machines. May operate computer numerically controlled (CNC) equipment. Includes lead sawyers. Workers who primarily program or operate CNC equipment are classified in “Computer Numerically Controlled Tool Operators and Programmers” (51-9160).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-7041.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Sawing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Wood is $42,770, which is 16% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 77 by median pay. With 40,850 jobs, it makes up about 2.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Sawing Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Wood pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($52,680), Washington ($51,940) and Minnesota ($51,330). The lowest was in New Mexico ($34,720), West Virginia ($35,590) and Virginia ($35,790). The most jobs were in California (3,230), Ohio (2,180) and North Carolina (2,020). The occupation is most concentrated in Mississippi, where its share of all jobs is 6.2 times the national share.
