Tire Builders: pay in the United States, May 2025

Tire Builders in the United States earned a median wage of $57,390 a year ($27.59 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Tire Builders is $57,390, which is 13% higher. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 21 by median pay. With 20,770 jobs, it makes up about 1.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Tire Builders pay by state

The highest median pay was in Tennessee ($74,100), North Carolina ($63,950) and Illinois ($59,830). The lowest was in Florida ($44,150), Georgia ($44,220) and Michigan ($44,420). The most jobs were in North Carolina (3,750), Tennessee (1,360) and Indiana (1,320). The occupation is most concentrated in North Carolina, where its share of all jobs is 5.7 times the national share.
