Magnetic Resonance Imaging Technologists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Magnetic Resonance Imaging Technologists in the United States earned a median wage of $95,480 a year ($45.91 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Operate Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) scanners. Monitor patient safety and comfort, and view images of area being scanned to ensure quality of pictures. May administer gadolinium contrast dosage intravenously. May interview patient, explain MRI procedures, and position patient on examining table. May enter into the computer data such as patient history, anatomical area to be scanned, orientation specified, and position of entry.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-2035.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Magnetic Resonance Imaging Technologists is $95,480, which is 87% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 43 by median pay. With 43,390 jobs, it makes up about 2.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Magnetic Resonance Imaging Technologists pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($128,710), California ($124,000) and Washington ($122,750). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($44,810), Mississippi ($71,710) and South Dakota ($77,010). The most jobs were in California (3,570), New York (3,240) and Texas (2,930). The occupation is most concentrated in South Dakota, where its share of all jobs is 1.7 times the national share.
