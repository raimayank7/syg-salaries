Tailors, Dressmakers, and Custom Sewers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Tailors, Dressmakers, and Custom Sewers in the United States earned a median wage of $41,640 a year ($20.02 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Tailors, Dressmakers, and Custom Sewers is $41,640, which is 18% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 81 by median pay. With 13,920 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Tailors, Dressmakers, and Custom Sewers pay by state

The highest median pay was in New York ($60,210), District of Columbia ($57,720) and Washington ($54,300). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($23,190), Mississippi ($25,340) and Oklahoma ($26,300). The most jobs were in California (2,180), Texas (1,540) and New York (1,160). The occupation is most concentrated in Utah, where its share of all jobs is 1.9 times the national share.
