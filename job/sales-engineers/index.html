Sales Engineers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Sales Engineers in the United States earned a median wage of $124,900 a year ($60.05 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Sell business goods or services, the selling of which requires a technical background equivalent to a baccalaureate degree in engineering. Excludes “Engineers” (17-2011 through 17-2199) whose primary function is not marketing or sales.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 41-9031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Sales Engineers is $124,900, which is 145% higher. Among the 22 occupations of the field Sales and Related, it ranks number 1 by median pay. With 51,790 jobs, it makes up about 3.3 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Sales Engineers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Delaware ($167,200), Maryland ($153,410) and Rhode Island ($149,750). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($62,970), Alaska ($71,080) and Indiana ($74,960). The most jobs were in Texas (6,800), California (6,310) and Massachusetts (3,150). The occupation is most concentrated in Colorado, where its share of all jobs is 2.9 times the national share.

How much do Sales Engineers earn in the United States?
