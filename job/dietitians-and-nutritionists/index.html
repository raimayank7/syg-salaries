Dietitians and Nutritionists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Dietitians and Nutritionists in the United States earned a median wage of $76,400 a year ($36.73 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Plan and conduct food service or nutritional programs to assist in the promotion of health and control of disease. May supervise activities of a department providing quantity food services, counsel individuals, or conduct nutritional research.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Dietitians and Nutritionists is $76,400, which is 50% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 49 by median pay. With 77,570 jobs, it makes up about 5.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Dietitians and Nutritionists pay by state

The highest median pay was in California ($98,850), West Virginia ($89,440) and Alaska ($87,980). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($47,210), Guam ($60,260) and Mississippi ($63,700). The most jobs were in California (8,720), New York (5,330) and Texas (5,320). The occupation is most concentrated in Maine, where its share of all jobs is 1.6 times the national share.
