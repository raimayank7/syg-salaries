Electric Motor, Power Tool, and Related Repairers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Electric Motor, Power Tool, and Related Repairers in the United States earned a median wage of $56,210 a year ($27.02 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Electric Motor, Power Tool, and Related Repairers is $56,210, which is 10% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 30 by median pay. With 14,450 jobs, it makes up about 0.9 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Electric Motor, Power Tool, and Related Repairers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Illinois ($79,320), Wisconsin ($75,550) and New York ($66,710). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($26,340), Tennessee ($36,240) and Alabama ($38,430). The most jobs were in Texas (2,250), Pennsylvania (1,480) and Florida (1,150). The occupation is most concentrated in Oklahoma, where its share of all jobs is 4.3 times the national share.
