Food Preparation Workers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Food Preparation Workers in the United States earned a median wage of $35,320 a year ($16.98 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Perform a variety of food preparation duties other than cooking, such as preparing cold foods and shellfish, slicing meat, and brewing coffee or tea.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 35-2021.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Food Preparation Workers is $35,320, which is 31% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Food Preparation and Serving Related, it ranks number 10 by median pay. With 893,600 jobs, it makes up about 57 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Food Preparation Workers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($40,910), District of Columbia ($39,600) and California ($39,350). The lowest was in Guam ($21,820), Puerto Rico ($22,400) and Louisiana ($23,620). The most jobs were in California (112,750), Texas (81,280) and Florida (73,100). The occupation is most concentrated in Louisiana, where its share of all jobs is 2.8 times the national share.
