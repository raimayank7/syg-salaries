Forging Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic: pay in the United States, May 2025

Forging Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic in the United States earned a median wage of $49,030 a year ($23.57 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Forging Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic is $49,030, which is 4% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 40 by median pay. With 8,930 jobs, it makes up about 0.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Forging Machine Setters, Operators, and Tenders, Metal and Plastic pay by state

The highest median pay was in Oregon ($69,210), Oklahoma ($61,720) and Michigan ($61,620). The lowest was in New Jersey ($35,630), Kentucky ($36,110) and West Virginia ($37,390). The most jobs were in Tennessee (1,140), California (750) and Texas (720). The occupation is most concentrated in Tennessee, where its share of all jobs is 6.1 times the national share.
