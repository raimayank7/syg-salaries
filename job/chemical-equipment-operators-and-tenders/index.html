Chemical Equipment Operators and Tenders: pay in the United States, May 2025

Chemical Equipment Operators and Tenders in the United States earned a median wage of $58,040 a year ($27.90 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Operate or tend equipment to control chemical changes or reactions in the processing of industrial or consumer products. Equipment used includes devulcanizers, steam-jacketed kettles, and reactor vessels. Excludes “Chemical Plant and System Operators” (51-8091).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-9011.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Chemical Equipment Operators and Tenders is $58,040, which is 14% higher. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 19 by median pay. With 139,630 jobs, it makes up about 9.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Chemical Equipment Operators and Tenders pay by state

The highest median pay was in Wyoming ($101,590), Alabama ($80,330) and Montana ($79,570). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($36,450), Utah ($39,020) and Florida ($44,360). The most jobs were in Texas (20,880), New Jersey (8,560) and Ohio (7,830). The occupation is most concentrated in Louisiana, where its share of all jobs is 3.7 times the national share.
