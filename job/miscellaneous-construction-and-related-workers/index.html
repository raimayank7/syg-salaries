Miscellaneous Construction and Related Workers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Miscellaneous Construction and Related Workers in the United States earned a median wage of $49,910 a year ($24.00 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Miscellaneous Construction and Related Workers is $49,910, which is 2% lower. Among the 60 occupations of the field Construction and Extraction, it ranks number 43 by median pay. With 28,380 jobs, it makes up about 1.8 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($55,130) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Miscellaneous Construction and Related Workers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Maine ($75,340), Massachusetts ($70,540) and Washington ($69,380). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($30,090), Kentucky ($35,420) and Mississippi ($36,260). The most jobs were in Texas (3,350), Georgia (2,920) and Florida (2,710). The occupation is most concentrated in Delaware, where its share of all jobs is 3.7 times the national share.
