Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products: pay in the United States, May 2025

Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products in the United States earned a median wage of $104,920 a year ($50.44 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Sell goods for wholesalers or manufacturers where technical or scientific knowledge is required in such areas as biology, engineering, chemistry, and electronics, normally obtained from at least 2 years of postsecondary education. Excludes “Sales Engineers” (41-9031).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 41-4011.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products is $104,920, which is 106% higher. Among the 22 occupations of the field Sales and Related, it ranks number 2 by median pay. With 284,800 jobs, it makes up about 18 of every 10,000 jobs in the country. The average ($119,730) is well above the median, a sign that a smaller group of high earners pulls the average up.

Sales Representatives, Wholesale and Manufacturing, Technical and Scientific Products pay by state

The highest median pay was in Colorado ($135,000), New Jersey ($131,360) and Kansas ($130,850). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($62,160), Arkansas ($71,830) and Oklahoma ($72,480). The most jobs were in Texas (44,400), California (27,590) and Florida (19,840). The occupation is most concentrated in New Hampshire, where its share of all jobs is 2.1 times the national share.
