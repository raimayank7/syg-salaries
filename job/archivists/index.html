Archivists: pay in the United States, May 2025

Archivists in the United States earned a median wage of $64,550 a year ($31.03 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Archivists is $64,550, which is 27% higher. Among the 64 occupations of the field Educational Instruction and Library, it ranks number 48 by median pay. With 7,970 jobs, it makes up about 0.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Archivists pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($93,040), Connecticut ($79,790) and Massachusetts ($78,180). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($40,900), Arizona ($47,010) and Kentucky ($48,810). The most jobs were in California (1,160), New York (960) and Maryland (660). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 5.5 times the national share.
