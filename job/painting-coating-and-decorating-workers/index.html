Painting, Coating, and Decorating Workers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Painting, Coating, and Decorating Workers in the United States earned a median wage of $41,600 a year ($20.00 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Paint, coat, or decorate articles, such as furniture, glass, plateware, pottery, jewelry, toys, books, or leather. Excludes “Artists and Related Workers" (27-1010), “Designers” (27-1020), “Photographic Process Workers and Processing Machine Operators” (51-9151), and “Etchers and Engravers” (51-9194).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-9123.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Painting, Coating, and Decorating Workers is $41,600, which is 18% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 82 by median pay. With 7,940 jobs, it makes up about 0.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Painting, Coating, and Decorating Workers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($61,210), Nevada ($60,190) and Colorado ($54,070). The lowest was in South Dakota ($31,550), Texas ($32,430) and Mississippi ($33,330). The most jobs were in Florida (720), Texas (550) and Indiana (490). The occupation is most concentrated in Wyoming, where its share of all jobs is 3.9 times the national share.
