Home Health and Personal Care Aides: pay in the United States, May 2025

Home Health and Personal Care Aides in the United States earned a median wage of $35,800 a year ($17.21 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Home Health and Personal Care Aides is $35,800, which is 30% lower. Among the 17 occupations of the field Healthcare Support, it ranks number 16 by median pay. With 4,305,810 jobs, it makes up about 277 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Home Health and Personal Care Aides pay by state

The highest median pay was in Washington ($47,730), Oregon ($44,410) and Rhode Island ($43,680). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($21,840), Louisiana ($22,520) and Mississippi ($23,860). The most jobs were in California (970,150), New York (663,140) and Texas (329,590). The occupation is most concentrated in New York, where its share of all jobs is 2.5 times the national share.
