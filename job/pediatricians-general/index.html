Pediatricians, General: pay in the United States, May 2025

Pediatricians, General in the United States earned a median wage of $210,040 a year ($100.98 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Diagnose, treat, and help prevent diseases and injuries in children. May refer patients to specialists for further diagnosis or treatment, as needed. Excludes “Family Medicine Physicians” (29-1215) and “General Internal Medicine Physicians” (29-1216).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 29-1221.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Pediatricians, General is $210,040, which is 312% higher. Among the 71 occupations of the field Healthcare Practitioners and Technical, it ranks number 22 by median pay. With 39,390 jobs, it makes up about 2.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Pediatricians, General pay by state

The highest median pay was in Louisiana ($502,720), Idaho ($297,680) and New Hampshire ($273,410). The lowest was in District of Columbia ($79,240), Puerto Rico ($98,350) and Georgia ($149,990). The most jobs were in California (6,680), New York (4,570) and Texas (2,740). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 4.3 times the national share.
