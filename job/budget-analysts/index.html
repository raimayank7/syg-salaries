Budget Analysts: pay in the United States, May 2025

Budget Analysts in the United States earned a median wage of $91,640 a year ($44.06 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Examine budget estimates for completeness, accuracy, and conformance with procedures and regulations. Analyze budgeting and accounting reports. Excludes “Financial and Investment Analysts” (13-2051).

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 13-2031.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Budget Analysts is $91,640, which is 80% higher. Among the 32 occupations of the field Business and Financial Operations, it ranks number 8 by median pay. With 47,160 jobs, it makes up about 3.0 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Budget Analysts pay by state

The highest median pay was in District of Columbia ($124,590), Maryland ($106,020) and Virginia ($105,470). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($50,560), Virgin Islands ($64,990) and Guam ($67,200). The most jobs were in California (4,160), Texas (3,340) and Florida (3,150). The occupation is most concentrated in District of Columbia, where its share of all jobs is 7.0 times the national share.
