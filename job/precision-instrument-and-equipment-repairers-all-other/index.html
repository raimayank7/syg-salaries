Precision Instrument and Equipment Repairers, All Other: pay in the United States, May 2025

Precision Instrument and Equipment Repairers, All Other in the United States earned a median wage of $68,990 a year ($33.17 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Precision Instrument and Equipment Repairers, All Other is $68,990, which is 35% higher. Among the 51 occupations of the field Installation, Maintenance, and Repair, it ranks number 12 by median pay. With 9,400 jobs, it makes up about 0.6 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Precision Instrument and Equipment Repairers, All Other pay by state

The highest median pay was in Alaska ($113,670), Washington ($98,340) and Hawaii ($88,580). The lowest was in Pennsylvania ($33,870), Iowa ($45,960) and Illinois ($51,350). The most jobs were in California (1,460), Tennessee (1,150) and Texas (580). The occupation is most concentrated in Tennessee, where its share of all jobs is 5.8 times the national share.
