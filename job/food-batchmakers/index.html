Food Batchmakers: pay in the United States, May 2025

Food Batchmakers in the United States earned a median wage of $42,290 a year ($20.33 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

What the occupation covers

Set up and operate equipment that mixes or blends ingredients used in the manufacturing of food products. Includes candy makers and cheese makers.

Official definition from the Standard Occupational Classification, code 51-3092.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Food Batchmakers is $42,290, which is 17% lower. Among the 105 occupations of the field Production, it ranks number 79 by median pay. With 174,520 jobs, it makes up about 11 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Food Batchmakers pay by state

The highest median pay was in Vermont ($50,180), Idaho ($49,570) and Wisconsin ($48,360). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($22,270), Louisiana ($34,620) and West Virginia ($34,660). The most jobs were in Wisconsin (18,040), Texas (13,250) and California (12,560). The occupation is most concentrated in Wisconsin, where its share of all jobs is 5.5 times the national share.
