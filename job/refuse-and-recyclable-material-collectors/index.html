Refuse and Recyclable Material Collectors: pay in the United States, May 2025

Refuse and Recyclable Material Collectors in the United States earned a median wage of $49,690 a year ($23.89 an hour) in May 2025, according to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics. Half of them earned more than that and half earned less.

How it compares

The median for all occupations in the United States was $50,980 a year. The median for Refuse and Recyclable Material Collectors is $49,690, which is 3% lower. Among the 50 occupations of the field Transportation and Material Moving, it ranks number 28 by median pay. With 147,240 jobs, it makes up about 9.5 of every 10,000 jobs in the country.

Refuse and Recyclable Material Collectors pay by state

The highest median pay was in Illinois ($75,050), Washington ($74,620) and New York ($64,950). The lowest was in Puerto Rico ($21,840), Guam ($32,520) and South Dakota ($32,950). The most jobs were in California (13,930), Texas (12,860) and Florida (11,650). The occupation is most concentrated in Puerto Rico, where its share of all jobs is 2.0 times the national share.
