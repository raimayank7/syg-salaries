What $1 from 1919 is worth today

On average over 1919, a dollar bought what $19.47 buys in October 2026. $100 from 1919 is worth $1,947.18. Prices are 19.47 times higher: an average rise of 2.81% a year over 107.3 years, which would double prices about every 25 years.

Inflation in 1919

Prices rose by 15.2% from 1918 to 1919, the 4th highest of the 111 years from 1914 to 2024. The average consumer price index was 17.3 in 1919 (15.0 in 1918).

Reading the numbers

  • The index measures a basket of goods and services bought by urban consumers; it does not follow any one price, so a house, a car or a college year may have risen more or less.
  • The yearly figure is the average of the 12 monthly values, so the value at a particular month of 1919 can differ a little: use the calculator for exact months.
  • The October 2026 figure uses the latest month in the data (index 337.511).

Common questions

What is $1 from 1919 worth in October 2026?

$19.47, because consumer prices are 19.47 times as high as the 1919 average.

What was the US inflation rate in 1919?

15.2% (the change in the yearly average index from 1918).

Sources
