What $1 from 1954 is worth today

On average over 1954, a dollar bought what $12.57 buys in October 2026. $100 from 1954 is worth $1,257.02. Prices are 12.57 times higher: an average rise of 3.56% a year over 72.3 years, which would double prices about every 20 years.

Inflation in 1954

Prices rose by 0.3% from 1953 to 1954, the 96th highest of the 111 years from 1914 to 2024. The average consumer price index was 26.8 in 1954 (26.8 in 1953).

Reading the numbers

  • The index measures a basket of goods and services bought by urban consumers; it does not follow any one price, so a house, a car or a college year may have risen more or less.
  • The yearly figure is the average of the 12 monthly values, so the value at a particular month of 1954 can differ a little: use the calculator for exact months.
  • The October 2026 figure uses the latest month in the data (index 337.511).

Common questions

What is $1 from 1954 worth in October 2026?

$12.57, because consumer prices are 12.57 times as high as the 1954 average.

What was the US inflation rate in 1954?

0.3% (the change in the yearly average index from 1953).

Sources

  • Every result is index arithmetic: amount × (index in the later month ÷ index in the earlier month).
